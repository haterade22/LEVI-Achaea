### Details

- **Harmonise**: The first step of the dance. The aspiring bladedancer will require no simple blade of unfeeling steel for their performance. Once you have acquired an appropriate rapier, head to the Grotto of Song and BATHE RAPIER IN SONG to prepare for the battles to come. * Bladedance will require a songblessed rapier.
- **Gripping**: Strong hands will save your life. While utilising this ability, you will prevent items from being knocked out of your hands.
- **Jab**: Control the distance. The simplest of techniques with the rapier, this swift jab teaches the student to gauge distance accurately while also inflicting piercing wounds to an adversary. As with other bladedance techniques, the jab will have different results based on your position in relation to your partner and where the jab lands. These results are as follows: From the front: At the arms: the limb will be broken. At the torso: afflicts with nausea. From the side: At the arms: afflicts with clumsiness. At the torso: afflicts with asthma. From the back: At the arms: gives weariness. If the jab breaks the arm, deals bonus damage. At the torso: afflicts with anorexia. If the jab breaks the torso, tonal trauma takes place(*). * See AB BLADEDANCE TONAL.
- **Tune**: Just the right pitch. Weave song about your person, warding off all forms of damage to an extent.
- **Punctuate**: Emphasis is key. This swift flurry of jabs with the rapier is used to break the defences of your partner. It will destroy barriers to further bladedance techniques if they are present. * Strips rebounding aura, then magical shields.
- **Hound**: The dance isn't over. Sometimes when you get behind a partner they're just going to try to make a run for it. With this technique you'll hound their steps, and, so long as you have their back still(*), you'll be able to charge back into the dance inflicting quite the damage while stunning them. * If someone is in an adjacent room and you have back position, this ability will move you into their room and damage them as well as conveying a moderate stun.
- **Tonal**: First the edge, next the sound. When you deal particularly powerful blows, the tonal resonance of your songblessed rapier shall cause terrible trauma to the impact site beyond even the norm: The head: afflicts with impatience. Torso: afflicts with asthma. Either arm: afflicts with slickness. Either leg: afflicts with prone. * This will be noted in relevant ability files.
- **Flick**: Sometimes steel, sometimes song. This flick with the blade focuses more on song than steel, permitting the bard to channel the nature of their song blessed weapon to greater affect. If successful your foe will be struck down with certain deadly conditions: From the front: crescendo, an enthralling condition which will only grow more dire the longer they dance to your tune(*). From the side: earworm, a condition that is sure to grow worse over time(**). From the back: both crescendo and earworm. * Each time someone afflicted with crescendo eats an herb that is not ash (which cures crescendo), the crescendo will build. At a certain point, this makes other abilities available (see AB COMPOSITION FINALE). ** All bladedance attacks that deal damage deal additional damage against a target afflicted with earworm. This damage increases the longer the earworm remains uncured. *** When used against denizens, this ability deals psychic damage to your foe.
- **Flourish**: You're mesmerising, you are. This elegant flourish with your blade may not inflict any harm, but it'll certainly give you the opportunity to adjust your footwork while your partner is spellbound by your peerless grace. In practice, you will advance two positions through the bladedance immediately (so from front to back, side to front, back to side).
- **Highsun**: A high point of the day. A downward strike with the edge of the blade aimed at the head or arms of a partner, the highsun strike delivers the first affliction that your foe does not have from: When aimed at the head: From the front: clumsiness, weariness, or recklessness. From the side: addiction, generosity, or confusion. From the back: paralysis, generosity, or diminished(*). When aimed at the arms: From the front: clumsiness, weariness, or haemophilia. From the side: weariness, clumsiness, or healthleech. From the back: paralysis, healthleech, or sensitivity. * Diminished prevents the use of any active cures while afflicted with it.
- **Heelsnap**: Two legs are for amateurs. This low lateral slash with the rapier is aimed at one of the legs of your partner. As with other techniques with the rapier, it has various affects based upon your position in relation to your partner: From the front: breaks the leg in question. From the side: if aimed at the left leg, afflicts with healthleech. If aimed at the right leg, afflicts with sensitivity. From the back: afflicts with hamstrung and dismounts your partner if they are mounted.
- **Sunrise**: Down goes your partner. As the sun ascends to greet the dawn, so too shall your rapier rise to meet your adversary. Delivered not as a traditional lateral or vertical slash but as a diagonal blow to the hip and up into the torso, this strike can be utterly devastating. If the torso and leg adjacent to the hip you are aiming at are sufficiently damaged(*), both will be brokenas a result of this strike, and tonal trauma takes place upon both limbs. * Both limbs must be damaged enough that the next bladedance attack would break them. ** Se AB BLADEDANCE TONAL.
- **Sunset**: Light on falling steel. As the sun descends at the close of day, so too shall your rapier fall upon your adversary. Delivered not as a traditional lateral or vertical slash but as a diagonal blow down upon either the left or right shoulder, this strike can be utterly devastating. If the head and arm adjacent to the shoulder you are aiming at are sufficiently damaged(*), both will be broken as a result of this strike, and tonal trauma takes place upon both limbs(**). * Both limbs must be damaged enough that the next bladedance attack would break them. ** Se AB BLADEDANCE TONAL.
- **Deathfrombelow**: From earth to sky with blade and voice. Sometimes those of lesser footwork will think to take to the skies or trees, foolishly believing that surely you cannot be as sublime in such environs as your peerless grace upon the ground. You will soon disabuse them of this folly, for with a single bound and a cry(*) you will bring them straight back down to your level with a strike of your blade(**). * This uses your warcry if you have one set. ** This deals damage and knocks them prone to the ground, as well as incurring a relatively nontrivial blackout.
- **Footwork**: Poetry in motion. Your footwork is the envy and ambition of lesser dancers: poetry in motion to make even the most cold-hearted soul stop and regard. As you enter the deadly dance with a partner, you will take the front position. As you strike, you will move around them, taking the side position, and eventually entering their blindspot in the back position. The novice bladedancer will move into side position after five strikes in the front position and into back position after two further strikes. They will return to the front position after a single attack in the back position. Most bladedance techniques function differently against your partner depending on your position, and your partner will be unable to parry attacks entirely performed from their blindspot. Additionally, you will deal bonus damage to any denizens when striking. Note that if your partner escapes you, reengaging swiftly will let you preserve your positional sense, but if they evade you for too long you will need to return to the front position.
- **Backflip**: You make it look so easy. With the casual grace that only you possess you can flip over obstructions such as adventurers blocking you or icewalls.
- **Acrobatics**: Stay light, stay mobile. With this ability, you will exercise your dexterity, becoming far more difficult to hit. Be warned that it comes with a serious endurance drain.
- **Dodging**: For the toughest of crowds. This ability will allow you to dodge attacks from the air.
- **Tempo**: Timing is everything. The tempo of your footwork is of prime importance for any dancer of the blade. The three styles will dictate how rapidly you may transition between positions, and all have their place: Adagio: 4, 4, 3. Moderato: 3, 2, 2. Allegro: 2, 1, 1. Vivace: 1, 6, 3. No tempo: 5, 2, 1. * Numbers relate to number of bladedance attacks performed in front, side, and back respectively.
- **Hawkstep**: Move and strike, move and strike. This dance is one of swift motion and exacting precision. When dancing the hawkstep attempts to hinder your escape from a room (by such things as rites of piety and the like) shall be less likely to succeed. * Note that you can only dance one thing at a time, so the hawkstep is exclusive with the dance of the harrying, for example.
- **Somersault**: Keep it moving. An advanced form of tumbling, you may quickly somersault passed barriers or similar, even when bound(*). * This functionally acts as a survival tumble which completes faster.
- **Wavedance**: For never do you tire. Dancing as the waves do, you shall be as relentless as they in turn, and yet never quite unleashed(*). * You will not be parried while dancing this dance, but will also do no limb damage.
- **Balancing**: Poise weeps at your perfection. With this ability, you will have superior balance, making it much more difficult to knock you off your feet with abilities such as sweepkick, or the earthquake vibration. There is a mana drain associated with it, however.
- **Encircle**: Work with an ally to encircle foes. Stand beside an ally, ready to encircle your foe. Using this ability on a mutual ally will allow you to circle behind your target from the front position, so long as your partner is in the room.
- **Harrying**: The show is not over. The dance of the harrying is one focussed on swift blows of the rapier to keep your enemy pinned within your deadly encirclement. Though these blows typically do no real harm, the threat of your blades point will make it difficult for even the fleetest foe to escape your lethal performance. *You may switch from harrying to another dance without consuming balance.

### Details

- **Performance**: The fundamentals of the stage. The fundamental of the performer, what more critical piece of the undertaking can there be than the beginning? Performances are split into two parts: Songs: these strike all your enemies in your location periodically in some manner dependent on the type of song you are playing. Movements: these passively benefit all of your allies in your location in some way as long as your song is playing. When starting out, your performance can only contain one movement, but as your skill grows, so too will the scope of your performance.
- **Refrain**: The blade sings. Melding song and steel in a perfection that only a true composer could appreciate, you may now strike with a song alongside a bladedance technique as well(*). * The refrain hits before the bladedance attack. Song effects when used as the refrain: Paean: Paralysis Bhajan: Dizziness Nomos: Breaks shield and lyre if either are present. If neither, breaks rebounding Gusheh: Addiction Ode: Asthma Ghazal: Slickness Elegy: Lethargy *You may also use a finale in place of a refrain. See the finale ability for details.
- **Cadence**: As the music plays on. You have advanced in your mastery to a point where you may now suspend a performance and resume it at will(*). * A suspended performance will not decrease in time remaining while you're online, and a suspended performance will not hit any enemies.
- **Symphony**: You are the maestro. You may now weave multiple movements into your song, having up to three simultaneously tied in with your grand performance.
- **Crescendo**: The rising swell of the song. As you approach mastery of the performing arts, so too are your performances enhanced greatly. Now, all who are passively influenced by one of your songs who have a crescendo building about them will find that that crescendo draws faster with greater speed(*). * Each time your passive song hits someone the crescendo affliction gains a stack if present.
- **Duet**: Such harmony profound. Though your own performances are doubtlessly grand beyond the reckoning of all that would behold them, sometimes one may simply wish to raise the level of another, perhaps tragically less gifted, musician. By performing this ability you will greatly raise an actively building crescendo about another(*), so long as someone other than yourself is responsible for it(**). * Sets their crescendo to one stack below the finale threshold. ** You cannot be the one who gave the crescendo affliction in question.
- **Finale**: The curtain falls. If a crescendo has built sufficiently about a target(*), you may bring your ongoing performance to a grand finale. This ends your current performance. * Five stacks of crescendo. Finales: Paean: gives the reeling affliction for 20 seconds, which causes all bladedance attacks to be performed from the back position until it fades. Bhajan: Strikes the hearer with both stupidity and epilepsy. Additionally, if they are suffering from head trauma, they will lose focus balance for a significant period of time. Nomos: mangles an already level two broken leg. Prioritises the left over the right. Gusheh: mangles an already level two broken arm. Prioritises the left over the right. Ode: your foe will be rendered unable to communicate by any means for five minutes. Ghazal: causes your foe to succumb to a love-wrought madness for thirty seconds. While succumbed to said madness, they will be unable to. perceive the state of themselves (similar to recklessness) and all physical damage shall become unblockable. Elegy: any blessing of the stars protecting your target will be removed, and any starburst tattoos upon their person will be removed. Prosodion: transfixes the hearer with the power of the Accentato, making earworm incurable for twenty seconds.
- **Paean**: And off to war they went. This song of war will cause all of your enemies who tremble beneath its hearing to know the bloodshed of times gone by(*). * All enemies bleed. Either for a small amount or twenty percent of their current bleeding, whichever is higher. When refrained: gives paralysis. Finale: gives the reeling affliction for 20 seconds, which causes all bladedance attacks to be performed from the back position until it fades.
- **Bhajan**: That dread which stokes the fear. This dark song will stoke the fears of all of your enemies that hear it(*). * Gives the fear affliction. When refrained: gives dizziness. Finale: strikes the hearer into such a state of inaction that they will lose focus balance(*) for a significant period of time, as well as afflicting them with both stupidity and epilepsy. * Head must be damaged for focus knock.
- **Nomos**: The most disciplined resolve. The relentless march of your nomos shall crush the resolve of your enemies, destroying reflections raised about their person and revealing them for who they are beneath(*). * If they have only one reflection, it is destroyed. Otherwise the number of reflections protecting them is halved. When refrained: breaks shield or prismatic, if present. If neither is present, breaks rebounding. Finale: mangles an already level two broken leg. Prioritises the left over the right.
- **Gusheh**: Of joy and feasts with friends. A song of the many joys of life, this grand performance is sure to cloud the senses of any enemy who hears it(*). * Stuns for a short time. When refrained: gives addiction. Finale: mangles an already level two broken arm. Prioritises the left over the right.
- **Ode**: For heroes do we sing. This song about legendary heroes shall take the breath away from any enemy that hears you sing it, making them unable to say anything for a short time(*). If they are asthmatic, this time is increased. * Only impacts SAY. Telepathic channels are not influenced. When refrained: gives asthma. Finale: your foe will be rendered unable to communicate by any means for five minutes.
- **Ghazal**: So sweet the love sublime. A song of heartache and love, when your enemies hear this song they will become lovestruck. While not as potent as the true lovers curse, this will make the afflicted potentially forget someone is an enemy in any given moment(*). * All enemy checks have a chance to fail outright. When refrained: gives slickness. Finale: causes your foe to succumb to a love-wrought madness for thirty seconds. While succumbed to said madness, they will be unable to perceive the state of themselves (similar to recklessness) and all physical damage shall become unblockable.
- **Elegy**: Weep for the dead. This solemn song for the dead will lay the weariness of loss upon all who hear it. Each time an enemy hears your song, their celerity will be greatly reduced(*), and if they are already lethargic, it will be eliminated entirely(**). * Their celerity will be set to one movement below maximum to whatever they can normally move. ** They will have their celerity reduced to the lowest possible level. When refrained: gives lethargy. Finale: any blessing of the stars protecting your target will be removed, and any starburst tattoos upon their person will be removed.
- **Prosodion**: Of grandeur most Divine. The Prosodion is a song about the Gods, and all your enemies that hear it shall be struck down with a sliver of that grandeur. * Deals some damage passively to all enemies. When refrained: gives sensitivity. Finale: transfixes the hearer with the power of the Accentato, making earworm incurable for twenty seconds.
- **Prelude**: Before the beginning. This movement which prepares the body of you and your allies shall grant some resistance to magical damage.
- **Aria**: You'll keep this party going. This upbeat movement bolsters you and your allies, reducing all willpower and endurance use by around ten percent(*). * Some abilities are unmodified by anything when it comes to this cost. As with other enhancements, this movement will not help in these cases.
- **Maqam**: To lend keenness to blades unsheathed. This movement will lend the blows of you and your allies with greater precision, granting a small bonus to critical hits.
- **Scherzo**: Through thick and thin. his fast paced movement will periodically regenerate your health and that of your allies. This operates independently of other regeneration sources (so stacks with artefacts and similar).
- **Sonata**: To unburden all with ears to hear. The movement of the sonata shall unburden you and your allies of their afflictions periodically, healing one at random.
- **Pastorale**: To soothe the mind and soul. A tranquil movement to bring peace to you and your allies, all that are affected by the pastorale will have a small chance to resist being forced to act against their will.
- **Rondo**: Onwards we march. The rondo movement bolsters you and your allies for the march ahead, halving the time it takes to cross rough terrain and similar(*). * Rubble, for example. This does not apply to moving through Charnel crypts, however.
- **Nocturne**: For silent you will be not. This greatest movement may be added to any performance, and then not even a silence vibration or similar shall stop all from hearing your masterful song.

### Details

- **Scene**: Ambience is everything. Craft a scene for all in your location to witness(*). * Creates an illusion. You may embed up to one newline with \n.
- **Recite**: Once upon a time... To tell a tale, to spin a yarn, to bring to life a story of long gone days. The teller of great sagas of the past knows the power in such things, and so tell them you shall. Each saga will have multiple stages. All sagas will do something upon being completed, and some will do things at other points in the tale as well. Consult each saga for specifics upon the nature of your grand design.
- **Gossip**: You'll never believe what I heard... The art of storytelling is one step removed from the art of conversation, and as a master of both, sometimes you just need to get your message out there in a less obvious way. By seeking out various establishments(*), you can, around once an Achaean day, spread some gossip. Similarly in said locations, you may also listen for gossip every so often, and in such a way gossip is bound to spread. * Inns and similar, generally.
- **Countermand**: No, this is how it went. Every storyteller knows there is just that one person who has to be the centre of attention even when they've just got nothing worthwhile to say. Clearly, anything you have to say is far more facinating than such lesser tellers of tales, and now you will prove it! You may countermand any saga that you know being told by another, having a chance to completely interrupt their flow and aborting it before it can reach a grand culmination(*). * You are not guaranteed to succeed. You can increase your chances through various means, but never to a hundred percent.
- **Prologue**: Delay the conclusion. Masters of storytelling gain the ability to delay the conclusion of a tale for dramatic effect. Using this ability will recite another tale up to its culmination, at which point the completion will be delayed. The tale may be completed for the next 5 minutes instantly at the cost of 3 seconds of balance. This will incur a cooldown of 5 minutes. No tall tales can be completed in this way.
- **Troubador**: Where ever the story takes you. A good storyteller must go where ever the tale may take them, though equally it is true that some legends just don't hit as hard when constantly on the move. You may now move while performing many of your sagas for an audience, them no longer being interrupted if your location changes between recite stages(*). * Channeled sagas are excluded.

### Details

- **Condemned**: The coldest dish so served. The tale of the Condemneds vengeance is one long in the making and more tragic still for the fact that he may yet have stayed his hand if not for a single dismissive word. Yet there is no doubt that come the time of choice Han-Silnar slew the Wanderer in defiance of those that hold his chains: in this, he would not be commanded otherwise. * Cyrenians that hear this tale shall be granted the steelmind defence, making the next forced action against them fail.

If such is the case, then Black Pazuzu's evil is a burden to be borne by a City and its Peoples from now unto distant futures unknown. And so when you recite the tales of Black Pazuzu, Prince of Woe and self-proclaimed New God, it is the weight of Remembrance that you will bear: both ancient transference and sacred charge. This is his story, but you are not afeared.

When you speak this tale, all who are present at the culmination shall find that for a time they will be unable to change those they consider ally or enemy by any means.
- **Nishnatoba**: The end of the beginning. The tale of the great battle upon Nishnatoba is one foundational to history, as is the last charge that came at its grand culmination. Speak this tale and all of your citymates that are members of the standing army in your location will rally behind you(*), ready to charge into battle. * They will be added to your group.
- **Enheduanna**: Weep for what was lost. The tragic tale of Enheduanna is one of sorrow and betrayal, love and hatred, and terrible, terrible wrath. At the culmination of this tale all in your location that hear you, ally or enemy, will be suffused with a fragment of that terrible fury, all of their actions becoming enhanced(*). * Balance and equilibrium time is reduced while present in the room where this tale was spoken for those that heard it, though not for long. * This tale may only be spoken once every five minutes.
