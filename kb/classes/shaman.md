### Details

- **Commune**: Commune with the spirits. By communing with a spirit, you may bind it to yourself. This will grant you some of the powers that that particular spirit possessed during its life. You may bind up to five spirits at once. You may also attune yourself completely to a spirit. This will grant additional benefits, although you may only attune two spirits at a time. Once you have mastered the spiritual arts in their entirety, you may attune three spirits at a time. Communing requires a ritual drum, which can be purchased from most general stores around Sapience.
- **Daina**: Daina, The Viridian. By channeling the power of nature, you are able to drive diseases from your body with greatly increased efficiency.
- **Jhulian**: Jhulian, The Nomad. The spirit of Jhulian, The Nomad will allow you to travel to a mutual ally, given a short delay.
- **Arius**: Arius, The Druid Once you have bound the spirit of Arius, The Druid, you will be able to channel the mighty power of the hydra to unleash a terrible roar at your opponent, disrupting their mental equilibrium. When targeted against denizens, it will do damage and cost equilibrium rather than balance.
- **Silvanix**: Silvanix, The Fool. The skilled jester has deft wits and defter hands, and Silvanix was no exception. By binding his spirit, you will be able to snatch arrows from the very air.
- **Ri'shen**: Ri'shen, The Master. One who has mastered the practices of kaido is able to restore their body from almost any injury. While the power granted to you through the connection with Ri'Shen is not to that level, it will allow you to perform lesser feats of regeneration.
- **Maligus**: Maligus, The Cleric. The practitioners of Apostasy have long been feared for their mastery of unholy powers, and Maligus in life was no exception. Upon binding his spirit, you will gain the ability to forceably attune a vodun doll to the soul of an unfortunate subject, improving its detail potentially far quicker than would normally be possible. This ability will be more effective against those who lack mental fortitude. Additionally, various afflictions, listed from most impactful to least, improve the power of a soulrend if the victim suffers from them: manaleech, confusion, shyness, paranoia, dementia. This ability can be combined with curses. * The rend takes place before the affliction is delivered from a curse, so cursing manaleech and soulrending at once will not work. ** Note that with the exception of manaleech, if these modifier afflictions are masked they are 33% less impactful.
- **Aspar**: Aspar, The Iron Handed. Alchemists know more about how to turn a body against itself than perhaps anyone else in Sapience. In life, Aspar was most feared for his ability to manipulate the sanguine humours of his enemies. By binding his spirit, you will be able to infect open wounds with disease, forcing the blood to clot but doing far more harm than good. Diseases which may be inflicted in this way are impatience, paralysis, asthma, slickness, anorexia, weariness, lethargy, and clumsiness. This ability is combinable with curses.
- **Aelkesh**: Aelkesh, The Faceless. Aelkesh was an Occultist who lived during the period of heightened strife with the Church. He developed an ability to allow him to travel great distances to facilitate his escape in the event of danger, and while binding his spirit will not grant such mastery, it will allow you to fling yourself out of potentially fatal situations. Be warned, however it is far from accurate.
- **Teraile**: Teraile, The Swordsman. The skill of the practitioners of the legendary Two Arts can not be overstated. By binding the spirit of one of the long dead masters of this art, you will be able to lacerate the flesh of your enemies. This ability will cause the victim to gain the haemophilia affliction, there by removing their ability to clot their wounds. If your victim already has haemophilia, they will suffer bleeding. This ability may be combined with curses.
- **Arcanor**: Arcanor, The Sorcerer. To fight on your terms as a mage is the first step toward victory. By binding the spirit of Arcanor, you will be able to invoke his power to summon great walls of ice to block the escape of all those who would flee from your wrath.
- **Syvis**: Syvis, The Venomous. The masters of venom have long been feared for their deception and cunning when it comes to the elimination of those that would cross them. By binding the spirit of this ancient assassin, you will be capable of causing diseases that your victim believes they have cured to relapse shortly there after. Be warned, you are no true master of the art. You may only perform this ability every so often. This ability must be combined with curses.
- **Marak**: Marak, The Eviscerator. This ability will allow you to destroy a vodun doll to balancelessly activate one of the following vodun-based abilities on the target: confusion, dizzy, sleep, bind, paralyse, break, throttle, reckless, bleed, cripple, mangle, imbibe. The doll must be fashioned to twice the extent that the ability used would normally require. This ability must be comboed with curses.
- **Anthius**: Anthius, The Crusader. Anthius was one of the faithful Templars who sought out and eliminated those that would corrupt the sanctity of Creation. By channeling this spirit, you are able to unleash holy judgement at a heretic in an adjacent location. The spectral fires will scythe past magical defences, and will not be denied.
- **Garon**: Garon, The Knight. While this spirit gives you sufficient knowledge to craft magical totems, you lack the knowledge required to etch them with runes of power. To this end, a different medium is required. To carve a totem, you will require 20 wood, 10 leather, and 2 silver commodities. Once you have obtained and implanted one into the ground, you may draw either a circle of binding or pain about its base. When an unfortunate enemy looks upon the totem empowered by such a circle, he shall find himself either bound in place or struck down by great pain, dependent on which circle has been drawn. Drawing a circle requires two black inks.
- **Designate**: Designate the owners of your tribal totems. With this ability you may designate an owner for a specific totem. Only the crafter of the totem may use this ability. The totem remains owned by the crafter until the designated owner (or a member of the designated owner organisation) CLAIMs the totem. If the totem is claimed, then the designated owner becomes the true owner. If the totem is rejected, then the crafter remains the owner. At that point the crafter may attempt another designation as desired. Houses, Cities, Orders, Oakstone, and ndividuals may own totems - clans may not. When you designate a totem, its tuning is automatically set against the enemies of the new owner.
- **Tarnel**: Tarnel, The Walker. The walkers of the forest know the value in snaring an unsuspecting victim. The prey that doesn't see the trap coming has its throat cut first. At the chance of breaking your bond with Tarnel, The Walker, you may bind an enemy. This invokation is very fast.
- **Kosuira**: Kosuira, The Child Sorceress A protege in the art of Terminus, Kosuira learned much more of the art than any modern day Depthswalker. Though much of this knowledge is now out of reach, by tapping into her spirit you may bring down an invocation, freezing the ground in your location and doing similarly for all adjacent locations.
- **Enkindle**: For the most desperate of times. The master practitioner can learn to slacken bonds to the point of breaking while in communion, and yet in a time of need draw them all back to full potence for a brief time, though with dire consequences. * Slackening a bond puts it into a dormant status, it keeps its attunement. You cannot have a slackened and active bond with the same spirit. * You can have as many slackened bonds as you can have active bonds and these limits do not impact one another, so you can have five active and five slackened for instance. * When you enkindle, all active bonds are severed and all slackened bonds are made active. After five minutes all bonds will be broken and you will be unable to commune for a further five minutes.
- **Arayan**: Arayan, The Healer. The power of priests to heal their fortunate allies is legendary. Though you shall require a focus in the form of a vodun doll fashioned to at least 50 times, you will be able to channel this power. By destroying the doll in its entirety, you may drag your comrade back from the very state of death itself, restoring him or her to a physical body.
- **Tether**: Desperate times lead to temporary measures. For those times when a traditional communion may not be possible, the able practitioner will find himself able to form a makeshift bond with one of the spirits. Tethering is inferior to binding; it will not permit attunement of said spirit and it will break after a single use of the related spirit's power, but it may be performed outside of any trance. You may also solidify a tether, transforming it into a full binding; though the mental equilibrium cost of doing so is substantial.
- **Spiritwalk**: Walk free of the confines of mortal flesh. This greatest of the spiritual arts allows you to leave your body and walk as a spirit about the land. The further from your body you stray, the faster your willpower shall drain. If you run out of willpower, your spiritual form shall fade and your body will die. Be further warned. Leaving your body alone is a terribly dangerous affair. If an enemy were to stumble upon it, they would be able to destroy it, and any hope you have of returning to a physical form.
- **Khentimen**: If you dare. Long have shamans been cautioned against reaching out to those damned souls who practice that art most maligned. * Performing this ability shall create a mirage style effect upon the location. See AB CHARNEL MIRAGE for full details - the mechanics are identical. ** Note that no higher communion is necessary to attempt this most foolish of undertakings. After all. It won't help.
- **Mastery**: Mastery of the spiritual arts. Now that you have mastered the spiritual arts in their entirety, you will be able to attune up to three spirits at a time.
- **Adchachel**: Demiurge Adchachel, The Rose of Pain Demiurge Adchachel was one of the key leaders in the battle of the deeps during the Black Wave. Having consumed the heart of Agith'maal, the Tsol'teth's insidious remnant twisted and consumed him, changing the charismatic Black Lotus of Mystery into something not remotely resembling that which came before. Going on to sire many children, the Rose Of Pain can trace his line all the way to the hand that destroyed great Seleucar, his black influence stretching down the centuries long after he was immolated in the purging flames that ended his reign of terror. By channeling this fell spirit, you may corrupt even the most sacred of locations, destroying a random holy rite that protects a room. If the presence of a practitioner of the holy art Devotion stands in said location, their pure influence shall cause this process to take some time.

### Details

- **Fear**: Instill great fear in your target. This ability works to instil fear in your victim.
- **Discernment**: Discern the severity of your victim's wounds. With a practiced eye, determine how severely your victim is bleeding.
- **Deaf**: Dampen an opponent's hearing. Remove the hearing of your victim.
- **Bleed**: Curse your opponent with uncontrollable bleeding. Induce bleeding in your target. This ability will work on Denizens as well as adventurers.
- **Clumsy**: Your victim will fumble about. Cause your victim to fumble about.
- **Vertigo**: A devastating fear of heights. Afflict your target with a great fear of heights.
- **Impatience**: Inability to concentrate on focused tasks. Afflict your victim with an inability to concentrate on focused tasks.
- **Amnesia**: Make your victim forget what he meant to do. Make your victim forget what he meant to do.
- **Agoraphobia**: Fear of open spaces. Afflict your victim with a fear of open spaces.
- **Breach**: Cut through an opponent's curseward. Rip down your opponent's cursewards.
- **Dizzy**: Causes problems when trying to move. Afflict your target with severe dizziness.
- **Claustrophobia**: Fear of being under cover. Afflict your target with a great fear of enclosed spaces.
- **Paranoia**: They are out to get you, believe me. Instil a fear of other people in your victim.
- **Blind**: Curse an opponent with blindness. Remove your poor victim's eyesight.
- **Masochism**: The love of your own pain. Make your victim love pain so much they will inflict it upon themselves.
- **Asthma**: Creates difficulty when breathing. Make it difficult for your victim to breathe.
- **Truecurse**: The ability to drive your curses through magic shields. With the ability of truecurse, adventurer's magical shields will no longer get in the way of your dreaded curses.
- **Reckless**: A dangerous disregard for personal well-being. Give your target a dangerous disregard for their personal well-being.
- **Epilepsy**: Disrupt the nervous system of your victim. Cause your victim to go into seizures.
- **Manaleech**: Cause the victim's mana to bleed away. Sap away mana from your target.
- **Peace**: Fill your enemy's mind with thoughts of peace. Fill your enemy's mind with thoughts of peace, granting the pacifism affliction.
- **Dementia**: Distorts the victim's perceptions of the world. Distorts the victim's perceptions of the world.
- **Paralyse**: Freeze the limbs of your enemy. Cause your victims body to freeze with paralysis.
- **Plague**: Curse someone with the voyria affliction. Afflict your victim with the deadly Voyria.
- **Addiction**: Creates a terrible addiction. Cause your target to become addicted.
- **Swiftcurse**: The ability to deliver curses much more quickly. This potent defence will drastically speed up your next ten curses after initiation. Breach is excluded.
- **Stupid**: Make a victim dumb as a rock. Make a victim dumb as a rock.
- **Healthleech**: A victim's health will slowly bleed away. Sap away your target's health.
- **Sensitivity**: Cause your victim to become extremely sensitive to pain. Cause your victim to become extremely sensitive to pain.
- **Vomiting**: Upset the stomach of your enemy. Induce severe vomiting in your hapless victim.
- **Sleep**: Put an enemy to sleep, or remove his sleep defences. Put an enemy to sleep, or remove their sleep defences.
- **Haemophilia**: Deny them respite from their wounds. Deny your victim the ability to clot their wounds.
- **Anorexia**: A terrible condition, preventing one from eating. A terrible condition, preventing one from eating.
- **Weariness**: Weaken the body of your opponent. Weaken your opponent's body.
- **Blight**: The most feared of the Curse abilities. A blight is a magical construction, sourced in the netherworld, wrapped around a traditional curse which will mask the effects of your attacks. Note that the use of the blight is more mentally draining than a traditional curse. Curse mana costs are increased by +120
- **Inflame**: Set a victim's very blood aflame. This potent curse will cause your target's blood to catch a flame. It requires that your victim first be bleeding; the more they suffer, the greater the potency of the curse.
- **Tzantza**: Bring a most painful end to your victim. This requires at least one of the listed afflictions to pull off. Though once you have their mind in your grasp you can use the devastating Tzantza curse to shrink their head. Watch the fun as their skin draws tighter and their skull implodes upon itself. The more afflictions your victim suffers over, the faster the process will proceed. At six afflictions, death is instantaneous. Valid afflictions are: Agoraphobia, Amnesia, Claustrophobia, Confusion, Dementia, Dizziness, Epilepsy Impatience, Masochism, Paranoia, Recklessness, Stupidity, Vertigo, Addiction
- **Jinx**: The ultimate shamanic curse. This highest of the shamanic arts allows a practitioner to combine two curses into one assault, delivering both simultaneously. However, due to the extreme complexity of such an undertaking, the practitioner must prepare themselves to channel the malign energies, by utilising the lesser curses to build up a sufficient charge.

### Details

- **Fashion**: Fashion vodun dolls from rope and cloth. After the first fashioning to create the doll, you will have a crude humanoid vodun doll. Wield the doll and FASHION DOLL OF <target>. As you fashion your doll further, you'll notice that it gets closer to being a semi-living state. Further, the more fashions you have with your doll, the more Vodun powers you'll be able to channel through the doll. Additionally, your doll will decay at a rate of 50% every 6 minutes after the last fashion. This effect does not occur for dolls of allied adventurers. If you find yourself in need of destroying a doll that is no longer in your possession, you may unravel it from afar. Note that this is a difficult feat, and will result in being thrown off balance for a long period of time.
- **Gripping**: Grip your dolls tighter than ever before. While using this ability, you will prevent items from being knocked out of your grip.
- **Confusion**: Muddle the thoughts of your victim. This will cause your victim to become mentally confused.
- **Dizzy**: Cause your enemy to become unsteady on his feet. Curse your victim with the dizziness affliction.
- **Sleep**: Send someone to the dreamworld. Either put your victim to sleep, or remove a sleep-related defence.
- **Illusion**: Cause your victim to see an image of your choosing. Cause your doll's namesake to see something specific. You may embed a single newline in the illusion by including the characters \n at the appropriate spot.
- **Spy**: See the surroundings of your target. Get a view of what's in your target's surroundings. If your doll is above 30 fashions this skill requires nothing to use.
- **Status**: Find out what the health and mana of your target is. Through your vodun doll, you may see the health and mana of your target.
- **Strip**: Strip someone's defences. This ability will strip a defence from your doll's namesake.
- **Paralyse**: Lock up the muscle system of your victim. Paralyse the nervous system of the victim.
- **Break**: Break both the arms or both the legs of someone. With this ability, you may break both the arms or legs of your victim.
- **Throttle**: Crush the life out of your victim. As long as the doll's living twin is in the area you can inflict the effigy with a choking curse, and watch in joy as its likeness asphyxiates.
- **Reckless**: Induce a sense of extreme bravado in your opponent. With this ability, you'll cause your victim to think he or she is always on full health.
- **Tickle**: Tickle the feet of your doll. By tickling the bottom of your dolls' feet, you will cause your victim to laugh convulsively.
- **Burn**: Set your victim alight. Set your victim alight!
- **Bleed**: Bloodlet your enemy. Cause blood to begin pouring from the pores of your unlucky opponent.
- **Travel**: Travel through the spirit pathways to your doll. With this ability, you may cross a continent to travel to your victim.
- **Cripple**: Break all the limbs of a victim. Break all the limbs of a victim.
- **Command**: Your wish is their command. Command your victim to perform a specific action.
- **Slow**: Make a victim move through time more slowly. Slow the movement of your victim through time.
- **Puncture**: Destroy your target's internal organs. Push your fingers sadistically into the gut of your victim, damaging their torso in the process. Sometimes this damage is enough to cause them to no longer be able to eat.
- **Blackout**: Rob your victim of some of his senses. Blackout some of the senses of your opponent, temporarily.
- **Summon**: Summon your victim to you. Summons your doll's model to you providing he is in the same area or an adjacent room. This will bypass monolith sigils for people who consider you an ally if you are within standard ranged distances or line of sight.
- **Bind**: Tie up your hapless opponent. Tie up a victim through your doll.
- **Listen**: Hear talking through your target's ears. With this ability, you'll be able to hear anything anyone who is in the room with your victim says.
- **Intuition**: You know them more than they know themselves. As you refine your doll, your knowledge of your victim shall grow to the point that even while clumsy your efforts to bring down curses upon them shall succeed more often than not(*). * The doll must be wielded and this ability has no impact until you have at least ten fashions.
- **Inventory**: See the inventory of your target. See the possessions in the inventory of your victim.
- **Imbibe**: Afflict your opponent through your doll. Imbibe a venom directly into the bloodstream of your doll's model with a pin. You must have a vial of whatever venom you wish to imbibe with in your inventory.
- **Mangle**: A pain beyond that of mere breaking. Though breaking someone's limb is certainly painful, mangling it is far worse.
- **Concussion**: Bruise the very brain of your doll's model. An extremely potent affliction, the concussion gives one afflicted with it amnesia every second.
- **Leech**: Attach yourself to the spirit of your victim. This interesting and useful ability will allow a Vodun master to leech experience from a victim who is in a hunting party. What it essentially does is give experience to the Vodun master as if he or she was a part of the hunting party that the victim is in.
- **Truefashion**: The hallmark of a master of Vodun. With TRUEFASHION, you have achieved a level of skill whereby sometimes you can get two fashions in the time it normally takes to get one.
