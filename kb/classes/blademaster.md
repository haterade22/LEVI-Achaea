### Details

- **Preparation**: A scabbard is as important as one's blade. A properly positioned scabbard is integral in drawing one's sword with ease, grace, and power. Neglect your scabbard at your peril, for the First Art is useless without it.
- **Bladename**: Every sword is unique. Every sword has a name, just as every sword has an owner; the first step towards mastery of the Two Arts is to learn this. In order to gain your sword, seek out Kavaya near the Eastern Ithmia, east of the City of Delos.
- **Drawslash**: A single, measured slash. Draw your blade and aim a single slash at your target before resheathing. With the Weaponry ability of TARGETING, this attack may optionally be targeted at a particular body part.
- **Gripping**: Clench your blade with an unshakable grip. While using this ability, you will prevent items from being knocked out of your grip.
- **Doya**: Strike true with the slow might of the Earth stance. Stance: Earth Ever steady underfoot, the Earth is a fixture of steadfast might. This strong, grounded stance favours sure strikes, granting a moderate increase to damage and accuracy at the cost of speed.
- **Raze**: Cleave through defensive auras. With raze, your sword will become a weapon of destruction, attacking the defences of your enemies that serve to keep your weapons out.
- **Armslash**: Cut across the arms. Draw and slash your blade across the arms of your enemy from either left or right. Such an attack will strike both arms in immediate succession, the second with less force than the first.
- **Engage**: Lash out at a cowardly foe. There is nothing more despicable and dishonourable than a coward, and this ability will allow you to punish cowards, as is their due. Once you have engaged an adventurer, if he is otherwise unhindered and tries to leave the room, you will get in an extra strike, whether you have equilibrium and balance or not. This ability may be initiated if off balance.
- **Discernment**: Quickly assess an opponent's wounds. With a keen and practised eye, discern the severity of any bleeding wounds your opponent may endure.
- **Legslash**: Slash across the legs. Draw and slash your blade across the legs of your enemy from either left or right. This horizontal attack will strike both legs in immediate succession, the second less powerfully than the first.
- **Thyr**: The whirling Wind stance neglects power for speed. Stance: Air Unceasing and all-pervasive, the Wind flows and soars invisibly and unpredictably. This graceful stance permits somewhat greater speed and accuracy of attack, but blows dealt will be significantly less powerful.
- **Impale**: Drive your blade straight through your target. This wicked ability may be used when your hapless victim has fallen to the ground and lies there, helpless. With it, you will drive your sword into his body, causing regular damage as long as he is on the sword. Further, while impaled, he will find himself unable to move, though you too will be unable to move.
- **Centreslash**: Carve up or down across the head and chest. Draw your blade and slash your enemy with a rising or falling vertical blow. This attack will strike your foe's head and torso in succession, depending on from which direction the blow comes, dealing less damage to the second area struck.
- **Balanceslash**: Drive your enemy back with a balanced slash. This powerful, measured blow will drive your target back, knocking them off-balance.
- **Infusefire**: Wreathe your blade in crackling flame. By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may heat your blade to a scorching temperature. A weapon so empowered will burn as fire the next time it is drawn, potentially setting your target ablaze. If a target is already ablaze, extra damage may be dealt. ** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
- **Pommelstrike**: Deliver a stunning blow with the hilt of your sword. Drive the pommel of your blade into your opponent's skull, delivering a swift and accurate blow.
- **Mir**: The adaptive Water stance favours defence. Stance: Water Patient and unchanging, yielding yet impervious, Water is supremely adaptive to its surroundings. This flowing stance is slow to attack but affords tremendous physical defence.
- **Predict**: Know yourself, know your foe. The trained blademaster shall be able to assess the state of a foe's limbs with a single glance.
- **Bladesuicide**: If death must come, be it by your own blade. In the direst of circumstances, so long as you still keep your blade, your life remains in your own hands. By driving your sword through your own heart, you may bring immediate death.
- **Retaliationstrike**: Unleash a fierce strike against your attacker. Prepare yourself to strike back against your foes. Any time a physical blow is struck against you, you have a chance to loose your blade upon your attacker in retaliation.
- **Infuselightning**: Command lightning into your sword. By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may charge your blade with volatile electrical currents. A weapon so empowered will strike as lightning the next time it is drawn, and shall cause your foe's muscles to lock up in clumsiness. ** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
- **Bladetwist**: Torment an impaled foe. Should your enemy be impaled upon your sword, you may twist the blade, gouging through their innards and letting a torrent of blood.
- **Arash**: Aggressive and powerful, the Fire stance is one of attack. Stance: Fire Flickering and ephemeral, Fire manifests in the weaving, surging flame and the overwhelming force of sudden combustion. Blows struck from this reckless, offensively focused stance will deal significantly more damage, with more speed, but you will be more vulnerable to attacks.
- **Infuseice**: Suffuse your blade with ice. By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may chill your blade to a glacial state. A weapon so empowered will strike as ice the next time it is drawn, and may freeze your target into inaction. ** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
- **Multislash**: Spill blood with a succession of slashes. By compounding your focus, you may slash your target thrice in a single, continuous motion. Such an attack will deal more damage and draw more blood than a lesser slash, but not permit an accompanying strike.
- **Compassslash**: Send their blood to the points of the compass. Draw and slash your target from one of the cardinal directions, causing more bleeding than a lesser slash. The points of the compass: NORTH: head EAST:left arm SOUTHEAST: left leg SOUTH: torso SOUTHWEST: right leg WEST: right arm
- **Infusevoid**: Wield the Void and your sword as one. By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may fold the recondite power of the Void about your blade. A weapon so empowered will flow as a shadow the next time it is drawn, carving through armour and other physical protections as if a tool of magic, and may negate one of your opponent's defences. ** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
- **Impaleslash**: Let their blood flow freely. When an opponent writhes impaled upon your blade, with this technique you may carve through vital organs, causing severe internal bleeding and hampering your enemy's ability to clot their wounds.
- **Sanya**: The total focus of the Void stance amplifies inner power. Stance: Void Empty of all things, Void is absolute nothingness without boundary or end. The clarity gained by driving away thought, and by freeing your mind of distraction, in contemplation of Void, will enhance your connection with your physical form. Striking from this stance will yield more Shin energy, and less will be consumed by most abilities.
- **Brokenstar**: Draw the broken star across your foe. Unleash the fatal fury of a master swordsman by carving the broken star. In the sanguinary heat of battle, when your opponent's bleeding reaches a suitably profuse intensity, let your blade guide your hand as four perfect, precise strokes cut free their soul.

### Details

- **Constitution**: Maintaining your intestinal equilibrium. This ability will gain you a stomach of steel.
- **Sternum**: A painful blow to the breastbone. Inflict significant pain with a splintering strike to your target's breastbone, located at the centre of their ribcage.
- **Shoulder**: Induce a state of weariness in your target. By harassing pressure points near an enemy's shoulder with a frontal strike, weaken important muscles and leave them weary.
- **Hands**: Send a numbing pain into your foe's hands. By directing your strikes to your opponent's hands it will cause them to go numb briefly.
- **Fitness**: Control your breathing. With the increased lung power you have gained from your bodily mastery, you are able to purge the effects of asthma.
- **Ears**: Turn your opponent into a clumsy fool. The inner ear helps to control one's balance. Box your victim's ears with twin open-handed blows to leave them clumsy and staggering.
- **Nose**: A disfiguring strike to turn even the most loyal. Comprised of delicate cartilage, the nose is a vulnerable point. By smashing the organ with a swift strike, leave your victim unrecognisable to allies, and unable to command loyal minions.
- **Knees**: Send your target sprawling to the ground. Manipulating your opponent's knee joint with a well-placed blade hand will reliably send them falling to the ground. If they are elevated by sitting upon a steed or similar, you'll instead be able to get a grip upon them and yank them from their mount.
- **Alleviate**: Cure yourself of pesky afflictions. Detailed understanding of the body has therapeutic benefits. By actively stimulating relevant muscles and pressure points, you may heal yourself of an ailment.
- **Neck**: Cause your opponent's limbs to lock up. Clustered nerves in the neck leave a target vulnerable to lasting paralysis if struck with precision.
- **Kidney**: Impede your foe's ability to process elixirs A blow to the kidneys will impede your foe's ability to process elixirs, causing symptoms similar to addiction.
- **Eyes**: Force your enemy to witness madness. By rupturing blood vessels in a target's eyes, leave them blind and unable to trust what they see.
- **Airfist**: Channel Shindo into a blustering gale. Through Shin mastery, channel the wind itself to scourge a foe, hindering their attempts to parry or guard blows. ** Shin energy is necessary to perform elemental strikes. Only one element may be channelled upon a foe at a time.
- **Hamstring**: Cripple your target's movement. Crippling the hamstring muscles of a target's upper leg will greatly hinder their ability to walk for a short time.
- **Temple**: Send your foe into a stupor. The head has many exploitable weak points. A strike to either temple, above and behind the eye, will rock the brain and dull your victim's mind, leaving them in a stupor.
- **Chin**: Envelop your opponent in a restful sleep. Jolt an opponent's skull with a powerful, upwards blow to their chin, nudging them into a brief slumber.
- **Icefist**: Channel Shindo into an icy strike. Through Shin mastery, channel the cold to encase a foe in a freezing rime of ice, rendering untouchable any magical tattoos they may bear. ** Shin energy is necessary to perform elemental strikes. Only one element may be channelled upon a foe at a time.
- **Flamefist**: Channel Shindo into a fiery strike. Through Shin mastery, channel fire to engulf a victim in a cloak of flames that will scorch away any impending anti-weapon aura of rebounding. Such conflagrating will, however, cauterise the wounds of the target, resulting in them bleeding somewhat less for the duration. ** Shin energy is necessary to perform elemental strikes. Only one element may be channelled upon a foe at a time.
- **Voidfist**: Channel Shindo into a powerful vortex. Through Shin mastery, channel the very emptiness of the void upon a target, enabling each of your future strikes to be enhanced with the draining power of the void. When striking a voidfisted opponent, their very mind shall be sapped, and they shall lose mana. ** Shin energy is necessary to perform elemental strikes. Only one element may be channelled upon a foe at a time. Note: Shin generation is halved when striking a voidfisted opponent.
- **Groin**: Instil a sense of heroism into your target. A vulnerable point for both males and females, the groin, when struck, may cause a foe to block out the overwhelming pain endured, wilfully ignoring any further damage to their body.
- **Chest**: Afflict your enemy with constant affliction. This combination of strikes to pressure points upon the chest of your victim will induce a variety of painful sensations upon them, mimicking the ongoing symptoms of numerous other ailments.
- **Throat**: Remove your foe's ability to breathe. A blow to the vulnerable throat of an enemy will leave them gasping and unable to breathe.
- **Stomach**: Imbue a loss of desire to eat. A precise strike to the stomach will cause muscles to spasm, leaving your winded target unwilling to eat and unable to digest.
- **Underarm**: Force them to secrete a slick sweat. By assaulting the sensitive armpit of a foe, you may stimulate their sweat glands, causing an unnaturally profuse degree of perspiration.
- **Deathstrike**: Unleash the fury of your martial prowess. With absolute knowledge of your victim's anatomy, unleashing a fatal combination of strikes to vulnerable points is but a matter of timing.

### Details

- **Weathering**: Rigorous training yields a toughened physique. Your budding control over your body will assist you in weathering the storm of life by increasing your constitution by one point.
- **Augment**: Your bladework reaches new heights of potency. By augmenting your reflexes with shin energy, you will reach such a degree of skill with the blade that you are able to deflect blows aimed at your limbs, even when prone. ** This ability is used in conjunction with the parry ability.
- **Breathing**: Control your breathing to resist choking attacks. This ability will grant a short-term immunity from anything doing asphyxiation damage. However, any offensive activity or consumption of balance or equilibrium will cause you to begin breathing once again.
- **Shintrance**: Sink into a reverie of battle. Swordplay and Striking are supremely graceful arts, the fury of bloodshed tempered by rhythm, control, and prowess. Entering the Shin trance, you may sensitise yourself to the cadence and flow of combat, accumulating Shin energy with every strike you land upon a foe. Successfully channelling this energy is essential in order to unleash many of the advanced Shindo, TwoArts, and Striking techniques. The more powerful your opponent, the more Shin energy you will gain from striking them. Shin energy will slowly dissipate over time.
- **Shadow**: Surveil from the shadows. From the ground or in the trees you may attempt to shadow an adventurer in an adjacent location. If you are not detected, you will enter their location unseen and begin to follow them.
- **Detect**: Find others throughout the land. A useful benefit of the intense training of body and mind inherent to the practise of Shindo is an increased sensitivity to the latent telepathic powers all mortals possess. By honing your focus, you may detect the location of other adventurers.
- **Deaf**: Will silence upon the world. Through force of will, you may block out all sound, rendering yourself deaf.
- **Hearing**: Restore aural sensation. No trauma may deprive you of your hearing should you wish otherwise. Concentrate, and the sounds of the world will return.
- **Blind**: Render your eyes unseeing. Meditating upon the Void, you may will your eyes to cease their function, blinding yourself.
- **Sight**: See the world anew. You have sufficient control over your body to instantly repair your eyes when blinded.
- **Nightsight**: Vision in darkness as in light. The dark of night is no barrier to your trained eyesight.
- **Bind**: Brace yourself with Shin energy. Once so bound, any Shin energy accumulated beyond the maximum you may store will invigorate your body, sparking a renewal of health.
- **Blocking**: Deny them escape. With the ability to block exits, you cut off the chances for your cowardly opponent's to flee your blade.
- **Toughness**: Strengthen your body against assault. By increasing the density of your skin, you are able to decrease the amount of damage you will take from physical cutting attacks.
- **Projectiles**: Weave and dodge missiles. Heightening your reflexes, you may sense and avoid many projectiles.
- **Dodging**: Elude attacks from the air. This ability will allow you to dodge attacks from the air.
- **Sturdiness**: Stand steadfast against acts of force. By concentrating on increasing your mass, you are able to gain immunity from most attempts to move you against your will. It is lost when you next use balance or equilibrium.
- **Leap**: Vault over obstacles. Walls, rubble, and other impediments are no longer a problem to one with your training. What you cannot get around, you may go over.
- **Immunity**: Withstand the foulest of poisons. By stirring your circulation, you may prepare yourself to shrug away the next poison that enters your bloodstream. As venoms are so wide in variety, such a state of readiness requires the whole of your focus; performing any action will end your immunity.
- **Burst**: A crude release of force. Shin energy is a source of power both pliant and potent. You may unleash a measure of this energy upon a target in a crude but damaging burst of raw force. This attack will bypass, but not destroy, magical shields and prismatic barriers, and does more damage against both prone opponents and those suffering a damaged head(*). * These bonuses both stack with one another, but with diminishing returns.
- **Dash**: Fly across the ground. Sprinting in a particular direction, you may rapidly cover a great distance.
- **Waterwalk**: Dance over the waves. So swift are your footsteps that water will not part beneath your feet, permitting you to glide across the surface.
- **Clarity**: Behold no deception. Those who would hide in the shadows cannot escape your well-trained eyes and ears. Your increased awareness may also see the truth behind magical illusions, although the chances of detecting this type of artifice are less reliable.
- **Mindnet**: Know of all who approach. Reaching out with your every sense, abilities heightened by rigorous mental training, you may become aware of the comings and goings of others nearby. Such concentration will be a constant burden on your mana reserves.
- **Consciousness**: Maintain awareness at all times. Such is your iron will that you may cling to consciousness where lesser minds would fail.
- **Healthtrans**: Heal your wounds with Shin power. Releasing Shin energy inwardly, you may instantly mend wounds and restore your body. The more energy accumulated, the more you may transmute in this feat of regeneration.
- **Manatrans**: Fortify your mind with Shin power. Allowing Shin energy to focus your thoughts and elucidate your mind, you may instantly replenish your mana reserves. The more energy accumulated, the more you may transmute in this way.
- **Highleap**: The sky is within your reach. So powerful are your legs that you may leap high into the sky, briefly eluding gravity's grip. Beware that you lack the control to move about while up in the air.
- **Annihilation**: Fury given fiery form. Sparked into flame by inner fury, you may release the unbridled might of your accumulated Shin energy in a flood of flames that will scorch all within reach. So forceful is this burst of heat that others may be flung from the location. Focus and control are essential in performing this advanced technique, lest you yourself succumb to the fire.
- **Thunderstorm**: Unleash a galvanic surge of power. As the storm releases its might through crackling bolts of lightning, so may you channel Shin energy into a savage electrical assault upon all in the vicinity. The galvanic forces released are likely to jangle the nerves of those struck.
- **Blizzard**: Quench your foes with a storm of ice. Through a secret Shindo technique familiar only to masters of the art, you may draw the heat from the air around you into yourself. Manipulating the resultant freezing atmosphere into a shearing blizzard, you may scourge all nearby with lashes of ice, freezing them and destroying any heat vibration that might be present at your location. In addition, once your power leaves your control, it shall settle in the location, forming a temporary obscuring snowstorm.
