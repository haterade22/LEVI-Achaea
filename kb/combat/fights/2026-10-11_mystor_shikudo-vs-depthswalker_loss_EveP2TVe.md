## Answers to the open questions

1. **Telepathy without a lock: not tested.**
   - Every MIND ability was sent while locked. The lock finished at 12 and at 85, and was never reported lost after 85.
   - MIND IMPATIENCE worked while Mystor was both locked and paralysed (288-297).
   - One Sapience cast ran about 32 s (94 to 1101) with no lost-lock line, even at death.
   - It is not shown whether Sapience survives a lost lock. Nothing printed between 59 and 105, but Belaziel may simply have been idle in his dilation meditation (58).
2. **Blackout:** none in the log.
3. **Kata limits and stumbles.**
   - Rain reached 15 (530), and the transition to Oak at 15 went through (612-616). Oak reached 9 (817).
   - No stumble, rhythm or refused-transition line, and no ADOPT.
   - Parried strikes still count toward the kata (335, 425, 530).
   - The system's ` Kata is N` suffix runs one behind when a combo's first strike is parried (418-424).
4. **Kai Surge / Choke cost:** neither was used.
   - KAI BOOST cost 11 kai (606 to 632) and doubled kai gain from +5% to +10% per reap.
   - TRANSMUTE was 1:1 mana to health with no kai change (272, 409).
5. **Dawnkick's epilepsy line:** there was no dawnkick in this fight, and no delayed line followed any of Mystor's strikes.
6. **Light, parried and absorbed strikes.**
   - Five strikes were parried. For each, the system prints `You: <strike> <limb> -> Belaziel` with no %, then the game prints "Belaziel parries the attack with a deft manoeuvre." (331-332, 418-419, 521-525, 623-624).
   - A parried strike has no effect line and no justice rebound.
   - No light or absorbed strikes, and no fumble line, even though Mystor was clumsy from 20:17:33 to the end: 18 strikes, 13 landed, 5 parried.
7. **Prone extras for hiru / hiraku / risingkick:** not testable here. Belaziel was never prone: he was thrown off his mount three times and remounted each time. The three risingkicks on a mounted target printed only the normal line (619, 716, 811).
8. **Shields and rebounding:** none on either side, and no SHATTER. The only defence stripped was Mystor's fangbarrier, by slickness (771).

## Lessons for our package

**For our Shikudo offense**
- **A mounted target.** Frontkick only throws him off the mount (126, 191, 329), and he re-vaults within 1-2 s (145, 241, 354).
  - Our strike tracking (triggers 563-585) must read "throwing him from his steed" as dismounted, not prone.
  - Prone, the precondition for Dispatch, never happened in this fight.
- **His parry is visible.** Sapience shows his `parry <limb>`.
  - After `parry right arm` (360), all four strikes to the right arm were parried (418, 521, 524, 623). Strikes to other limbs landed.
  - The one right-arm strike that landed under that parry (815) came straight after nervestrike paralysed him (813-814). That fits paralysis preventing parry, but it is one sample.
  - Steer away from the parried limb, or open with nervestrike.
- **Nervestrike has an attacker-side line** when the paralysis lands (719, 814). We can confirm paralysis from it, and kb/combat/shikudo.md should be corrected.
- **A separate `transition to oak form` before the combo works** at Rain 15, and Oak counts from 1 (612-629).
- **Kai was banked, not spent.** He reached 64% (998) and used none of it after the boost.
- **Kelp afflictions alone do nothing against him.** All 8 were erased round by round. MIND IMPATIENCE at least cost him a plumbum (387).

**For our defence against a Depthswalker**
- **Cure timeloop the moment it lands.** One uncured timeloop (374) doubled 8 instills.
  - Rank it above justice: justice cost a monk only 43-66 per strike (529, 621, 628).
  - Rank it above retribution, which bites only on a mana sip (478-479).
  - I did not check our SSC tables for any anti-Depthswalker ordering.
- **Treat mana as life.** DICTATE kills below 40% mana, plus 5% per amplifier (kb:153).
  - Against this class, do not TRANSMUTE (about 4,010 mana spent, 270-705).
  - Do not FOCUS under shadowmadness (400 each, 679, 936).
  - Do not sip mana with retribution up (966 damage).
- **Anorexia is the key to the softlock.**
  - FOCUS picked masochism and dizziness instead (875, 933).

  - Hypochondria later gives impatience on its own, with no attacker line (945), which closes off FOCUS. Cure hypochondria early (argentum, 598).
- **Recklessness fakes the vitals.**
  - From 998 the prompt read exactly full health and mana while he was truelocked and his last real mana reading was 52% (973).
