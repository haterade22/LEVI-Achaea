Pebble (Elementalism)  ABADMIN ID: 3290       Known: No (1336 lessons required)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            CAST PEBBLE AT <target>
Required Channels: earth

Works on/against:  Adventurers
Cooldown:          2.30 seconds of equilibrium
Resource:          90 mana
Details:
Compress a handful of earth into a single dense pebble and hurl it at a
target. Should the pebble strike true, it will shatter one of your
target's healthy limbs, as well as causing damage. If every limb has
already been broken, the pebble will still wound your target, and your
resonance will not grow.
