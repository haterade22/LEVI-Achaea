Overload (Crystalism)  ABADMIN ID: 3289       Known: No (3301 lessons required)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            VIBE DESTABILISE <vibe> <target> <affliction>
Details:
Having mastered the art of destabilising crystalline vibrations, you may
force the unravelling frequency beyond its limits and direct the
overloaded resonance at a foe standing alongside you. As the vibration
is destroyed and its unstable effects unleashed upon the location, the
overloaded frequency lashes out at your chosen target, afflicting them
with paralysis, asthma, slickness, anorexia or stupidity as you choose.

Overloading a vibration demands the same focus as destabilising one, and
requires both balance and equilibrium. The equilibrium cost will be that
of the destabilised vibration or of the overload itself, whichever is
greater.
