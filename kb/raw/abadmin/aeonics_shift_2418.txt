Ability: Shift (Record #2418)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: A slip sideways through time.
Syntax: CHRONO SHIFT [BOOST]
Abfile:
This powerful manipulation of the timestream shall cause your health and
mana to switch their current values. Note that this ability is potent
enough that even when not boosted, it shall incur an age cost (half the
standard boost cost).

If boosted, the equilibrium cost is significantly reduced.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 600 age
