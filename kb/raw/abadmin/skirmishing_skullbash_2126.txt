Ability: Skullbash (Record #2126)
Associated skill: Skirmishing
Skillpoints required: 30999
Blurb: Split their skull with a mighty blow.
Syntax: SKULLBASH <target>
Abfile:
Deliver a powerful blow to the head of an opponent.

Against adventurers, this requires that your target be prone and the
damage of this attack will be increased based on how damaged the
target's head already is.

When targeted against a denizen, this attack will deal damage, assuming
they lack the vitality to properly dodge your strike(*).

* This attack deals vastly reduced damage against denizens unless they
are below 33% health.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.30 seconds of balance
