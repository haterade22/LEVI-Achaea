Ability: Bladefire (Record #3037)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: Your sword shall cleave the shadows.
Syntax: PERFORM BLADEFIRE
Abfile:
You may now strike with flame as well as steel; after preparing to do
so, for the next 20 seconds or three strikes of your weapon(*) your
weapon shall set your target ablaze (or increase their burning) when it
strikes.

* On skills that strike twice such as doubleslash or doublewhirl, this
will only fire once per use.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of wrath balance
Resource: 10% Holy Wrath
