Ability: Sweep (Record #2495)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: A prone opponent is at their most vulnerable.
Syntax: SWEEP
Abfile:
Using your staff, you may sweep someone off their feet. Note that this
consumes both of your arm balances, so may not be combined with other
strike attacks (though it may be combined with a kick).

Note that this ability along with all other Shikudo strikes and kicks
must be performed in a combination. See AB SHIKUDO COMBO for details.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
