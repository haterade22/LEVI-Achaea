Ability: Dehydrate (Record #2050)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 21999
Blurb: Make them vulnerable to the consuming flame.
Syntax: CAST DEHYDRATE AT <target>
Abfile:
This working of fire and water will sap the excess moisture from your
victim, making them become dehydrated for a time(*) and suffer some
damage.

* Gives the nausea and weariness afflictions.
** If they already have weariness, they will be given a stack of
burning. If they already have nausea, gives a stack of freeze. If they
have both, it gives burning (not both freeze and burning).
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 45 mana
