Ability: Companion (Record #2731)
Associated skill: Emulation
Skillpoints required: 10999
Blurb: Before the fall.
Syntax: ENACT COMPANION <servitor type>
SERVITOR ACT
SERVITOR STATION
SERVITOR RETURN
ENACT COMPANION DISMANTLE
Abfile:
The Servitors that wander the city of Nur were once the hands of the
Aldar in everyday life. Not truly of the living, these constructs
handled everything from crafting to maintaining the wildlife.

Alas, no more true servitors shall be created, for the Finality upon his
throne is unlikely to return to such a pastime in the modern age. Yet
for all that some scraps of knowledge remain, and you may bring forth
one of these servitors at a time:

Aklan: causing this servitor to act shall have it inform you of everyone
in its local area.

Kindler: causing this servitor to act shall cause it to flood its room.

Varthiis: causing this servitor to act will cause it to strike all in
the room with its blades, inflicting a very small amount of damage.

You may cause a servitor to act when it stands with you, or remotely
if you have stationed it. Causing it to act remotely will make it return
to your side.

Note that imperfect as these servitors are, they shall not fight on your
behalf: all they come into being with is their directive to perform
their core function.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
