Ability: Care (Record #1031)
Associated skill: Spirituality
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Have your angel care for your physical well-being.
Syntax: ANGEL CARE [ON|OFF]
Abfile:
This ability will cause your Angel to regularly heal your afflictions at
a significant cost of power to her.

Note that this ability is mutually exclusive with devotion rite of
HEALING; only one of these two effects may take effect at any one time
even when both are active.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.80 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 Angel power
