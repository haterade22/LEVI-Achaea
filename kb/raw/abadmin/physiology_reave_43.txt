Ability: Reave (Record #43)
Associated skill: Physiology
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Kill another with catastrophic organ failure.
Syntax: REAVE <target>
Abfile:
If at least one of your target's humours is tempered, you can attempt to
shut down their internal organs, causing instant death. This process
will take some time to complete, during which you can perform no other
actions. Having more tempered humours simplifies the process, reducing
the time required to induce arrest.

* Reave takes approximately ten seconds to complete with a single
tempered humour. Each additional tempered humour reduces the duration by
two seconds, with a minimum time of four seconds when all four humours
are tempered.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 12.00 seconds of humour balance
Resource: 250 mana
