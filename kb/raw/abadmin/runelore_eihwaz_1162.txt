Ability: Eihwaz (Record #1162)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: A powerful vibration-dampening rune.
Syntax: SKETCH EIHWAZ ON GROUND/<weapon>
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Blue, 1 Yellow
Symbols: Yew

When sketched upon the ground, this rune will dampen non-protected
crystalline vibrations.

When sketched upon a weapon, this rune will randomly mask the effects of
venoms delivered by the weapon. To sketch it upon a weapon requires the
weapon to be a RUNEBLADE.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
