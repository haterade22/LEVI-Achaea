Ability: Gallowshumour (Record #2680)
Associated skill: Pranks
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: A tough crowd, this one.
Syntax: GALLOWSHUMOUR <target>
Abfile:
Sometimes the infamous greyfaces need a bit of a different approach to
coax out a smile. If wielding a puppet of one such poor soul, you may
enlighten him or her with some good old gallows humour, adding some real
character to his face in the process(*). Even then, you might need to
soften them up a bit first(**).

If used against a denizen, this skill does not require a puppet and
behaves differently. With your morbid humour, you will inflict psychic
damage upon the target using the better of your intellect or
strength(***). The closer they are to death, the sharper your wit
cuts(****).

* This will improve the fashioned state of your puppet.
** The more afflictions the target has, the more fashions you will
receive. They will require at least two and several things are excluded:
broken limbs, writhe afflictions, and anything with a nonstandard cure
such as time.
*** This skill deals damage based on whichever stat is higher between
your intelligence or strength.
**** You will deal increased damage to denizens under 50% health. This
bonus increases further on denizens below 25% health.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.10 seconds of balance
