Ability: Rake (Record #3263)
Associated skill: Valour
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: Command your eagle to fall upon a foe.
Syntax: EAGLE RAKE <target>
Abfile:
Command your eagle to fall upon a denizen opponent, raking them with its
talons and dealing damage(*). Note that you can only command your loyal
in this fashion every so often(**).

* This damage is based on your eagle's trained strength and whether it
is a Nightrender (see AB VALOUR NIGHTRENDER).
** Every 30 seconds.

Useable on: denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Cooldown
