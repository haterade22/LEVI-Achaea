Ability: Scales (Record #2866)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: Balance in all things.

The symbol of the scales represents balance. Yet when traced against an
enemy imperfections can be introduced in such a way as to make them
unbalanced, and the ruin this brings upon your foe shall leave them
reeling(*).

* Gives clumsiness.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
