Ability: Still (Record #2935)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 44999
Blurb: The silence of the grave.

endless eternity, quelling sound above a whisper(*).

* All forms of telepathic communication are greatly stifled, only
permitting one per person every 3 seconds. This effect lasts one minute.

Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.10 seconds of equilibrium
