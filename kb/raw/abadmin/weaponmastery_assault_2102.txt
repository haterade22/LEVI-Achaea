Ability: Assault (Record #2102)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 44000
Blurb: Unleash a terrible assault against a helpless opponent.
Syntax: ASSAULT <target> <HEAD|TORSO>
Extra info: Requires: 7 momentum
Uses: 5 momentum
Abfile:
This brutal attack can only be performed against a prone opponent. It
deals significant damage, and has different results based on the limb
targeted.

Head: raises the damaged state of the limb by a full level if possible.
Torso: fractures multiple of your target's ribs in one strike(*).

When targeted against the head it consumes 7 momentum; when targeted
against the torso only 3. This ability also has a longer balance cost
when used against the head.

* Two ribs if the target has no internal trauma. Three if they have mild
internal trauma. Four if they have serious internal trauma.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.80 seconds of balance
Skill specialisation: knight_dualblunt
