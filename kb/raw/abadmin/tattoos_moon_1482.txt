Ability: Moon (Record #1482)
Associated skill: Tattoos
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Regeneration of mana through the power of light.
Syntax: TOUCH MOON
Extra info: Inks: 1 Blue ink
1 Red ink
1 Yellow ink
Abfile:
This is a tattoo that will work automatically. When you have it
inscribed upon you (and activated), it will automatically restore a
portion of your mana anytime you have less than full mana. Each time it
works, there is a small chance of the tattoo fading. Activating this
tattoo will take balance.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
