Ability: Falcon (Record #511)
Associated skill: Discipline
Skillpoints required: 299
Blurb: Use the basic skills to interact with your falcon.
Syntax: FALCON
Abfile:
The art of Falconry is a noble one indeed. It is a sport reserved solely
for those of chivalrous breeding, for a bird as noble as the falcon
would never respond to commands given by one without honour.

When you initially gain Falconry, the first thing you must do is find a
hatchery. At the hatchery, FALCON REQUEST.

Hatcheries are located in:
* the Mhojave desert near the Ruins of el'Jazira
- the Ashtan resource hub
- the Cyrene resource hub
- the Eleusian resource hub
- the Hashan resource hub
- the Mhaldor resource hub
- the Targossas resource hub
- the manor of the Grand Merchant Collective, in Delos

(*) this is the only public hatchery.

Each hatchery gives out a different breed of falcon. The breeds vary in
appearance only: capabilities are identical.

Your falcon comes to you with assorted statistics which have been
randomly assigned.

If you cause your falcon to die, you will be refused another falcon.
There is absolutely no excuse for killing or conspiring to kill your
falcon.

Below is a list of commands that may be used once you have gotten your
falcon.

FALCON ASSESS    : See information about your falcon.
FALCON RECALL : Recall your falcon to your wrist. Requires a falconry
glove.
FALCON SANCTUARY : Order your falcon to a sanctuary where it will be
safe.
FALCON LAND      : Order your falcon to land on the ground.
FALCON CIRCLE    : Order your falcon to fly up into the skies.
FALCON TRAIN : Order your falcon to go train, in order to raise a stat.
FALCON REPORT : Gain information on where your falcon is and who they
are tracking and attacking.
FALCON REPORT LOCATION : This will tell you the location of your loyal
(this will incur an equilibrium cost.)
FALCON SLAY      : Order your falcon to slay someone.
FALCON HUNT : When your falcon is hungry, have it go hunt for its food.
FALCON FLEE : Set the amount of health remaining when your falcon will
flee battle.
FALCON SLEEP     : Order your falcon to sleep.
FALCON FEED RAT  : Feed the corpses of rats to your falcon.

As your falcon gains in
* Stamina:  its health will increase.
* Speed: it will attack more quickly as well as fly from place to place
more quickly.
* Strength: it will attack with more power as well as be able to carry
larger items.
