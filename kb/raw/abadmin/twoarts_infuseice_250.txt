Ability: Infuseice (Record #250)
Associated skill: TwoArts
Skillpoints required: 31999
Blurb: Suffuse your blade with ice.
Syntax: INFUSE ICE
Abfile:
By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may chill your blade to a glacial state. A weapon so empowered will strike as ice the next time it is drawn, and may freeze your target into inaction.

** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.

Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
