Ability: Subversion (Record #3097)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Until the well runs dry.
Syntax: ACCURSED PERFORM THE RITUAL OF SUBVERSION <target>
Abfile:
The ritual of Subversion is both the pinnacle of Memorium and the
ultimate expression of the depths that the art can be twisted to by one
of the Accursed. With mastery of the Logographs and the unhallowed will
of the Accursed combined, blood and sorcery may be combined in a ritual
better left lost.

After you have laid claim to the blood of your victim within one of your
stone jars, you may take up knife to craft the manner of their doom in
the Ritual Chamber of your crypt. Only freshly obtained blood will
suffice: stored blood may be sufficient for lesser works, but this is no
such working. You will require a full jar, for the work is long and
complex.

The ritual of subversion will take an ability of your victim and make it
yours as well, for a time.

* Each class has an ability or abilities associated for subversion. If
there are multiple choices, it is random which you will receive, though
there is a subtle suggestion during the ritual that this might be the
case.
** The class of the target at the time blood was taken is the one
targeted by the ritual. You may find some classes do not have abilities
to subvert, most obviously other Pariahs.
*** There is a cooldown on this ability, and it is a channel to
activate.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 15.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 4000 mana
