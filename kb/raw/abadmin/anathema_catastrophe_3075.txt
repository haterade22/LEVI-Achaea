manifestation; this terrifying form shall be more than enough to unleash
total destruction upon an appropriate victim(*).

and the other must not be.

Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of equilibrium
