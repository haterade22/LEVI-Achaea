Ability: Overhand (Record #2063)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 5000
Blurb: Split their skulls with a mighty blow.
Syntax: OVERHAND <target>
Abfile:
This powerful blow aimed at the head shall deliver skull fractures. The
more skull fractures your victim suffers from, the longer it shall take
them to recover from sipping a healing elixir or tonic.

Additionally, while afflicted with skull fractures, the target shall
periodically receive nausea. This periodic afflicting becomes faster
based on how many fractures are present.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance cost varies based on weapon speed
Skill specialisation: knight_twohanded
