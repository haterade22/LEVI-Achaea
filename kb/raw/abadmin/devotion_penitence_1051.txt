Ability: Penitence (Record #1051)
Associated skill: Devotion
Skillpoints required: 11999
Blurb: Condemn the unfaithful to eternal penance.
Syntax: PERFORM PENITENCE <target>
Abfile:
Through your piety to the Bloodsworn, you are able to force an
individual to perform penance for their crimes against Creation.

When used against an adventurer, this will brand them and upon granting
redemption to this person through death, your righteous work will be
rewarded with an increase in your Devotional energies.

Against denizens, this will scour your foe in righteous fire, forcing
them to take ten percent more damage from your mace(*) and the
weaponmastery attacks of your fellow Paladins(**). In this case, the
devotion cost of this holy art will be reduced but its equilibrium cost
will be slightly increased.

* SPIRITUALITY SMITE.
** All weapon-based attacks in the WEAPONMASTERY skill when performed by
a Paladin.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 300 devotion and 100 mana
Skill specialisation: devo_priest
