Ability: Flux (Record #2591)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: Your control was never in question.
Syntax: FLUX <target> <task1> <task2>
Abfile:
For one such as you multitasking when it comes to your element is
trivial. You may now strike your foe with up to two abilities at a
time(*). In addition, the first ability used in this way is more
powerful, and may have secondary effects(**).

* Abilities that can be fluxed are: choke, drench, perspire, thin, roil,
parch, wreck, latch, coldfate, torrent, blade, and downpour.
** See the individual ability files for more specifics on a per ability
basis.

Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
