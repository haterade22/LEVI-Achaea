Ability: Jackinthebox (Record #694)
Associated skill: Pranks
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: An innocent musical toy with a vicious bite!
Syntax: CONSTRUCT JACK-IN-THE-BOX
WIND JACK-IN-THE-BOX FOR <target>
Extra info: Commodities: 1 iron, 1 wood
Construct cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
Wind cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
Abfile:
With this ability, you may construct a jack-in-the-box that, when wound and targetted for a specific person (see syntax above), will kill that person when the puppet pops out, assuming the target is still in the room. You may not do anything else while you wind the jack-in-the-box.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance
