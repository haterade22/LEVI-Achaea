Ability: Shipcloak (Record #1776)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: A seaspell to conceal your ship for a time.
Syntax: INVOKE CLOAK[ING]
DISSOLVE CLOAK[ING]
Extra info: Power cost: 200
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
A seaspell to surround your ship in a cloak of darkness from other
ships. Flares shot within a certain distance of your ship will bring
your ship to light, however. Shooting a flare from your own ship while
cloaked will also dissolve your cloaking.
