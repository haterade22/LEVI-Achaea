Ability: Metabolise (Record #3275)
Associated skill: Avoidance
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Drive out the impurities.
Syntax: METABOLISE <affliction>
Abfile:
You may now focus upon a specific affliction(*) and train your
metabolism to drive off rapid multiple origin delivery(**) of that
affliction. For example, you may now METABOLISE IMPATIENCE, and if
Makarios gives you impatience you shall be afflicted as normal, but if
Ictinus then tries to give you impatience again in a short window there
after you shall resist it. However, if Makarios gives you impatience
then rapidly reapplies it, both will still afflict you.

* Only certain afflictions can be metabolised. Generally speaking: if
the tree tattoo cannot cure it and it is not cured by eating or smoking,
it cannot be metabolised.
** The window for this ability to consider delivery rapid is around two
seconds.
*** This ability only works against players as of this time.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
