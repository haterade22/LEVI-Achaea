Ability: Thurisaz (Record #1151)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 21999
Blurb: Call forth a spout of molten lava from the earth.
Syntax: SKETCH THURISAZ ON GROUND FOR <target>
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Blue, 1 Red
Symbol: Volcano

When sketched upon the ground, this rune calls forth a spout of molten
lava to injure a target. It fades shortly after use.

It will also prevent rooms that it is sketched in from being flooded for
a time.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
