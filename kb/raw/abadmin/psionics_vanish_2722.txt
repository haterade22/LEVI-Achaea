Ability: Vanish (Record #2722)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 47999
Blurb: Perception is everything.
Syntax: PSI VANISH <PARTIAL|TOTAL>
Abfile:
This advanced psionic ability will permit you to consistently annihilate
the knowledge of your presence from the minds of those in your
proximity. You may select partial(*) or total(**), total having all of
the benefits of partial but lasting half as long. This ability is very
taxing, and you may only enact any form of vanish every so often.

* A partial vanish shall hide your presence in a room.
** A total vanish shall hide your entry and exit, as well as your
presence on alertness.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: Yes
Useable paralysed: Yes
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
