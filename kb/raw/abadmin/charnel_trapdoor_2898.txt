Ability: Trapdoor (Record #2898)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: A step into the abyss.
Syntax: CRYPT TRAP BUILD TRAPDOOR
CRYPT TRAP DISMANTLE TRAPDOOR
Abfile:
Many things lie in wait for those that will trespass in your domain, but
may they dread each step and breath. You may create a trapdoor in a
location with an exit to the down; this will conceal said exit.
Furthermore, when your unsuspecting foes step onto the trapdoor they
shall plunge down into the blackness beneath.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
