Ability: Heartstop (Record #1403)
Associated skill: Survival
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Slay yourself by stopping your heart.
Syntax: HEARTSTOP
Abfile:
This lethal ability will cause your heart to immediately stop, killing yourself in an instant. Use it with caution, for it will cost you more experience than a standard death.

If you are a Vodun or Puppetry user, please be aware that using heartstop will destroy all puppets of the last person to do damage to you before you heartstopped.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
