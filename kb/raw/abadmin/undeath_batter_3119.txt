Ability: Batter (Record #3119)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Until they move no more.
Syntax: SOULBLEED BATTER <target>
Abfile:
Hammer at your foe with your undying fists. If they are protected with a
shield or prismatic barrier, this will be broken; if they are prone they
will take damage and be stunned, and if they are not prone they will be
knocked prone and take less damage but also be stunned.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.80 seconds of balance
