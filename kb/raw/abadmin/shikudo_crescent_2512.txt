Ability: Crescent (Record #2512)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: The deadly Kai-enhanced kick.
Syntax: CRESCENT
Abfile:
This kick may only be performed against a foe who lies prone. If that
foe has half their health or less, your Kai-enhanced kick shall slay
them instantly; if they do not, they will manage to dodge this kick, in
which event your recovery time shall be greatly increased.

Upon launching this kick you shall immediately flow into the Tykonos
form, so it is wise to be certain of your target's demise before
committing.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
