Ability: Distortaura (Record #965)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Distorting your aura will protect you from physical harm.
Syntax: DISTORTAURA
NORMALAURA
Abfile:
Tapping into your karma, you can distort your own aura, causing a
portion of physical damage dealt to you to be fed back on the attacker.
Your mana reserves will be constantly drained as long as your aura is
distorted. NORMALAURA will return your aura to its natural state.

A distorted aura additionally mitigates some damage from denizens,
though not from players.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 0.50% karma
Drain: Mana
