Ability: Hugalaz (Record #1153)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 26999
Blurb: Call down a hailstorm around you.
Syntax: SKETCH HUGALAZ ON GROUND
SKETCH HUGALAZ ON <weapon>
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Blue
Symbol: Ball of Ice

When sketched upon the ground, this rune brings a hailstorm down upon
your enemies. It fades immediately upon use.

When sketched upon a weapon, this rune provides some chance that the
weapon will deliver an additional burst of hail damage when it strikes.
To sketch it upon a weapon requires the weapon to be a RUNEBLADE.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
