Ability: Avoid (Record #3260)
Associated skill: Avoidance
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: The fundamentals of defensive posture.
Syntax: AVOID <PHYSICAL|ARCANE|MENTAL|AOE>
Abfile:
Learning to strike a foe is but half the battle, for they too will seek
to end your life. Studying this ability will allow you to focus on a
type of incoming attack and mitigate the damage you suffer from said
attack if you successfully avoid it. This ability does improve
considerably as you rise through the ranks in avoidance.

Note that some abilities are beyond your ability to avoid, and you may
only change what you are avoiding once every thirty seconds.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
