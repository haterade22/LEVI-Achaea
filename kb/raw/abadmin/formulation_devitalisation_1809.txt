Ability: Devitalisation (Record #1809)
Associated skill: Formulation
Skillpoints required: 24000
Blurb: The air itself turns against your victims.
Syntax: WIELD DEVITALISATION
THROW DEVITALISATION <AT GROUND|DIRECTION>
Extra info: Reagents: 2 aurum, 1 cuprum
Abfile:
This experimental compound is insidious indeed. Those that inhale it
shall find that over time their maximum health reserves shall steadily
decrease, and they will be unable to recover such until they have
separated themselves from the substance for a time. The alchemist should
be especially wary, as at this point all attempts to create a filter via
their protective mask have failed.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of balance
