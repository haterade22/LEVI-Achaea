Ability: Battaglia (Record #1663)
Associated skill: Harmonics
Skillpoints required: 28999
Blurb: Project your harmonics into neighboring rooms.
Syntax: PLAY BATTAGLIA <direction>
Abfile:
Send harmonics in the direction specified. For example, PLAY BATTAGLIA EAST will attempt to send all harmonics out of your current location towards the east. Harmonics do not always move, depending on limits in adjacent rooms.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 200 mana
