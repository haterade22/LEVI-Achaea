Ability: Reshape (Record #2918)
Associated skill: Charnel

Your command over your domain has grown to the point where you can now
reshape the chambers of your domain to fit your needs. There are various
types of chambers that your domain can possess, and you can reshape any
chamber within your domain excluding the initial chamber(*).

* This is the one you arrive in when crypt entering.
