Ability: Sever (Record #2368)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 19999
Blurb: A precise and deadly strike.
Syntax: WEAVE SEVER <target> [LEFT|RIGHT]
Abfile:
Using a sword, you will sever the muscles in one of your target's
arms(*). If you do not specify an arm, this attack will always target
the left by default.

* Delivers the clumsiness affliction.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
