Ability: Ironwill (Record #2713)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Your mind against theirs.
Syntax: PSI IRONWILL [TARGET]
Abfile:
By focusing all of your attention on the process(*), you may drive out
foreign influence upon your mind or the mind of a friendly target(**),
breaking any mindlocks if one is present.

* This is a channelled action: you may perform no other actions while
focusing on severing the mindlock.

** In the case where you target it at the person you have lifebonded it
will be the same delay as when targeting it at yourself: in all other
cases the channel is twice as long.


Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
