Ability: Comet (Record #2560)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: They cannot outrun the fire.
Syntax: IGNITE COMET <target>
Abfile:
When standing near an appropriate source of heat(*), you may rocket
across distances, smashing into those that would flee from you(**).

If performed from within your crucible and your foe does not stand
within a crucible already, your crucible shall shift with you to the new
location.

* Must be standing within your crucible or have a firewall between you
and your target. If they are multiple rooms away and you do not stand
within your crucible, firewalls will need to be between all of the
locations.
** The target must be in line of sight and will take damage as well as
being set ablaze.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
