Ability: Tentacles (Record #3080)
Associated skill: Anathema
Skillpoints required: 30999
Blurb: They shall not escape your grasp.
Syntax: MUTATE TENTACLES
FLAIL TENTACLES
Abfile:
One of the more complex basic mutations, you may mutate tentacles of
your own to clutch at your enemies that would seek to leave your
location(*). You may also flail your tentacles, yanking those in the sky
back to the ground.

* This is similar to the tentacles of an Occultism practitioner, though
due to your less refined touch with the delicate aspects of auric
manipulation you will find your tentacles last a far shorter time.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of anathema balance
Resource: 2% karma
