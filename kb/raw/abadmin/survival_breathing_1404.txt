Ability: Breathing (Record #1404)
Associated skill: Survival
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Controlling your breathing to avoid asphyxiation.

Abfile:

asphyxiation damage. However, any offensive activity or consumption of
balance or equilibrium will cause you to begin breathing once again.
