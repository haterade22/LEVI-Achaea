Ability: Squeeze (Record #2544)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: Crush the life from them.
Syntax: TERRAN SQUEEZE <target>
Abfile:
Take hold of your unfortunate foe and squeeze the life from them. This
attack will do more damage based on how much internal trauma your target
is suffering; if they have both serious internal trauma and a calcified
torso, they will be slain instantly.

Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
