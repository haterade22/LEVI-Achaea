Ability: Ensnare (Record #2121)
Associated skill: Skirmishing
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Mesmerise them with your flawless skill.
Syntax: ENSNARE <target>
Abfile:
You may ensnare the gaze of a prone opponent with your flawless skill
with your chosen weapon. This shall cause them to be transfixed if the
blindness defence is not present; otherwise it shall strip the blindness
defence.

Note: your target must be prone.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
