Ability: Smother (Record #2156)
Associated skill: Reclamation
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: Rob the flames of oxygen.
Syntax: RECLAMATION SMOTHER
Abfile:
When standing within your reclaimed network, you may cause the vines to
smother any flames that may have sprung up, extinguishing them all
immediately across the entire expanse of your reclaimed land. In
addition, when standing adjacent to your network in a room that is on
fire, you may utilise this ability to immediately expand your network
and smother the flames at the same time.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
Resource: 250 mana
