Ability: Thunderclap (Record #2183)
Associated skill: Weatherweaving
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: A burst of sound to rupture the ears.
Syntax: CAST THUNDERCLAP AT <target>
Abfile:
This spell will generate a shockingly loud clap of thunder. Against
adventurers it shall restore the target's hearing if they are deaf, or
instill the sensitivity affliction in them if they are not.

When used against a denizen, this spell will deal blunt damage.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.35 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 38 mana
