Ability: Conqueror (Record #2837)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 51999
Blurb: Go forth in His name.
Syntax: HELLFORGE CHANNEL CONQUEROR
Abfile:
Each time you slay an adventurer you will immediately regain balance
(though not equilibrium) and will also gain a surge of health. When you
slay a denizen you will gain the health, but not regain balance.

This is an ongoing hellforge armour effect.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
