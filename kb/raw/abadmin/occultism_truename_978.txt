Ability: Truename (Record #978)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Find the truename of your enemy.
Syntax: TRUENAME CORPSE
TRUENAMES
TRUENAMES FORGET <target>
UTTER TRUENAME <target>
Abfile:
Your occult knowledge has grown to such an extent that you can discern
the truename of an individual, the name that vibrates to that person's
soul, from his or her corpse. TRUENAMES will tell you whose truenames
you have and how many times you may utter it. If the truename of a
person is uttered in front of him or her, that person will be harmed in
body and mind and be inflicted by the Aeon curse. Once uttered, that
particular truename will be forgotten.

Truename requires entity balance and you cannot command an entity until
you recover equilibrium from using it.

Due to the mental strain of remembering an individual's truename, you
are only able to remember up to three for each person, for a total of
fifty people.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
