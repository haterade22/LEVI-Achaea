Ability: Skullcrush (Record #2100)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 25000
Blurb: Crush their skulls.
Syntax: SKULLCRUSH <target>
Extra info: Requires a blunt weapon of some form.
Abfile:
If you are wielding a blunt weapon in your hand, you may try to crush
someone's skull. Your opponent does not have to be in the room with you
when you begin your attempt, but by the time you get the second
attack-related message, he must be in the room, or the skullcrush will
be stopped.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
Skill specialisation: knight_dualblunt
