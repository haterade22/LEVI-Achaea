Ability: Jousting (Record #1530)
Associated skill: Riding
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Joust against other mounted opponents.
Syntax: JOUST <adventurer>
Abfile:
Similar to ram. You can attempt to joust a mounted opponent. If successful, you will knock him off his steed and viciously skewer him. You must be wielding a lance in order to joust. Be warned, however, that depending on your skill in riding and the level of your mount versus your opponent's skill in riding and level of his mount, there is a chance that you will be the one knocked off your steed and potentially skewered (for if your opponent has the jousting skill and is wielding the correct weapon, he will automatically lower it and attempt to skewer you).
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.50 seconds of balance
