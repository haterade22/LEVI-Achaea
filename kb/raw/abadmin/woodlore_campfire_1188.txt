Ability: Campfire (Record #1188)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Build a campfire to soothe the body and soul.
Syntax: BUILD CAMPFIRE

endurance and willpower at a greatly increased rate. When the fire
starts to burn out, you may stoke it to keep it going.

Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
