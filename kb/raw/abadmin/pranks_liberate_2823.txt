Ability: Liberate (Record #2823)
Associated skill: Pranks
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: They weren't using it anyway.
Syntax: LIBERATE TONGUE OF <target>
Abfile:
Once you've got a good idea of how someone's wagging their tongue, you
can grab that beauty right out of their mouth(*). You'll need to get it
just right though, so it'll take some observing.

Once someone is afflicted with stuttering, each time they
communicate(**) you'll get a better idea of what you need. Once you've
observed enough(***), the rest is up to you!

* Yes, they die. Tragic but what can you do.
** Any form of communication triggers this, as well as the puppet tickle
ability. Laughter might not be the best medicine in this case.
*** You'll need to observe four times to be able to liberate their
tongue.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.60 seconds of balance
