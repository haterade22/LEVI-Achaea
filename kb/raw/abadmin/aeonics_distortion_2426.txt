Ability: Distortion (Record #2426)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 53999
Blurb: Those that forget the past...
Syntax: CHRONO DISTORTION [BOOST]
CHRONO DISTORTION NORMALISE
Abfile:
This powerful act of time manipulation allows you to distort the
timestream in a location, causing all of your personal enemies to have a
chance to be moved slightly back in time each time they attempt to
leave. This will result in them failing to escape your location.

Note that boosting this ability removes the eq cost. However, the older
you are, the less able you are to maintain this spell, resulting in it
becoming progressively less potent the older you grow.

You may also normalise the temporal flow in a location, removing any
distortions. This is instant if the only distortion present was created
by you or if there are no other Aeonics practitioners present: otherwise
it has a channel.
Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 300 age
The ability name DISTORT is mapped to this ability.
