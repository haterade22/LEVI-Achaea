Ability: Infect (Record #2879)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Your minions carry much.
Syntax: SWARM INFECT <swarm> <plague>
Abfile:
Your deadly swarms bring ruin upon your enemies, but sometimes a more
insidious approach is required. Each swarm can be infected with one of
the deadly plagues that your arcane art may manufacture: these can then
be conveyed through various methods.

Note that the process of infecting takes time, so preparation before
battle is key.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
