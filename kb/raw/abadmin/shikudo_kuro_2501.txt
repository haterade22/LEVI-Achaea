Ability: Kuro (Record #2501)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: A lower strike to numb the legs.
Syntax: KURO <LEFT|RIGHT>
Abfile:
By targeting the muscles in your foe's legs, you may weaken them
severely, afflicting said target with weariness. Should your foe already
suffer from the weariness affliction, the blow will render them
lethargic.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
