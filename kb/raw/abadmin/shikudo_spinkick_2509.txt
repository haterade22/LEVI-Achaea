Ability: Spinkick (Record #2509)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 44999
Blurb: A crushing kick backed up by momentum.
Syntax: SPINKICK
Abfile:
This powerful kick will strike a target's torso if they're on their feet
or their head if they are sprawled upon the ground(*). In the event you
strike your foes head, they will take significantly more damage.

* If they are prone with a damaged head, this kick will immediately
mangle the target's head.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
