Ability: Epitomise (Record #3261)
Associated skill: Avoidance
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: A shadow of perfection.
Syntax: EPITOMISE
Abfile:
The master of avoidance may, for but a brief time(*) and only so
often(**), enter a state of such epitomised martial perfection that they
shall find themselves able to avoid onslaughts of multiple adventurers
when surrounded(***).

* Nine seconds duration.
** Fifty seconds cooldown.
*** When multiple attackers attempt to assault you, they will find that
many of their attacks outright miss.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
