Ability: Compression (Record #2629)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: An example must be made.
Syntax: AERO COMPRESSION <target>
Abfile:
After you have elevated the pressure surrounding a target
sufficiently(*), you may crush their skull like a grape, ending their
life in a manner fitting one who has dared cross you.

* The target must have had the pressure surrounding them elevated at
least six times.
