Ability: Exterminate (Record #148)
Associated skill: Necromancy
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Destroy all plant life in a location.
Syntax: EXTERMINATE
Extra info: Cooldown upon completion: 12.00 seconds of equilibrium.
Abfile:
Using your command of the energies of death, you are able to wipe a
forest area clean of plant life, replenishing some of your essence. Be
warned though, there is an essence cost to this ability, and unless
there is a sufficient amount of plant life in the room, you may lose
essence using it. It should also be noted that an exterminated room is
no longer considered a forest room, so many Druidic abilities will not
work in such a room.

Take note: prolonged use of extermination will take a toll on your
essence reserves, and you will find that each subsequent attempt incurs
a greater cost in essence than the last to perform.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 6.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 5% life essence and 1000 mana
