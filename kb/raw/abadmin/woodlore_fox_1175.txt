Ability: Fox (Record #1175)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 1499
Blurb: Summon a lithe vixen to attack your enemies.
Syntax: SUMMON FOX
Abfile:
With this ability you may call a sly and cunning vixen to attack your
enemies. When she attacks your victim, if they are suffering from the
haemophilia affliction, the amount of bleeding she can inflict will be
higher.

* HELP MOBILES will show you what commands you can use with it.
** Those enemied to the forests will not be able to call upon the aid of
Nature to assist them.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
