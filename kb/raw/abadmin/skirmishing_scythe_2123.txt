Ability: Scythe (Record #2123)
Associated skill: Skirmishing
Skillpoints required: 23999
Blurb: Slice through the defences that would protect your foes.
Syntax: SCYTHE <target>
Abfile:
This ability with your chosen weapon will allow you to randomly strip a
defence from an enemy. It shall always target defences in this order:
prismatic barriers, reflections, mass, then a random defence.

* Note that it strips all reflections at once, not just one.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
