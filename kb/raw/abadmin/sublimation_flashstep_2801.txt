Ability: Flashstep (Record #2801)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 49999
Blurb: One with energy.
Syntax: ALIGN FLASHSTEP <direction>
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Only a master of the school of Alignment would be so bold as to risk
configuring their own nature. The flashstep allows you to align yourself
so utterly with the energy of the world that for the briefest instant,
you are one and the same. Transverse to any adjacent location after a
short delay: only being prone after initiation will prevent your egress.

Overdraw effect: the windup takes a second longer to complete but will
not be stopped by being prone.

A short balance loss will be incurred when flashstep resolves
successfully.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 1
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 5% Wellspring Energy
