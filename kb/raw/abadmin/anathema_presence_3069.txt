Ability: Presence (Record #3069)
Associated skill: Anathema
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: You are the terror that walks.
Syntax: UNNAMABLE PRESENCE [RELAX]
Abfile:
You may radiate the terrible presence of the Unnamable, a terror so
profound that it shall inspire fear in any mortal not warded against its
threat in some form(*).

* Citizens of Ashtan are immune outside the arena unless they are one of
your personal enemies.
** Gives the fear affliction. If it hits someone who already has fear,
forces a fear tick on that person.
*** While you are suffused with this presence, any manaleech affliction
you inflict upon someone shall also ravage their body.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of anathema balance
Resource: 2.50% karma
