Ability: Eruption (Record #2800)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 31999
Blurb: Delayed devastation.
Syntax: ALIGN ERUPTION <target>
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
This act of alignment will cause a minor volcanic eruption beneath the
feet of your target, dealing damage. This takes some time to enact, but
does not take concentration. You may perform other actions while the
eruption is being prepared. Note that you must start this within three
rooms of your target, but it shall strike them on completion so long as
they remain within the same area as they were on initiation.

Overdraw effect: the eruption will complete more swiftly.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: continent-wide
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 5% Wellspring Energy
