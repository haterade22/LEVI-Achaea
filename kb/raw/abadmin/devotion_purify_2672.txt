Ability: Purify (Record #2672)
Associated skill: Devotion
Skillpoints required: 54999
Blurb: Cleanse the land of necromantic corruption.
Syntax: PERFORM PURIFY
Abfile:
Drive out necromantic corruption, destroying the hands of the grave that
may clutch at the living in a location. If a Necromancer stands guard
over said hands, there is a delay during which the faithful may perform
no other actions, so focussed on their task are they.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 1
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.80 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 500 mana
