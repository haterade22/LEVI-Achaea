Ability: Insertion (Record #2712)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: A subtle touch of misdirection.
Syntax: PSI INSERTION <target> <CITY|PARTY|ARMY|TELL> <talker> <message>
Abfile:
Interfering in an individuals telepathic communication channels is a
trivial enough process if one knows how. It is said that the true born
Aldar could project their influence across an entire network, but alas
such is lost to all but the barest few. You may target an individual and
make them see a message on the channel you so desire, though the
telesense defence will make it apparent to them that someone is
interfering (though they will not see who).
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.40 seconds of equilibrium
