Ability: Seal (Record #3252)
Associated skill: Hypnosis
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: Poised to strike.
Syntax: SEAL <target> <delay in seconds>
Abfile:
The act of sealing a hypnotic trance primes it for use. You may seal
your trance at any time for three seconds and upwards, and suggestions
tick periodically every four seconds there after.

However, if you seal your trance while your victim suffers from a
deadened mind, the trance will sink more deeply into their psyche, tying
itself more intrinsically and becoming vastly more potent when
triggered(*).

* The delay can be set as low as one second, and the suggestions trigger
every three seconds there after.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: equilibrium and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 200 mana
