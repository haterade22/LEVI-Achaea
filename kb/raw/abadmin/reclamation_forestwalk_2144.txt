Ability: Forestwalk (Record #2144)
Associated skill: Reclamation
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: You are everywhere at once.
Syntax: RECLAMATION FORESTWALK <target>
Abfile:
You may cross the distance between yourself and someone standing in one
of your reclaimed locations in a single step, providing you are also
standing upon ground you have claimed.

This ability will be blocked by the presence of a monolith sigil or
similar.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 200 mana
