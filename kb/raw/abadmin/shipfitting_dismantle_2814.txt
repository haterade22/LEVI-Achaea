Ability: Dismantle (Record #2814)
Associated skill: Shipfitting
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Break down ship weapons and equipment.
Syntax: SHIPFIT DISMANTLE <equipment>
Abfile:
For those who find themselves without need of a piece of equipment or
weaponry, you may disassemble the offending article, returning some of
the commodities used in its construction in the process.

Note: Dismantling a float will not return any commodities.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
