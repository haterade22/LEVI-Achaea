Ability: Sacrifice (Record #2890)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: The price of power.
Syntax: SWARM SACRIFICE <swarm>
Abfile:
When need is high, you may sacrifice one of your swarms by flooding it
with your arcane power creating a pulsating burst of damage. It is a
costly use of your minions, though doing so will cause all in your
location (except yourself) to be struck down with the arcane plague that
said swarm was carrying. They shall also suffer damage from the excess
energy released.

This will tick up to three extra times - once for every 500 insects over
1000 consumed in the swarm. Each extra tick does less damage and no
plague.

* This will destroy the swarm utilised on use, including all insects
contained therein.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
