Ability: Strike (Record #2092)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 53000
Blurb: Unleash your ferocity against your foe.
Syntax: SHIELDSTRIKE <target> <HIGH|MID|LOW|DEFENCES>
Abfile:
Once you have generated sufficient ferocity, you may unleash a
devastating blow with your shield. This attack does not usually consume
balance(*) and may be followed with a standard combination attack.

When targeted high, the strike will stun your opponent.

When targeted to the midsection, the strike will deliver sensitivity.

When striking low, you will knock your opponent to the ground.

When striking at the defences, you will shatter a protective barrier
surrounding your target(**).

* When striking low before an attack, balance is incurred.
** Rebounding if present, then a magical shield if present.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Skill specialisation: knight_snb
