Abfile:
While both Aeonics and Shadowmancy made the Tsol'teth terrible indeed,
it was the art of Terminus that allowed them to perform many of their
more cataclysmic feats of power. Terminus allows one to use the ancient
Tsol'teth language to enact various outcomes; some are relatively minor,
and some are near miracles in their scope.

However, the knowledge of the Tsol'teth was guarded jealously, and no
one person could learn it all. As you progress through the skill you
shall gain access to various research trees that you may study. The more
you study a tree, the more abilities you shall unlock. As you progress
you shall also be able to study more trees, as your mental capacity
shall expand to accommodate.

Note: tier 1 through 4 abilities all cost one research point. Tier 5
abilities cost 2, though tier 5 abilities are not available until later
in Terminus.

You must be in an equipped Terminus lab to research trees - the only
currently known lab is accessed through the MEMORY ability in
Aeonics.
