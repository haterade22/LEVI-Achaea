Ability: Discard (Record #2420)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 35999
Blurb: The mind is your weapon.
Syntax: CHRONO DISCARD <amount> [BOOST]
Abfile:
You may discard away your vitality in order to expand your mind,
swapping health for mana at a 50% loss (so discard 1000 health, get 500
mana).
When boosted, this ability will not incur an equilibrium cost.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 150 age
