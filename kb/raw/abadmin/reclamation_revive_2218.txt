Ability: Revive (Record #2218)
Associated skill: Reclamation
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: Breathe life back into the fallen.
Syntax: REVIVE <target>
Abfile:
While standing on your reclaimed ground, you can breathe life back into
the corpse of a fallen adventurer.

NOTE: this ability will only work if the target is a mutual ally, or if
they are a member of the same city as you.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of equilibrium
