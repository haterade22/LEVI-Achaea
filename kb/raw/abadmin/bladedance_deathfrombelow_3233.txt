Ability: Deathfrombelow (Record #3233)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: From earth to sky with blade and voice.
Syntax: DEATHFROMBELOW <target>
Abfile:
Sometimes those of lesser footwork will think to take to the skies or
trees, foolishly believing that surely you cannot be as sublime in such
environs as your peerless grace upon the ground. You will soon disabuse
them of this folly, for with a single bound and a cry(*) you will bring
them straight back down to your level with a strike of your blade(**).

* This uses your warcry if you have one set.
** This deals damage and knocks them prone to the ground, as well as
incurring a relatively nontrivial blackout.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same location at any elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
