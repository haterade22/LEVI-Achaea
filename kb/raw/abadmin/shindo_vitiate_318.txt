Ability: Vitiate (Record #318)
Associated skill: Shindo
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: Your strength becomes another's frailty.
Syntax: VITIATE <target>
Abfile:
The potent forces of Shin energy can prove crippling to the uninitiated.
By directing them upon an opponent, you may weaken your foe for a time,
sapping their strength and dexterity. This also has a chance to
interfere with their physical and mental regeneration until they shrug
off the vitiating power.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 40 Shin energy
