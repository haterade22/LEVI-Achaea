Ability: Hound (Record #3163)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: The dance isn't over.
Syntax: BLADE HOUND <target>
Abfile:
Sometimes when you get behind a partner they're just going to try to
make a run for it. With this technique you'll hound their steps, and, so
long as you have their back or side(*), you'll be able to charge back
into the dance inflicting quite the damage while stunning them.

* If someone is in an adjacent room and you have back or side positions,
this ability will move you into their room and damage them as well as
conveying a moderate stun.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same location or adjacent at same elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 2
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
