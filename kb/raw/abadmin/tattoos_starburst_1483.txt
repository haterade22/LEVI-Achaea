Ability: Starburst (Record #1483)
Associated skill: Tattoos
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: The power of the stars provides immediate resurrection.
Syntax: TOUCH STARBURST
RELAX STARBURST
Extra info: Inks: 1 Blue ink
1 Gold ink
1 Green ink
1 Purple ink
1 Red ink
1 Yellow ink
Abfile:
This tattoo will work automatically upon your death, once you have
touched it. It will resurrect you immediately, and cause you to lose
only half the normal amount of experience that you would lose from a
death. RELAX STARBURST will remove the defence once activated.

* Certain abilities will prevent the resurrection effect of the
starburst, but will not dispel the active starburst you currently have.

Ability range: none

Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
