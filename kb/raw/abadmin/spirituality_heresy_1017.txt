Ability: Heresy (Record #1017)
Associated skill: Spirituality
Skillpoints required: 8999
Blurb: Summon rage at the heretics and hunt them with fervour.
Syntax: HUNT HERESY
Abfile:
Engulf your mace in righteous flame for a time, empowering it to burn
through your foe's defences.

When smiting adventurers with your mace, you will burn away the aura of
rebounding from your foes.

When smiting denizens, the strike will burn through their magical
shield. In such cases, your smite deals half its normal damage, and its
damage type becomes fire.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 400 mana
