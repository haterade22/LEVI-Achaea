Ability: Spatial (Record #2789)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: The ultimate form of Manipulation.
Syntax: MANIPULATE SPATIAL
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
To attempt to manipulate the fundamental laws of space and time is a
formidable task indeed. Those of the school of Manipulation know well
the dangers of the latter, and have mastered the former. You may twist
space in a location just so, that any attempting to leave rooms adjacent
to the distorted location via magical means of travel will have the
potential to fail said attempts.

A unique aspect of this ability is its capacity to enhance the
Displacement ability of Alchemy. An alchemist's attempt will bypass the
protective measures of a monolith sigil placed at the target's location.
However, the attempt may still fail due to the major spatial effect,
unless the alchemist is in a room directly adjacent to the target.

Overdraw effect: the duration of the effect is increased, and the
caster's room is included in the effect.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 10% Wellspring Energy
