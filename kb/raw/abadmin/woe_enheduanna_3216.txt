Ability: Enheduanna (Record #3216)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 6999
Blurb: Weep for what was lost.
Syntax: RECITE ENHEDUANNA
Abfile:
The tragic tale of Enheduanna is one of sorrow and betrayal, love and
hatred, and terrible, terrible wrath. At the culmination of this tale
all in your location that hear you, ally or enemy, will be suffused with
a fragment of that terrible fury, all of their actions becoming
enhanced(*).

* Balance and equilibrium time is reduced while present in the room
where this tale was spoken for those that heard it, though not for long.
** This tale may only be spoken once every minute.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 16.00 seconds of voice balance
