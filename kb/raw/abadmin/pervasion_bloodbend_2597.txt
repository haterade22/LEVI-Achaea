Ability: Bloodbend (Record #2597)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: They shall dance on crimson strings.
Syntax: MANIFEST BLOODBEND <target> TO <command>
Abfile:
Reach out to the vital fluids that flow through anyone that stands
within your immediate area, commanding them to act(*). In order to carry
this out, they must be bleeding sufficiently for you to take advantage
of their vulnerability(**).

* This does not work against fellow Elemental Lords of Water.
** They must be bleeding for 250 health or more.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
