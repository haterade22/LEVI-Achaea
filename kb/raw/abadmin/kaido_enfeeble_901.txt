Ability: Enfeeble (Record #901)
Associated skill: Kaido
Skillpoints required: 56999
Blurb: Unleash a massive blast of kai energy at your opponent.
Syntax: KAI ENFEEBLE <target>
Abfile:
This ability will throw a massive shockwave of kai energy at your
opponent, cutting his current health and mana in half. If your opponent
does not lie prone before you, this will be reduced to a 25% reduction
in health and mana.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 61 Kai energy
