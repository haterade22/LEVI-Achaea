Ability: Thoth (Record #3226)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 43999
Blurb: Of Death alive but not.
Syntax: RECITE THOTH
Abfile:
There was a time, long after His demise, when Thoth once more walked and
fought with those who would see Woe brought low. However, that which has
gone beyond will soon fade once more, and this tale will make such
manifest to all(*).

* On the first tick with no necromancer present, gravehands are
destroyed. On the second tick gravehands are destroyed even if a
necromancer is present. On the third tick, infestation maggots are
destroyed.
** If a necromancer (Oppression users count as necromancers for the
purposes of this ability) is present, this recital is a channeled
action.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 18.00 seconds of voice balance
