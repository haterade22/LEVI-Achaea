Ability: Remotebuoy (Record #2820)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Activate a buoy remotely
Syntax: SHIP BUOY ACTIVATE <buoy>
SHIP CARGOBOX TRANSFER <box>
SHIP CARGOBOX DUMP <box>
Abfile:
For those who command enough sway over their ship, one may activate a
buoy from a ship other than the original owner of the buoy. This
requires captain permissions on the owning ship, and that the collecting
ship be adjacent. The cargo will be loaded on board into a crate, to be
transferred to the owning ship at a later date.

A ship may only carry one box of cargo at a time. If a ship's captain
finds themself in possession of a cargo box they no longer wish to
carry, they may dump it overboard, though this will be known to the
original owners of the cargo.

Requires legs: 0

Useable paralysed: No
