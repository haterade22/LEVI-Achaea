Ability: Combo (Record #841)
Associated skill: Tekura
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Combo attacks together.
Syntax: COMBO <target> <kick|stance> [limb] <punch1> [limb] <punch2> [limb] [JPK]
Abfile:
This ability allows you to perform tekura combos. You may choose to omit
or include any of the limb targets, but must always specify a full combo
(one kick, two punches). The combo can only be executed if you possess
balance on both arms and legs.

* If you append JPK to a combo, you'll attempt to jumpkick before the
rest of the combo is executed assuming that combo does not already
include a jumpkick attempt.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance
