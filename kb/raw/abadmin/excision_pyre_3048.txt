Ability: Pyre (Record #3048)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: Consign them to the flame.
Syntax: PERFORM PYRE <target>
Abfile:
Begin consigning a heretic to the flame, purging Creation of their
blight(*).

* Gives the pyre affliction. This makes it impossible to cure
ablaze/burning below the level of the pyre (so if you have level 3 pyre,
you cannot cure burning lower than level 3).
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of wrath balance
Resource: 5% Holy Wrath
