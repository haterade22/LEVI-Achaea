Ability: Martellato (Record #1635)
Associated skill: Swashbuckling
Skillpoints required: 38999
Blurb: Knock your opponent to the floor with this tunesmithing.
Syntax: TUNESMITH <your songblessed rapier> MARTELLATO
Abfile:
The power of your songblessed rapier will knock your opponent to the
floor on your next jab.

It will also pierce the rebounding defence if your target is already
prone. If this occurs, you will need to re-apply Martellato to your
rapier.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 50 mana
