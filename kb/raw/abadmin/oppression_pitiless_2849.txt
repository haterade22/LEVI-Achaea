Ability: Pitiless (Record #2849)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: Death is no barrier to His triumph.
Syntax: HELLFORGE CHANNEL PITILESS
Abfile:
When channeling the pitiless focus of the Lord through your hellforged
armour you shall strike out at all in your location with His vengeance
when you fall. This is similar to the necromantic method of doing so,
though with a single difference: any who are truly slain by your
vengeful blow shall immediately plunge into the Halls of the Finality.

This is an ongoing hellforge armour effect.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
