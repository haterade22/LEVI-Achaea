Ability: Exsanguinate (Record #2703)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 49999
Blurb: They'll never eat again.
Syntax: WEAVE EXSANGUINATE <target>
Abfile:
By driving your blade into the guts of your foe, you'll ravage their
internal organs, making them violently sick(*). If they're already
bleeding significantly then they'll also lose any desire (or ability) to
eat(**).

* Gives nausea.
** Gives anorexia.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
