Ability: Anguish (Record #2847)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 36999
Blurb: Pain is nothing before your will.
Syntax: HELLFORGE CHANNEL ANGUISH
Abfile:
Draw upon the fires of the Inferno, weaving them into your armour.
Though the searing heat will periodically cause you to suffer, if the
unthinkable happens and you fall in service to the Lord you will return
through the Halls of the Finality with greater swiftness, so as to
rejoin in His work with all haste.

Additionally, if you are revived by a starburst tattoo or similar, most
of your defences will be retained so that you may continue the battle
with all due haste.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
