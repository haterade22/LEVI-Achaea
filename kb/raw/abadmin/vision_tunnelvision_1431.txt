Ability: Tunnelvision (Record #1431)
Associated skill: Vision
Skillpoints required: 1000
Blurb: Focus your attention upon a single target.
Syntax: TUNNELVISION SHOW
TUNNELVISION ON [target]
TUNNELVISION OFF
TUNNELVISION ADD <option>
TUNNELVISION REMOVE <option>
Extra info: Options are: DRINKING, EATING, SMOKING, APPLYING, DEFENCES, SAYS, ENTRIES, EXITS
Abfile:
This ability allows you to disregard much of what those around you are doing. By focusing your target upon an adventurer (with SETTARGET), you will be able to ignore certain groups of actions performed by all other individuals in your location.

If you specify a person when enabling this ability, they will be considered your target for the purposes of tunnelvision when you do not have a target set.
Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
