Ability: Attrition (Record #2507)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 37999
Blurb: The death of a thousand strikes.
Syntax:
Abfile:
The Sentaari masters of old could strike someone with minimal force but
with major results. Indeed, many enemies that these grandmasters would
encounter would find themselves incapacitated without a single bone
broken or drop of blood spilled.

By appending LIGHT after a staff strike in a combination, that attack
shall not deal any limb damage, but the health damage and affliction
shall still be delivered (if applicable). Note that this only applies to
staff attacks, you may not deliver light kicks. An example: THRUST LIGHT
HEAD, NERVESTRIKE LIGHT, etc.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
