Ability: Sunrise (Record #3160)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 37999
Blurb: Down goes your partner.
Syntax: BLADE SUNRISE <target> <LEFT|RIGHT>
Abfile:
As the sun ascends to greet the dawn, so too shall your rapier rise to
meet your adversary. Delivered not as a traditional lateral or vertical
slash but as a diagonal blow to the hip and up into the torso, this
strike can be utterly devastating. If the torso and leg adjacent to the
hip you are aiming at are sufficiently damaged(*), both will be broken
as a result of this strike, and tonal trauma takes place upon both
limbs.

* Both limbs must be damaged enough that the next bladedance attack
would break them.
** See AB BLADEDANCE TONAL.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
