Ability: Rake (Record #3264)
Associated skill: Discipline
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: Command your falcon to strike with its talons.
Syntax: FALCON RAKE <target>
Abfile:
Command your falcon to fall upon a denizen opponent, raking them with
its talons and dealing damage(*). Note that you can only command your
loyal in this fashion every so often(**).

* This damage is based on your falcon's trained strength and whether it
is a warhawk (see AB DISCIPLINE WARHAWK).
** Every 30 seconds.

Useable on: denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Cooldown
