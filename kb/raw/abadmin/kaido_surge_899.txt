Ability: Surge (Record #899)
Associated skill: Kaido
Skillpoints required: 49999
Blurb: A blast of focussed power.
Syntax: KAI SURGE <target>
Abfile:
By directing a surge of pure kai energy into an enemy, you may set their
muscles to violently spasming. This will prevent them from being able to
remount, as well as slightly disrupting tumble attempts(*). This ability
also prones and causes some damage. The effect will fade over time.

* Each damaged limb currently extends the length of a tumble attempt;
the kai surge status will increase this penalty. Tumble with no damaged
limbs is unaltered.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.20 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 31 Kai energy
