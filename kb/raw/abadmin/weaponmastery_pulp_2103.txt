Ability: Pulp (Record #2103)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: End their life in a single strike.
Syntax: PULP <target>
Abfile:
You may pulp the brains of a suitably vulnerable victim, ending their
life in a single terrible strike.

For the purposes of this ability, suitably vulnerable requires they be
prone with either a mangled head -or- five or more skull fractures.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.10 seconds of balance
Skill specialisation: knight_dualblunt
