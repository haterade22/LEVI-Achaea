Ability: Kaitrance (Record #895)
Associated skill: Kaido
Skillpoints required: 37999
Blurb: Entering the mystical Kai Trance.
Syntax: KAI TRANCE
BREAK TRANCE
Abfile:
This is the basis for the most powerful Kaido abilities. It is the
ability which allows you to gather Kai energy to use in later abilities.
Everytime a person on your enemies list does something offensive in your
location, you will gain Kai energy from it. How much you gain depends on
how much energy the ability generates. While you are in a Kai Trance,
you will lose Kai energy relatively quickly, as well as losing some
mental energy. When you BREAK TRANCE, you will lose all your accumulated
Kai Energy.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Drain: Mana
