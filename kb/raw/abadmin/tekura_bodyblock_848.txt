Ability: Bodyblock (Record #848)
Associated skill: Tekura
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: Using your body to block blows.
Syntax: BDB
UNBLOCK BDB
UNBLOCK ALL
Abfile:
At the cost of a constant mana drain, this block attempts to stop or reduce physical damage aimed at your body.

With any block, you may UNBLOCK <block> to lower it. Alternatively, you may UNBLOCK ALL to lower all of your blocks.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 50 mana
Drain: Mana
