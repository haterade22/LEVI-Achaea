Ability: Deconstruct (Record #2705)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 57999
Blurb: They are unfit for Creation.
Syntax: WEAVE DECONSTRUCT <target>
Abfile:
If at least two of your target's mind, body and spirit are both
unravelled at least three times each, you may utterly deconstruct that
person, slaying them instantly.

This is immune to the effects of clumsiness.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
