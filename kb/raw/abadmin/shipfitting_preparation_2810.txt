Ability: Preparation (Record #2810)
Associated skill: Shipfitting
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Prepare commodities for maritime use.
Syntax: PREPARE [1-5] <commodity>
Abfile:
As a shipfitter, commodities that were suitable on land are no longer of
use to you. You will need to prepare your materials to withstand year
after year of exposure to the harsh conditions of a vessel at sea.
Material costs are as follows.

Shipwood:  50 wood and 10 iron
Shipiron:  50 iron and 10 steel
Shipcloth: 50 cloth
Shiplines: 50 rope and 10 iron
Pitch:     50 wood and 10 animal fat
Useable on: none

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
