Ability: Extinction (Record #2853)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: Burn to bone and blackened husk.
Syntax: IGNITE EXTINCTION <direction>
IGNITE EXTINCTION
Abfile:
If they come against you in numbers, let them perish in numbers. This
ability takes two forms:

The first can be directed at a single firewall, causing a small amount
of damage and setting ablaze all that are not of the primal fire. This
requires and consumes moderate spark.

The second utilises all firewalls in a location, causing more damage the
more walls you have to work with(*), and a great deal of concentration
during which you may perform no other actions. This requires and
consumes all of your primal spark.

* This caps at five firewalls.
Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 1000 willpower
