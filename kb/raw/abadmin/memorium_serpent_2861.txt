Ability: Serpent (Record #2861)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Of deadly fangs and poison.
Syntax: TRACE SERPENT <target>
Abfile:
The striking serpent shall sink its ethereal fangs into the neck of a
foe when traced, paralysing them with its power. In addition, this
logograph is unique in the fact that when traced in an epitaph following
the logograph of the scorpion, your enemy will also find that the voyria
plague has been introduced into their bloodstream(*).

* See AB MEMORIUM EPITAPH for details.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
