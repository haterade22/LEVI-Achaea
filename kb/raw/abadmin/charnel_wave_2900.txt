Ability: Wave (Record #2900)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 53999
Blurb: A deluge of a drier kind.
Syntax: ACCURSED WAVE <target> <direction>
Abfile:
When you stand upon sandy ground, you may command it to rise up and
drive a foe from your sight. Obstructions will prevent the movement(*),
and if the sand was brought forth by your Accursed work, it shall be
consumed(**).

* Blocking, walls, etc.
** Eternity is cleared if it is the basis for the environmental check in
this case. This ability could be used freely in desert areas or sandy
locations created through more obscure means.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.80 seconds of equilibrium
