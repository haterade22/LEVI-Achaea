Ability: Tidalwave (Record #2588)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: Upon raging waters shall you come.
Syntax: MANIFEST TIDALWAVE <direction>
Abfile:
Summon forth a tidal wave, carrying you away in a direction for several
rooms. This will carry you over walls, but not through closed doors. Any
firewall in the path of your wave shall be immediately snuffed at your
passing. If the room you begin in is flooded, you shall not be hindered
in your movement.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
