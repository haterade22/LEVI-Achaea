Ability: Tin (Record #6)
Associated skill: Alchemy
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: Tin will grant you a reflective barrier.
Syntax: EDUCE TIN
Extra info: Symbol: Two parallel line segments connected by diagonals.
Abfile:
The energies of tin will raise a reflective barrier around your body,
this will persist for the next two attacks, and will return a portion of
the damage you would receive to your attacker.

Note that this is ineffective versus unblockable damage.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 500 mana
