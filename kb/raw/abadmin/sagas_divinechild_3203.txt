Ability: Divinechild (Record #3203)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: To take the power for One's own.
Syntax: RECITE DIVINECHILD
Abfile:
A story of theft and of power, of taking and of subversion, the sagas of
the Divine Child will bring forth a shifting field of absorption in your
location. Periodically it will shift, and all damage to your allies of a
given type will become impossible until it shifts once more.

* The shifting cannot be controlled.
Useable on: room
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 16.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
