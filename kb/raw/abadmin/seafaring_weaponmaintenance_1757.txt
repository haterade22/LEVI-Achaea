Abfile:
Actively work to maintain the ship's onboard weapons. You will need to
have the required commodities in your inventory at the beginning of the
session, and complete eight MAINTAIN or REPAIRs to either add one month
to the life of the weapon or repair some of the damage the weapon has
taken.
