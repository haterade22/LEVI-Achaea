Ability: Poster (Record #1609)
Associated skill: InkMilling
Skillpoints required: 31999
Blurb: Posters that can be hung in rooms like graffiti.
Syntax: POSTER <poster> DYE <colour>
POSTER <poster> TITLE <title>
POSTER <poster> DESCRIBE
Abfile:
 After purchasing a blank poster you can -
   (Only by the inkmiller)
DYE: dye a white poster with an ink of the required colour.
TITLE: give your poster a short title.
DESCRIBE: enter the editor to describe what your poster looks like.
   (By anyone)
HANG POSTER to display your glorious artwork for all to see. Only one
poster may be hung in any given location.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
