Ability: Drench (Record #2585)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 14999
Blurb: Soak them to the bone.
Syntax: MANIFEST DRENCH <target>
Extra info: Flux effect: If your foe suffers from asthma, slickness, and anorexia, tendrils of your summoned water will slither into their mouth and nose, robbing them of focus.
Abfile:
Drench your foe with water, soaking him with icy water that will leech
away at them(*).

* Gives weariness if they are an adventurer or deals cold damage if they
are a denizen. If the target is already weary, they will become dazed by
the cold, receiving confusion.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
