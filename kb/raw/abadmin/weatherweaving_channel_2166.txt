Ability: Channel (Record #2166)
Associated skill: Weatherweaving
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Open conduits to the Elemental Realms.
Syntax: CHANNEL <AIR|WATER|EARTH>
CHANNELS
SEVER ALL|<channel>
Abfile:
Using this ability, you may open a channel to one of the three elemental
realms that you have access to. Keeping these channels open has a cost
in willpower. SEVER AIR/WATER/EARTH will close that channel. CHANNELS
will list what channels you have open.


Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 200 mana
