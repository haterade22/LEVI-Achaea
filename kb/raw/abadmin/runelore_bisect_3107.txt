Ability: Bisect (Record #3107)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: As relentless as the storm.
Syntax: BISECT <target> [venom]
Abfile:
While wielding an edged runeblade of some form with the hugalaz rune
sketched upon the blade, you may attempt to bisect your foe with a
single empowered strike(*).

* This is a multi damage typed ability that deals first lightning damage
then cutting damage.

** If your target is an adventurer and at 20% of their health or lower
when the cutting damage would be applied, they will be slain outright
instead.

*** Notably, this attack bypasses auras of rebounding and reflections
but leaves them intact.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
