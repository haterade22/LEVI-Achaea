Ability: Condemn (Record #3104)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: You are the judge, and you find them wanting.
Syntax: PERFORM CONDEMN <target>
Abfile:
Condemn an enemy of Creation to burn.

* A condemned target may not cure ablaze below one stack, but a paladin
may also no longer raise the burning level.
** Condemnation can take the place of the pyre requirement where the
Fervour ability is concerned in regards to its healing aspect. The bonus
to darkshade will not function under condemnation (see AB EXCISION
FERVOUR).
*** You may not hunt a heretic who is condemned (see AB EXCISION HUNT
for details).
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of wrath balance
Resource: 25% Holy Wrath
