Ability: Placement (Record #2782)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 4599
Blurb: Every step an artform.
Syntax: ALIGN PLACEMENT
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
This lowest of the alignment abilities is a relatively simple task in
theory, but rather difficult in practice. Considered ideal for novices
to the school, one who masters this process shall be able to place their
feet just so, and will not trigger the next trap that they would step
into upon movement.

Due to the function of this as a training ability, overdrawing power
from the Wellspring serves no purpose.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 1% Wellspring Energy
