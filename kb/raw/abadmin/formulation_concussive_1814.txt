Ability: Concussive (Record #1814)
Associated skill: Formulation
Skillpoints required: 45000
Blurb: A compound that reacts violently when exposed to the air.
Syntax: WIELD CONCUSSIVE
THROW CONCUSSIVE <AT GROUND|direction>
Extra info: Reagents: 2 magnesium, 2 argentum, 1 potash
Abfile:
This compound will cause a violent explosion. While it lacks much
damaging potential, it will cause a concussive wave, able to launch any
who do not have themselves suitably secured from the location.

When the alchemist is at the centre of the detonation, he or she is able
to be more accurate, allowing for the explosion to occasionally bypass
defences which would otherwise hold a subject in place.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of balance
