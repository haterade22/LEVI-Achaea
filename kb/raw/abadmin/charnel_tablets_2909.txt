Abfile:
The ancient rituals of Charnel are scribed in tablets of stone. These
can be acquired in various ways, but one thing is universal: they may be
used but once and they will decay very rapidly when held outside your
crypt's Arcane Record. When you gain a tablet, you will gain access to
the ability associated to that ritual, and may learn how to utilise said
ritual.
