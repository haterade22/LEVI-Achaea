Ability: Continuation (Record #2061)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 3000
Blurb: Shrug off afflictions like the trivialities they are.
Syntax: BATTLEFURY CONTINUATION
Abfile:
You may use this ability to shrug off the effects of paralysis. There is
a fairly substantial delay before you may utilise this ability for a
second time.

Your body takes time to recover from this ability, removing your
capability to utilise fitness for a short time following it.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 7.00 seconds of battlefury balance
Skill specialisation: knight_twohanded
