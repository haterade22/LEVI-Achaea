Ability: Bloodroot (Record #2198)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: One of the most widely used herbs.
Syntax: PROPAGATE <HEAD/BODY/ARMS/LEGS> WITH BLOODROOT
Abfile:
When propagated, this plant conveys the following bonuses:

Head: You will regenerate endurance faster.
Body: You will regenerate health faster.
Arms: Your thornrend attack will deal increased damage to paralysed
adventurers or hindered denizens(*).
Legs: Move more quickly through caves and other such locations.

* Valid denizens battlerage afflictions are: clumsiness, amnesia, charm,
and stun.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
