Ability: Grave (Record #3138)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: The fate of all.
Syntax: SOULBLEED GRAVE
Abfile:
If you stand within a location that has fallen under the Underworld's
influence, you may channel the inevitability of the grave against all
who stand in the area(*). No matter how fast they flee and how far they
run, if you complete this fell ritual they will die(**).

* This skill will mark all those in your area for death and begin a 60
second channel where you cannot perform any actions.
** If this channel completes and you have not died or been interrupted,
all those marked for death will die no matter where they happen to be.
*** This ability has a long cooldown for both the caster and on the
area, and consumes one of your lives to use.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 2
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 10.00 seconds of balance
