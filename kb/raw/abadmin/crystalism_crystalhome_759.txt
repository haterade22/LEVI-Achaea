Ability: Crystalhome (Record #759)
Associated skill: Crystalism
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Transport yourself back to the Master Crystal.
Syntax: CRYSTALHOME
Extra info: A 4.00 second delay
Abfile:
This ability will allow you to return automatically to your Master
Crystal, with a short delay.
Note:
   - You must belong to a House with a Master Crystal and be at
     least of House Rank 3
   - Targossas and Eleusis may access their Master Crystal at
     City Rank 2

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
