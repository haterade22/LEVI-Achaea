Ability: Extrusion (Record #2526)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: From the threshold of defeat.
Syntax: TERRAN EXTRUSION
Abfile:
While this ability is active, if your health falls to critical
conditions the magma that makes up your superior form shall attempt to
reinforce injured areas, significantly restoring your health. Note that
once this happens the ability will be deactivated and it will be some
time before you are able to reactivate it.

Additionally, you must be at full health to activate this ability
initially.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
