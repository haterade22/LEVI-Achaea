Ability: Decompound (Record #74)
Associated skill: Alchemy
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: A way to devolve Talisman Pieces into pure gold.
Syntax: DECOMPOUND <number> <Talisman piece name> <piece level>
Extra info: You must be in an equipped metallurgic lab.
Abfile:
This ability allows the skilled alchemist to decompose talisman pieces
into gold. The amount of gold produced is dependent on the rarity and
level of the piece being decomposed.

* Note that only pieces sourced from bashing can be decompounded.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
