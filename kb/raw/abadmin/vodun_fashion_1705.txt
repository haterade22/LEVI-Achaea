Ability: Fashion (Record #1705)
Associated skill: Vodun
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Fashion vodun dolls from rope and cloth.
Syntax: FASHION DOLL OF <target>
VODUN UNRAVEL <target>
Extra info: Fashion cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance, 50 mana
Unravel cooldown: 10.00 seconds of balance, 750 mana
Abfile:
 Requires: 1 Rope, 1 Cloth

After the first fashioning to create the doll, you will have a crude
humanoid vodun doll. Wield the doll and FASHION DOLL OF <target>.

As you fashion your doll further, you'll notice that it gets closer to
being a semi-living state. Further, the more fashions you have with your
doll, the more Vodun powers you'll be able to channel through the doll.

Additionally, your doll will decay at a rate of 50% every 6 minutes
after the last fashion. This effect does not occur for dolls of allied
adventurers.

If you find yourself in need of destroying a doll that is no longer in
your possession, you may unravel it from afar. Note that this is a
difficult feat, and will result in being thrown off balance for a long
period of time.

Useable on: adventurers

Requires legs: 0
