Ability: Pantheon (Record #3231)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 57999
Blurb: Divine wrath unleashed.
Syntax: RECITE PANTHEON
Abfile:
In all the long histories of the world have the Pantheon rarely marched
to war united, and the resolve must be great indeed for such a dire
event to transpire. Speak this tale of battle met and all who share a
cause with you(*) will share in a fragment of that resolve, becoming
numb to the ravages of war(**). This tale is a difficult one to tell,
and you may only do so infrequently(***).

* All Cyrenians in your location.
** All affected will gain the numbness defence for twenty five seconds.
*** This tale can only be recited once every half hour.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 19.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
