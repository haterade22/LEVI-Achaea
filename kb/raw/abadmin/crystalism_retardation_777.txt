The retardation vibration slows time in the location, giving the aeon
effect to everyone in the room, including you. Unfortunately, due to its
time-bending effects, this vibration will disable any crystalline
vibrations in the room with it that offer passive effects, and affect
any weapons or projectiles thrown from outside of the vibration's
effect.
