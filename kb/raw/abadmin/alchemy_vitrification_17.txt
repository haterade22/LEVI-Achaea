Ability: Vitrification (Record #17)
Associated skill: Alchemy
Skillpoints required: 43332
Blurb: Turn an opponent's skin into glass.
Syntax: VITRIFY <target>
Abfile:
This ability will cause an enemy's skin to partially transmutate into glass for a time. Abilities that use balance will cause bleeding, with the amount scaling upward with the length of balance loss.

This effect is mutually exclusive with that of phlogistication.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 400 mana
