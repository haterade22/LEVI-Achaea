Ability: Kaichoke (Record #896)
Associated skill: Kaido
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: Crush a victim's windpipe remotely.
Syntax: KAI CHOKE <target>
Abfile:
By manipulating the Kai energy that flows around us all, you are able to
choke the life out of any adventurer in your line of sight, dealing
increased damage to those suffering under certain afflictions(*).

If instead you turn your focus onto a denizen, this skill becomes much
simpler to perform but can only be used on those foes in the same
location as you(**).

* This deals increased damage to targets that have one of: a
damaged/mangled head, a damaged/mangled torso, or are mind clamped.
** This skill requires and consumes no kai energy when used against a
denizen but requires the target to be in the same location as the monk
in these cases.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 10 Kai energy and 50 mana
