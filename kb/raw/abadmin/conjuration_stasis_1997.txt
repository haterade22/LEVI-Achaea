The bag of stasis is a magical item created to slow the decay of things
inside of it. Any five items will fit inside, and those inside will
decay at half the normal rate. Items inside something such as a pack
will decay at the normal rate but the pack itself would decay at half
the rate when inside the bag of stasis.
