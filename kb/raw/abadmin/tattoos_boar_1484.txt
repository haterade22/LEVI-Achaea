Ability: Boar (Record #1484)
Associated skill: Tattoos
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Feel the noble blood of the Black Boar beat in your veins.

Extra info: Inks: 1 Purple ink
2 Red inks
Abfile:
The Black Boar, along with the Snow Owl, is one of the two symbols of
the Logos Himself. A noble, proud, and strong creature, the boar tattoo
will automatically heal your health when you are hurt. Each time it
works, there is a slight chance of it fading. Using this tattoo will
take balance, but once touched, it will work automatically from then on.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
