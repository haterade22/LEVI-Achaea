Ability: Pilgrimage (Record #1049)
Associated skill: Devotion
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: Travel to someone in a Rite of Pilgrimage.
Syntax: PERFORM RITE OF PILGRIMAGE
PERFORM PILGRIMAGE <target>
Extra info: Rite cooldown: 5.00 seconds of equilibrium and 300 devotion.
Travel cooldown: 3.50 seconds of equilibrium and 450 devotion.
Abfile:
By performing the rite of pilgrimage, you are able to then pilgrimage to
anyone standing in that rite.

You may also perform a pilgrimage to any mutual ally who bears the
preach blessing, stripping their blessing in the process.
Useable on: adventurers

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
