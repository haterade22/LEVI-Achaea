Ability: Istria (Record #992)
Associated skill: Domination
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Istria, the pathfinder.
Syntax: ORDER PATHFINDER HOME
Abfile:
When called upon, Istria, known as the Pathfinder, will send to you a pathfinder entity. Wherever you summon the pathfinder entity will be home to it. Later, you may simply ORDER PATHFINDER HOME to be taken back to where the pathfinder was summoned from.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
The ability name PATHFINDER is mapped to this ability.
