Ability: Judgement (Record #1023)
Associated skill: Spirituality
Skillpoints required: 16999
Blurb: Pass a sentence of death upon the evil.
Syntax: JUDGE <target>
Abfile:
Through supplication and prayer, you may seek judgement that an
adventurer has committed a crime punishable by death, and await his
sentence.

This ability takes some time to work, though less if the heretic
possesses the justice affliction at the time you begin.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of equilibrium
