Ability: Seleucar (Record #3218)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 13999
Blurb: Of darkness roused from maddened depths.
Syntax: RECITE SELEUCAR
Abfile:
The might of once great Seleucar was a thing that broke the first Black
Wave, or at least such was the great design. By speaking this
foretelling, you and all allies that hear the grand culmination shall be
suffused with a portion of that might temporarily, gaining a bonus when
fighting particularly mighty foes(**).

* All allies and Cyrenians in your location.
** While enhanced in this way your attacks will do more damage against
denizens who cannot be struck with critical strikes.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 16.00 seconds of voice balance and 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
