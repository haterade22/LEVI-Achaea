Blurb: Slowly but surely.

Abfile:
Once you have begun reclaiming a location, you may expand on your
network by performing this ability adjacent to a room you have already
reclaimed. The restrictions are similar to reclaiming a room with the
reclaim ability, although you may reclaim as many rooms in this way as
you are able.
