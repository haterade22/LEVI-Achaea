Ability: Knees (Record #264)
Associated skill: Striking
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: Send your target sprawling to the ground.
Syntax: STRIKE <target> KNEES
Abfile:
Manipulating your opponent's knee joint with a well-placed blade hand
will reliably send them falling to the ground. If they are elevated by
sitting upon a steed or similar, you'll instead be able to get a grip
upon them and yank them from their mount.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.25 seconds of balance
