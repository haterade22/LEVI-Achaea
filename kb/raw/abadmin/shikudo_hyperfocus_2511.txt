Ability: Hyperfocus (Record #2511)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: A single opportunity is enough.
Syntax: HYPERFOCUS <limb|NONE>
Abfile:
By hyperfocussing upon a specific limb, you will be able to identify
gaps in your target's defence, avoiding all attempts to parry strikes
against that limb. However, focus of this nature comes with a cost:
though you will be able to bypass parry attempts, your strike on the
hyperfocussed limb shall only deal half as much damage to that limb as
normal.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
