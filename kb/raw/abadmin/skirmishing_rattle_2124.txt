Ability: Rattle (Record #2124)
Associated skill: Skirmishing
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: Rattle their brains with a blow to the head.
Syntax: RATTLE <target>
Abfile:
This ability will knock a prone and transfixed target unconscious. The
more health your victim is missing, the longer they shall remain
unconscious for. This duration is also increased if a rattle follows
within ten seconds of a skullbash, their brains being sufficiently
scrambled already.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
