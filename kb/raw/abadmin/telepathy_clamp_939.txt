Ability: Clamp (Record #939)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Set a telepathic mind clamp about your subject.
Syntax: MIND CLAMP
MIND UNCLAMP
Extra info: MIND UNCLAMP cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium.
Abfile:
This allows you to reach out with your mind and clamp down on the locked adventurer's mind, reducing his maximum health and mana levels by twenty percent. MIND UNCLAMP will allow you to release this hold on your opponent's mind, returning their health and mana levels to normal.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 250 mana
Drain: Mana
