Ability: Visions (Record #1053)
Associated skill: Devotion
Skillpoints required: 14999
Blurb: Condemn your foe with horrific visions.
Syntax: PERFORM VISIONS <target>
Abfile:
Condemn your foe as an enemy of creation, striking them down to never
know peace. First, they will suffer the dementia affliction, while
subsequent visions will confer shyness if the target is already
demented.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 100 devotion and 50 mana
