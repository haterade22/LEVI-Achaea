Ability: Shalestorm (Record #3142)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: A downpour of stone.
Syntax: CAST SHALESTORM AT <target>
Abfile:
This higher order earth spell will summon up a storm of rocks to
periodically pummel your victim(*). For every two times the rocks pummel
a victim, your resonance with the Elemental Plane of Earth will
decrease. The storm will cease once your resonance is gone entirely.

* This will do damage and break a random limb of your victim if your
victim is unshielded. If they are shielded, it will break the shield.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.40 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 100 mana
