Ability: Thornrend (Record #733)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 499
Blurb: Rend your foes with your lethal vines.
Syntax: THORNREND <target> [venom] [limb] [plant to propagate arms with]
Abfile:
You may now lash the flesh of your foes with your thorny vines, doing
damage.

If you have learned the TARGETING ability in Weaponry, you may
optionally direct your thornrend at a specific body part, causing
additional damage to an arm, leg, the head, or the torso of your
opponent.

If you have gained the ENVENOM ability in Weaponry then you may envenom
your arms, which will deliver a venom to your target when you thornrend.
Otherwise, you may SECRETE SAP to coat your arms in a single
application.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 80 mana
