Ability: Traps (Record #1173)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 699
Blurb: The ability to search for traps in your location.
Syntax: TRAPS
TRAPS LIST
Extra info: Traps cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Traps List cooldown: 5.00 seconds of balance
Abfile:
The TRAPS ability will search for traps in the room, giving you information about them. You will not be able to find traps laid by someone with higher skill than you. TRAPS LIST will show you all your traps around the world.
