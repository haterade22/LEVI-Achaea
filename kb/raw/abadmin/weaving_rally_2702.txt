Ability: Rally (Record #2702)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 45999
Blurb: Let them know your fervour.
Syntax: WEAVE RALLY [INDOMITABLE]
Abfile:
Weave power into your very voice, enabling you to shout a rallying
cry(*), healing yourself for a large amount of health(**). You can only
do this every so often, as it is somewhat taxing.

* This uses your warcry if you have one set.
** This can push your health above your maximum.
*** If you have mastered Emulation, you may also invoke your
indomitability as an optional addition to your rallying cry.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
