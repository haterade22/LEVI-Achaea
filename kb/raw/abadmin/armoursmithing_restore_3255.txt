Ability: Restore (Record #3255)
Associated skill: Armoursmithing
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Repairing damaged gear.
Syntax: GEAR RESTORE <gear>
Abfile:
At a cost of arcane ephemera, you may restore a piece of gear to an
undamaged state, clearing any decay that has taken place over time. You
may do this for gear you personally own or for others by using the GEAR
TRADE interface.

* A restore costs one alteration, and a given piece of gear may only be
altered up to three times.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.50 seconds of balance
