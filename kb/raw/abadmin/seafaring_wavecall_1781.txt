Ability: Wavecall (Record #1781)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: A seaspell to summon an immense wave for instant movement.
Syntax: INVOKE WAVECALL <direction> <distance between 1-8>
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
A seaspell to summon a wave to propel your ship a great distance,
instantaneously, at a great cost in personal endurance, hull damage, as
well as figurehead power. All costs increase with greater distance.

Personnel on other ships nearby with SHIPWARNING active will be
informed of the direction in which a ship wavecalls away.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
