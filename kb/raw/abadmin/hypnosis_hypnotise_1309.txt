Ability: Hypnotise (Record #1309)
Associated skill: Hypnosis
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Their minds are yours.
Syntax: HYPNOTISE <target>
SUGGEST <target> <suggestion>
SEAL <target> <delay in seconds>
SNAP <target>
Abfile:
This ability is the basis for all hypnotic manipulation. First you must
hypnotise a victim, to seize control of his or her mind. This can take
several seconds.

Once a trance is secured, you may begin implanting hypnotic suggestions,
and finally seal the victim's trance. Once this is complete, you may
snap your victim to trigger their trance, and the hypnotic suggestions
shall begin to fire in rapid succession.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
