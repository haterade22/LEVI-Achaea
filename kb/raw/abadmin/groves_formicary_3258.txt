Ability: Formicary (Record #3258)
Associated skill: Groves
Skillpoints required: 35000
Blurb: Summon the swarm to defend a natural location.
Syntax: EVOKE FORMICARY CREATE
EVOKE FORMICARY RECLAIM
EVOKE FORMICARY EMPOWER
EVOKE FORMICARY TUNE ELEUSIS|FOREST
EVOKE FORMICARY INFECT MURRAIN|FETTER
Abfile:
Formicaries are used to spread the influence of the swarm throughout
Eleusis and beyond. Each formicary holds a small portion of the swarm,
empowered by the raw energy of Nature itself, to defend the land from
the enemies of Nature. Almost all formicary skills will consume a
portion of the swarm, and all empowered formicaries will consume a small
portion of the swarm each month.

CREATE - At a cost of 20 fibre, 10 wood, and 2 bone commodities, create
a new formicary to house the swarm.

RECLAIM - Destroy a formicary.

EMPOWER - Call upon the raw power of Nature to empower a formicary. The
empowerment will fade over time, and most of the formicary's powers will
not function until it has been re-empowered.

TUNE - Tune a formicary to react against the enemies of either Eleusis
or Forest enemies.

INFECT - Infect the swarm housed in a formicary with either murrain or
fetter. Murrain will cause the swarm to infect enemies with painful
afflictions. Fetter will cause the swarm to hinder enemy movement.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Skill specialisation: forestal_org
