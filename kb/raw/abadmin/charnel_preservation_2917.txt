Ability: Preservation (Record #2917)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Sleep now, oh sleep.
Syntax: ACCURSED PERFORM THE RITUAL OF PRESERVATION
Abfile:
The ritual of Preservation is one performed upon the self, and grants
the power to preserve and consign. All those that you deliver the
killing stroke to shall have their bodies preserved(*), and their soul
immediately consigned into the tender clutches of the Finality(**). This
ritual lasts for around half an Achaean day.

* The corpse will look different.
** They will be immediately forced to embrace death upon true death.

Useable paralysed: No
