Ability: Knuckleslap (Record #1624)
Associated skill: Swashbuckling
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: Numb an opponent's hand temporarily.
Syntax: KNUCKLESLAP|KS <target> LEFT|RIGHT
Abfile:
Numb your opponent's hand, either left or right, for a few seconds, making it unusable. There is currently no cure or way to avoid a knuckleslap. Attempts to use a numbed hand will have unpleasant results.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
