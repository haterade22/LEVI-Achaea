Ability: Girding (Record #2818)
Associated skill: Shipfitting
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Disguise your ship from seamonsters.
Syntax: SHIPFIT GIRD HULL
Abfile:
The dragons of the deep are no laughing matter, and their putrid stench
carries for miles. One knowledgeable enough in the ways of shipfitting
can use their scales to disguise a ship, such that wandering seamonsters
will not rise from the deeps to investigate their passing. Doing so will
require two hundred handfuls of scales.

This is not permanent, and after a time the stench will fade.

Note well: This will not prevent an already-present seamonster from
attacking your ship, it will only prevent new seamonsters from rising to
the surface.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
