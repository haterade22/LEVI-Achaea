Ability: Pinshot (Record #1248)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 32999
Blurb: Focus your fire upon the feet of your victim.
Syntax: PINSHOT <target>
Abfile:
This powerful ability will focus your fire upon the feet of your victim,
making it difficult for them to flee from you. In addition, if your
loyal snake is present when the arrow lands, they will strike out
immediately at your unlucky foe.

If you evade from a room, all of your pinshots will be yanked loose of
your hapless victims.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
