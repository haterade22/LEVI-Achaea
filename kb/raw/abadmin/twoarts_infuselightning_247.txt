Ability: Infuselightning (Record #247)
Associated skill: TwoArts
Skillpoints required: 24999
Blurb: Command lightning into your sword.
Syntax: INFUSE LIGHTNING
Abfile:
By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may charge
your blade with volatile electrical currents. A weapon so empowered will
strike as lightning the next time it is drawn, and shall cause your
foe's muscles to lock up in clumsiness.
** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
Useable on: none

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 5 Shin energy
