Ability: Retribution (Record #3046)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 30999
Blurb: They will be avenged.
Syntax: PERFORM RETRIBUTION
Abfile:
By swearing to avenge any of your comrades(*) that fall in battle, any
time one of them is slain by an enemy of Creation(**), you will
immediately recover balance. In addition to this, your own arc attempts
will strike members of the faithful for less damage than it normally
would.

* Any member of Targossas or one of the Bloodsworn's orders.
** Anyone not from the above groups.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.40 seconds of equilibrium
