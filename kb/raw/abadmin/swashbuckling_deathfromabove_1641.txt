Ability: Deathfromabove (Record #1641)
Associated skill: Swashbuckling
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: The name says it all. Death, from above!
Syntax: DEATHFROMABOVE <target>
Abfile:
Leap down upon your unsuspecting enemy who is knocked to the ground and
caused to black out momentarily by your brilliant attack. You must be in
the trees, a ship's rigging or leaping into the air to begin with, and
your target must be on the ground below.

If your target is already prone this will deal increased damage.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
