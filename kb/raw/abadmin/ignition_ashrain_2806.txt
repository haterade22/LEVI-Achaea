Ability: Ashrain (Record #2806)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: To blot out the sky.
Syntax: MANIFEST ASHRAIN
ASHSTEP <target>
Abfile:
You may bring forth an apocalyptic rain of ash, covering an entire area
in your power. In such a location touched by Kkractle you may
immediately step to anyone who also stands within said fall of ash who
is outdoors. This bypasses monolith sigils, though none may follow you
through your ash-forged path.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
