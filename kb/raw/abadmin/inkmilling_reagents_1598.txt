Ability: Reagents (Record #1598)
Associated skill: InkMilling
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Knowledge of the common ingredients.
Syntax: None
Abfile:
Inkmilling reagents are broken up into eight different types as follows:

Red Reagents
------------
a lump of red clay
a sliver of red scorpion chitin

Blue Reagents
-------------
an ink bladder
a patch of lumic moss

Yellow Reagents
---------------
a sliver of yellow scorpion chitin

Gold Reagents
-------------
some flakes of gold

Common Reagents
---------------
some fish scales

Uncommon Reagents
-----------------
a piece of buffalo horn

Scarce Reagents
---------------
a shark tooth

Rare Reagents
-------------
a wyrm tongue

For categories that have more than one reagent listed, you may use either of the items in your milling.
