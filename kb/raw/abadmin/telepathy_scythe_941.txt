Ability: Scythe (Record #941)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 54999
Blurb: Cleave their psyche asunder.
Syntax: MIND SCYTHE
Abfile:
This formidable ability allows the skilled telepath to utterly eradicate
the psyche of their unfortunate victim. For this to work the subject
must suffer from a mangled head and at least two of: stupidity,
impatience, dizziness, epilepsy, or confusion.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 mana
