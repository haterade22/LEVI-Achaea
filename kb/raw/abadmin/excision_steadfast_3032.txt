Ability: Steadfast (Record #3032)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 399
Blurb: You will stand unmoved.
Syntax: BLOCK <direction> [STEADFAST]
Abfile:
Though your fellows from the other schools of weaponmastery may block
directions, none of them have quite the steadfast vigour you possess. By
blocking a direction in a steadfast way, you will be much more
formidable in this aspect(*).

* Functionally, blocking a direction with the steadfast argument
appended will cause you to serve as two people blocking said direction,
instead of just one.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 2
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
