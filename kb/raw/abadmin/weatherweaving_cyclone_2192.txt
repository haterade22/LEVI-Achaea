Ability: Cyclone (Record #2192)
Associated skill: Weatherweaving
Skillpoints required: 31999
Blurb: The greatest of the wind spells.
Syntax: CAST CYCLONE AT <target>
Abfile:
This powerful wind spell will cause dizziness when used against an
adventurer. If the subject is already dizzy, it will give impatience and
cause them to suffer damage.

Against denizens, this spell will deal blunt damage.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 40 mana
