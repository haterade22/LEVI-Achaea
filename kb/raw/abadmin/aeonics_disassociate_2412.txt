Ability: Disassociate (Record #2412)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: Gain now is pain later.
Syntax: CHRONO DISASSOCIATE [BOOST]
Abfile:
This ability shall allow you to become numb to all damage taken for nine
seconds. However, when the nine seconds end, you shall take the total
damage you would have suffered, and more.

If boosted, the disassociation shall only last for five seconds.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 400 age
