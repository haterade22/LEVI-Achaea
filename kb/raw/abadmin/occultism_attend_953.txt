Ability: Attend (Record #953)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: Force your students to pay attention.
Syntax: ATTEND <target>
Abfile:
Using the power of your will, you can exert your aura and force your
students to attend to their studies, curing them of deafness and
blindness.

Alternatively, when used against a denizen, you can lash their minds
with karmic energy, dealing psychic damage to any who have enough
vitality to pay attention(*).

* This skill only deals damage to denizens at 100% health or higher.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 125 mana and 20 willpower
