Ability: Fissure (Record #2863)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: That which sunders.
Syntax: TRACE FISSURE <target>
Abfile:
One of the crudest of logographs, the fissure possesses two properties
of interest. The former is its ability to sunder the magical shields
protecting your enemies; the second is that it does not chain from or to
anything. Notably however, it also does not interrupt the chaining of
other logographs, for reasons the ancient sorcerers were never able to
uncover.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.80 seconds of balance
