Ability: Fissure (Record #2537)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 36999
Blurb: Down into the darkness.
Syntax: MANIFEST FISSURE <target>
Abfile:
When an opponent is suitably unable to move(*), you may form a fissure
beneath them, dropping them down into the ground(**).

* The target must be prone with at least one broken leg for this to
work.

** This ability may only be performed in burrowable environments, and
your target will be forceably pushed back to the surface after a time
(providing they do not move themselves).
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
