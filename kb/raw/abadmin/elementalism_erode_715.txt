Ability: Erode (Record #715)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 13999
Blurb: Destroy the defences of your opponent.
Syntax: CAST ERODE AT <target> [defence] [MAINTAIN]
Abfile:
With the destructive fury of all the elements at your command, you may
destroy a defence on an opponent. This spell will strip the first
defence it finds from the below list, and if none are present
strip any random defence. You may also specify one of the listed
defences and target that one in particular:
Shield, chargeshield, insulation, and temperance.

* Note that this spell is special in that while instead of increasing
resonance, when you successfully strip a defence all resonance you
possess is decreased by one level unless you specify the MAINTAIN
argument.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 75 mana
