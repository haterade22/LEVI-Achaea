Ability: Backstab (Record #1250)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: Attack from the shadows.
Syntax: BACKSTAB <target> [<venom>]
Extra info: Initiation cooldown: 2.00 seconds.
Abfile:
Only useable while hidden in the shadows, backstab is a delayed ability. After initiating it, you will spend a few seconds circling around to get directly behind your opponent, and then you will leap from the shadows to plant a dirk or dagger in his back.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
