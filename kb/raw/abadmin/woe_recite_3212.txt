It is said that the weight of a life's sin is commensurate with their
evil. If such is the case, then Black Pazuzu's evil is a burden to be
borne by a City and its Peoples from now unto distant futures unknown.

And so when you recite the tales of Black Pazuzu, Prince of Woe and
self-proclaimed New God, it is the weight of Remembrance that you will
bear: both ancient transference and sacred charge. This is his story,
but you are not afeared.
