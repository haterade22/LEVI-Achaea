Ability: Percussia (Record #1659)
Associated skill: Harmonics
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Create a sonic explosion.
Syntax: PLAY PERCUSSIA [delay]
Abfile:
Creates a sonic-boom of sorts that stuns those present and may
knock them off their feet. Can be delayed in execution for 'delay'
seconds. If 'delay' is omitted, then the Percussia will occur in
approximately five seconds. Example: PLAY PERCUSSIA 9 - would play the
Percussia and then wait for nine more seconds before exploding on your
(hopefully) unsuspecting targets. This is not a true harmonic, in that
it cannot be called or moved with the Andante or the Battaglia.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 250 mana
