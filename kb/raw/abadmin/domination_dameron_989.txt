Ability: Dameron (Record #989)
Associated skill: Domination
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: Jy'Barrak Dameron, the Hand
Syntax: COMMAND MINION AT <target>
Abfile:
Jy'Barrak Dameron is the right hand of the infernal throne. The minion
he shall send to aid you can be commanded to unravel the very mind of
your target, slaying them instantly(*).

The minions of Dameron gain no benefit from being primebonded, though
when one is present it will protect all of your other entities from
being banished from the prime.

* They must be enlightened, as with unravel via the occultism skill.
Useable on: none

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
