Ability: Smudge (Record #1132)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Ability to destroy runes.
Syntax: SMUDGE <totem>
SMUDGE <totem> SLOT <1-6>
SMUDGE GROUND [rune]
SMUDGE <rune> OFF ME
SMUDGE <weapon> <rune>
SMUDGE LEFT/RIGHT <rune>
SMUDGE CIRCLE SURROUNDING TOTEM
Abfile:
With this ability you may remove (smudge) a rune from yourself, the ground or from a totem, assuming that your skill in Runelore equals or exceeds that of the one who originally sketched the rune. Smudging a totem or slot on a totem for any totem not owned by you will take longer, and may potentially notify the totem owner or owning organisation of the attempt.
Useable on: room and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
