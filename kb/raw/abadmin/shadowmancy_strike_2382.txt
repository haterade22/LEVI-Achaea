Ability: Strike (Record #2382)
Associated skill: Shadowmancy
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: You shall not suffer obstructions.
Syntax: SHADOW STRIKE <target> [KNEEL]
Abfile:
Command your shadow to rend asunder any magical shields that your
opponent may have raised as protection.

If they lack a shield and you append KNEEL to this command, their own
shadow shall turn against them, knocking them to the ground at your
feet. (This is faster if the target is afflicted with timeloop.)

You must be wielding a manifested dagger to carry out this ability.

In addition, this ability will drive your target closer to the next
affliction from an attuned shadow.

* Note that if your target is knocked prone this ability incurs a longer
balance recovery.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
