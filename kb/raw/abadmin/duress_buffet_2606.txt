Ability: Buffet (Record #2606)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 399
Blurb: A crude but effective technique.
Syntax: MANIFEST BUFFET <target>
Abfile:
Hammer at a target with solidified air, beating them into
unconsciousness and death(*).

If used in succession against the same target, this ability will
relapse, causing additional damage after two seconds.
* Deals damage based on how much pressure you have built around a target
as well as for each affliction from: disloyalty, tension, manaleech, and
deadening present.


Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
