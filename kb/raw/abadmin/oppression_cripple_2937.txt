Ability: Cripple (Record #2937)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: Break the body, forge the spirit.
Syntax: CRIPPLE <target> <limb1> <limb2>

Sometimes a whelp will require you to get a little more hands on when it
comes to discipline. Take them up by two of their limbs and show them
how generous you were previously to allow them the use of said limbs.

* Breaks both limbs and deals some minor damage.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.10 seconds of balance
