Ability: Risingkick (Record #2504)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: A high kick targeting the head or torso.
Syntax: RISINGKICK <HEAD|TORSO>
Abfile:
This high kick can be aimed at the head or torso. If aimed at the head
of a prone opponent, it shall stun them for a short time. Likewise, if
aimed at the torso of someone suffering internal trauma, it shall also
stun them.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
