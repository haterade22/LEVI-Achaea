Ability: Channel (Record #439)
Associated skill: Groves
Skillpoints required: 21999
Blurb: Transfer energy to the grove of another.
Syntax: GROVE CHANNEL <target>
GROVE CHANNEL STOP
Abfile:
This ability will allow you to transfer sunlight energy from one grove to another. Both you and the target adventurer must be standing in your respective groves. This can be a useful ability though it should be noted some energy is lost maintaining the channel.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: Sunlight
Drain: Sunlight
