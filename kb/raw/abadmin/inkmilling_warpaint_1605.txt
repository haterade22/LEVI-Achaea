Ability: Warpaint (Record #1605)
Associated skill: InkMilling
Skillpoints required: 14999
Blurb: Create warpaints for people to wear.
Syntax: MIX <colour> INTO <vial>
APPLY <warpaint> [style/extra]
WIPE OFF PAINT
Abfile:
Mix two inks of the specified colour into a vial of water to create a
vial of colour warpaint that can be applied to a person's face.

Style can be one of swirling, checkered, tribal, or half-faced.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
