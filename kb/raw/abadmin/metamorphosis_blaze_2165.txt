Ability: Blaze (Record #2165)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Engulf yourself in flame.
Syntax: BLAZE
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
This formidable power of the wyvern allows you to coat yourself in fire,
at the cost of some of your inner flame. While empowered, your mauls
will drive any they strike to the ground.

Additionally, while you are ablaze in this manner, your prey will find
it impossible to parry your mauls or frenzies so long as they are also
afflicted with the darkshade affliction.

Useable on: self

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
