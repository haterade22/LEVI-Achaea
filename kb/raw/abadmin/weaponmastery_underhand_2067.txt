Ability: Underhand (Record #2067)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 15000
Blurb: Deliver a devastating rising stroke.
Syntax: UNDERHAND <target>
Abfile:
This raising strike aimed at the torso aims to crack the ribs of your
opponent. The more cracked ribs they suffer from, the less healing
elixirs or tonics shall restore them for.
Additionally, while afflicted with cracked ribs, the target shall
periodically receive sensitivity. This periodic afflicting becomes
faster based on how many fractures are present.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance cost varies based on weapon speed
Skill specialisation: knight_twohanded
