Ability: Detect (Record #2877)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: That which may fall under your sway.
Syntax: SWARM DETECT
Abfile:
The intrepid sorcerer will require a swarm to do their bidding, and in
the early days a more hands-on process is required. While wandering the
land you may detect what insect life is nearby that you may enthrall;
different insects may be found in different locations, and some
locations will be more bountiful than others.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
