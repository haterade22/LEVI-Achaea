Ability: Bop (Record #661)
Associated skill: Pranks
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Deliver a vicious blow with a blackjack.
Syntax: BOP <target>
Abfile:
This ability requires that you have a blackjack wielded. With it, you may bop someone, either adventurer or denizen. It may be used in conjunction with the targeting ability in weaponry.

Additionally, when used against adventurers, it will deliver either amnesia, stupidity, or epilepsy.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
