Ability: Heartseed (Record #742)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: Send Nature's touch to destroy your enemy from within.
Syntax: HEARTSEED <target>
Abfile:
Among the most dangerous and forbidden of a sylvan's spells, the
Heartseed embodies Nature at its most baneful. Draining your victim's
vitality to speed its growth, the germination of a Heartseed will
rapidly end the life of he or she whom it is sent to plague.
If your victim manages to avoid their fate and cure the heartseed, their
body will be wracked by the process, causing them significant damage for
each injured limb they suffer from.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 750 mana
