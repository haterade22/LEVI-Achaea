Ability: Sunset (Record #3161)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Light on falling steel.
Syntax: BLADE SUNSET <target> <LEFT|RIGHT>
Abfile:
As the sun descends at the close of day, so too shall your rapier fall
upon your adversary. Delivered not as a traditional lateral or vertical
slash but as a diagonal blow down upon either the left or right
shoulder, this strike can be utterly devastating. If the head and arm
adjacent to the shoulder you are aiming at are sufficiently damaged(*),
both will be broken as a result of this strike, and tonal trauma takes
place upon both limbs(**).

* Both limbs must be damaged enough that the next bladedance attack
would break them.
** See AB BLADEDANCE TONAL.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
