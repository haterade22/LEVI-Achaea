Ability: Enthrall (Record #2878)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 399
Blurb: You are the nightmare that crawls and flies.
Syntax: SWARM ENTHRALL [type of insect]
SWARM BUILD <number of insect> <type of insect> [<number of insect> <type of insect> ...]
SWARM NAME <swarm> <new name>
SWARM SHOW <swarm>
SWARM DISPERSE <swarm>
SWARM DISMISS [RESERVES] <insect type>
SWARM DISBAND <swarm>
SWARM RESERVES
SWARMS
Abfile:
While in a location that you have detected sufficient insectoid life,
you may bend your arcane art to the task of bringing your soon-to-be
minions under your sway.

SWARM ENTHRALL: bring insects under your control. If there are multiple
types of insects in the location, you will need to specify which ones
you wish to enthrall.
SWARM BUILD: break your enthralled insects up into swarms.
SWARM NAME: give a personalised name to a swarm, for easy reference.
SWARM SHOW: see details about one of your swarms.
SWARM DISPERSE: disperse one of your swarms back into your reserves of
insects.
SWARM RESERVES: see your insects outside of swarms.
SWARM DISMISS: Get rid of unwanted insect types, even from your
reserves.
SWARM DISBAND: wipe one of your swarms.
SWARMS: see all of your swarms.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
