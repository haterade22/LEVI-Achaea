Ability: Atrophy (Record #1986)
Associated skill: Shadowmancy
Skillpoints required: 11999
Blurb: The strength just slips away.
Syntax: SHADOW INSTILL <weapon> WITH ATROPHY
Abfile:
The atrophy instillation was derived from the degeneration instillation
and has very similar properties, even if the arcane vector is slightly
different. In practice however, it strikes with weariness, then
clumsiness, and then paralysis, and if all three are present it will
deal nontrivial damage(*).

* The damage is significantly less if you do not have the target
attuned, and halved if you do not possess the target's shadow within
your phylactery.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
