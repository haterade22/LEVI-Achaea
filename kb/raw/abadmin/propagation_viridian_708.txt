Ability: Viridian (Record #708)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Become a mighty viridian.
Syntax: ASSUME VIRIDIAN [STAFF]
Abfile:
Meaning 'green' in an ancient language, a Viridian is a living, walking
embodiment of the flora of the land. Upon casting this spell, you will
quickly transform into one as thorny vines cover your body and burrow
into your flesh. You will become stronger, able to endure more damage,
and gain a protective bonus to physical damage done to you however your
movements will be slightly hampered.

HUMAN will allow you to change back into
your normal form. Be aware that remaining in this form is a constant
drain on your willpower.

* STAFF option may be added to allow transformation into the mighty
Viridian in areas that are not normally conducive to plant growth.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 200 mana
