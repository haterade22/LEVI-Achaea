Ability: Fireweapon (Record #1754)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: Basic use of shipboard weapons.
Syntax: LOAD <weapon> WITH <ammo>
UNLOAD <ammo> FROM <weapon>
FIRE <weapon> (When ship's target is set)
FIRE <weapon> AT <target ship>
FIRE <weapon> AT SEAMONSTER (For attacking seamonsters)
FIRE <weapon> UP (For firing flares)
Abfile:
 You now know your way around the basic weapons and armaments of a ship.

Different types of ship ammunition have different effects upon your foe.

Versus Ships
------------
Flare      - Burn brightly for a time, for signals, among other things.
           - Reveals nearby cloaked ships.
           - Available in numerous colours.
           - Fire from all ship weapons
Chainshot  - Sails damage, and will bind sails.
           - Sails will be unusable until they are CLEARed.
           - When dipped this will set the sails on fire.
           - Fire from an Onager.
Hookshot   - For grappling.
           - Fired from an Arcanian Arm.
           - Slows down target ship incrementally
           - Will reel in an immobilised vessel until adjacent
           - Effects last until target vessel chops the tether
Darts      - Hull damage and leaking.
           - When dipped they will set fires.
           - Rowing crew will be interrupted when hit.
           - Fire from a Ballista.
Spidershot - Binds a weapon on the enemy ship rendering it
             inoperable until it is CLEARed.
           - Fire from an Onager.
Starshot   - Encircles the ship's figurehead, causing any melded
             sailors to be unmelded.
           - The figurehead will remain unusable until it is CLEARed.
           - When dipped, it will start a fire on the bow.
           - Fire from an Onager.
War disc   - Causes the crew to duck and cower.
           - This will interrupt the crew for a short duration
             during which they will halt their current task and not
             accept orders.
           - Fire from an Arcanian Arm.

Versus Seamonsters
------------------
Flare      - Prevent a seamonster from moving for a very short time.
           - Fire from all ship weapons.
Chainshot  - Slow a seamonster's movement speed for a short time.
           - Fire from an Onager.
Darts      - Damage a seamonster.
           - Fire from a Ballista.
Spidershot - Decrease the damage of a seamonster's next attack.
           - Fire from an Onager.
Starshot   - Slow a seamonster's attack speed for a short time.
           - Fire from an Onager.
War disc   - Damage a seamonster (short range)
           - Fire from an Arcanian Arm.
