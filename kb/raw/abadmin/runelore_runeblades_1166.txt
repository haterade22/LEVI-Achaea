Ability: Runeblades (Record #1166)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: Runewardens may empower a weapon as a legendary runeblade.
Syntax: EMPOWER <weapon>
SKETCH PITHAKHAN ON <weapon>
SKETCH NAIRAT ON <weapon>
SKETCH EIHWAZ ON <weapon>
SKETCH HUGALAZ ON <weapon>
Abfile:
 With your expert training in Runelore, you can craft any knight's
weapon into a powerful Runeblade.

First sketch Lagul, Lagua, and Laguz upon the blade.
Then stand firm upon the earth and EMPOWER the blade.

Empowerment of a weapon lasts for 100 Achaean months, during which time
the Lagul, Lagua, and Laguz runes will not fade. In addition, a
Runeblade may have one other rune (Pithakhan, Nairat, Eihwaz, or Hugalaz
only) sketched upon it, enhancing its powers even further.

Useable on: adventurers

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 10.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 2000 mana
