Ability: Dawnkick (Record #2046)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 48999
Blurb: As inexorable as the rising Sun.
Syntax: DAWNKICK
Abfile:
This powerful kick targets the head, and can be performed against both a
prone foe and a standing one. When it lands, it will cause their brain
to be rattled and for them to see stars for a time(*). If they are
prone, this effect lasts longer and the damage is higher(**).

* Shortly after this kick lands, the target will be afflicted with
epilepsy.
** If they are prone, they will be afflicted again a short time after
the first time they receive epilepsy.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
