Ability: Explosion (Record #2566)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: Contained devastation.
Syntax: IGNITE EXPLOSION <target>
Abfile:
If your foe is marked with the brand of Kkractle you may cause it to
violently explode, causing them to take damage based on how many times
said brand has flared. Additionally, the more times the brand has
flared, the more additional effects shall strike them down:

Once: Afflicts with ablaze.
Twice: Afflicts the target with blackout.
Thrice: Stuns the target.

These are cumulative, so a brand that has flared three times will strike
with all of the three effects.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
