Ability: Bushwhack (Record #3265)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Ambush your prey with your animal companions.
Syntax: BUSHWHACK <animal> AT <target>
Abfile:
Through your link with your wild companions, you can send them to ambush
a healthy foe(*) and deal significant damage. This command requires that
the animal be summoned and at your side.

Due to your shared bond with Nature, when commanding your animal
companions in this way, they will draw upon your strength rather than
their own(**) during their ambush.

* Any denizen at or above 100% health.
** The damage of this ability scales with the Sentinel's strength stat.

Useable on: denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 5 endurance
