Ability: Multislash (Record #251)
Associated skill: TwoArts
Skillpoints required: 35999
Blurb: Spill blood with a succession of slashes.
Syntax: MULTISLASH <target>
Abfile:
By compounding your focus, you may slash your target thrice in a single, continuous motion. Such an attack will deal more damage and draw more blood than a lesser slash, but not permit an accompanying strike.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.50 seconds of balance
