Ability: Convocation (Record #1064)
Associated skill: Devotion
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: Call to you someone in a Rite of Convocation.
Syntax: PERFORM RITE OF CONVOCATION
PERFORM CONVOCATION <target>
Extra info: Rite cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium and 600 devotion.
Summon cooldown: 6.00 seconds of equilibrium, 600 devotion, 500 mana.
Abfile:
This powerful ability will allow you to create a Rite of Convocation. Anyone in a Rite of Convocation set up by you can then be summoned by you by doing PERFORM CONVOCATION <adventurer>, providing that adventurer does not have certain defences set up, such as the mass salve.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.80 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: Devotion and mana
