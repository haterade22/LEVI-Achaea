Ability: Portal (Record #432)
Associated skill: Groves
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: Open a portal to your grove for anyone in the forest.
Syntax: GROVE PORTAL <target>
Abfile:
With this ability, you can create a portal to any adventurer in the same area, who may then come to you through the portal. The portal is one-way: it may not be used to go to the adventurer.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 75 sunlight
