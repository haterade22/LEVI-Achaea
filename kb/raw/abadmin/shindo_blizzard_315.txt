Ability: Blizzard (Record #315)
Associated skill: Shindo
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Quench your foes with a storm of ice.
Syntax: SHIN BLIZZARD
Abfile:
Through a secret Shindo technique familiar only to masters of the art,
you may draw the heat from the air around you into yourself.
Manipulating the resultant freezing atmosphere into a shearing blizzard,
you may scourge all nearby with lashes of ice, freezing them and
destroying any heat vibration that might be present at your location. In
addition, once your power leaves your control, it shall settle in the
location, forming a temporary obscuring snowstorm.

Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 30 Shin energy
