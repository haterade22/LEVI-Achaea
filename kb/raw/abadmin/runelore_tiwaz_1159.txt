Ability: Tiwaz (Record #1159)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 37999
Blurb: This rune will remove the defenses of your enemies.
Syntax: SKETCH TIWAZ ON GROUND|<totem>
EMPOWER TIWAZ <target>
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Blue, 2 Red

Symbol: Upwards-Pointing Arrow

Strips away a defence of any enemy who enters and sees it. It disappears
after one use, unless it is on a totem.

When sketched upon the ground, it may also be empowered against a target
in the sky or trees above, and will draw them back down to your level.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
