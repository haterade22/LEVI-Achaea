Ability: Ruku (Record #2499)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 13999
Blurb: Numb the ribs or arms with a swift blow from your staff.
Syntax: RUKU <TORSO|LEFT|RIGHT>
Abfile:
Aiming blows at the arms or torso of a foe can have various effects. If
you target the arms they will become numbed, ruining motor control and
afflicting the target with clumsiness (or healthleech if the target
already has clumsiness). If you strike the ribs of your foe, you'll
inflict enough pain that they will begin to sweat profusely, afflicting
them with slickness.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
