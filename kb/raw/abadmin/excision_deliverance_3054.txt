Ability: Deliverance (Record #3054)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Deliver one of the faithful to your side.
Syntax: PERFORM DELIVERANCE <target>
Abfile:
This will allow you to summon an adventurer from any distance should
they have been blessed with the PREACHING ability by a priest. It should
be noted that a monolith sigil placed in the room of the summonee or
summoner will prevent this from working.

Be aware that delivering another adventurer out of combat may open you
up for repercussion.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: continent-wide
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
