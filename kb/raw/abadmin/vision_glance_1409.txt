Ability: Glance (Record #1409)
Associated skill: Vision
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: See summarised information about your surroundings.
Syntax: GLANCE/GL/QL
GLANCE <direction>
GLANCE GROUND
GLANCE HARBOUR
Abfile:
A simple ability that is inherent that allows you to see the contents of
your current room.

In addition, you may glance in a direction to see the contents of an
adjacent room, or glance ground if you are flying or in the trees, or
glance harbour if you are stood on the quarterdeck of a docked ship. It
should be noted these latter uses incur a minor equilibrium cost.

Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 0.50 seconds of equilibrium
The ability name GLANCING is mapped to this ability.
