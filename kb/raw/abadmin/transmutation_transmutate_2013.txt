Ability: Transmutate (Record #2013)
Associated skill: Transmutation
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Turn one mineral into another.
Syntax: TRANSMUTATE <#> <mineral> TO <mineral>
Abfile:
Using transmutation, one mineral may be turned into another. Ferrum to
antimony, realgar to arsenic, argentum to aurum: all is possible with
the correct knowledge and materials. In order to perform the
transmutation, you must be standing within a transmutation laboratory in
which you have permission to work.

Each transmutation requires reactants. The Cauda Pavonis has seen fit to
grant each city alchemist a stipend of 200 drams of transmutation
reactants per month. To use your stipend, simply perform a transmutation
within your home laboratory. Those who wish to perform additional
transmutations or to perform transmutations in other laboratories will
need to purchase additional reactants from one of the alchemists
residing within the alchemical laboratory complexes.

Your skill in Transmutation will determine which minerals you are able
to transmutate to (you may transmutate from any mineral, regardless of
skill).
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.75 seconds of balance
