Ability: Bonding (Record #371)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Increased rapport with the animal spirits.
Syntax: BOND SPIRIT
BOND SPIRIT OFF
Abfile:
When you bond with the spirit that you have metamorphosised into, your chance of being forced back to your mortal form is greatly decreased. The downside of this is that you will lose endurance more quickly from the increased strain.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.50 seconds of equilibrium
