Ability: Hermit (Record #608)
Associated skill: Tarot
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: Teleport to an uninhabited location.
Syntax: ACTIVATE HERMIT [tag]
FLING HERMIT AT GROUND [tag]
SNIFF HERMIT
HERMITS
Extra info: Activation: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium.
Abfile:
The Hermit is a solitary person. When you activate the hermit card, it will remember the location at which you activated it. Then, when you fling it, you will be transported to that location, provided there are no adventurers in that location at the time. SNIFF HERMIT will tell you the name of the location at which the hermit was activated.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
