Ability: Smoulder (Record #2559)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: A painful way to end.
Syntax: IGNITE SMOULDER <target>
Extra info: At least minor spark.
Abfile:
Set the flesh of your foe to smouldering, beginning their return to ash.
This attack deals damage and sets a target ablaze. If the target is
already ablaze, the damage will be significantly increased.

If your spark is at major or higher, this ability will decrease your
spark by one level, but will also reafflict your target with ablaze a
short time after you initially assault them and stun them for a short
while.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
