Ability: Dart (Record #1174)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 699
Blurb: A trap that shoots out a poisoned dart at its victim.
Syntax: ASSEMBLE DART TRAP
LAY DART <direction>
ENVENOM DART <direction> WITH <venom>
Abfile:
 Commodities: 3 wood

The dart trap shoots out a dart when sprung. It does no damage, but may
be envenomed from vials with venom in them, in which case the dart will
poison the victim with whatever venom was applied to the trap. Like all
traps, it has a bit of magic in it and will only affect your enemies.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
