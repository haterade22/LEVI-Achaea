MINERALS
Abfile:
The alchemical minerals are present throughout the land, and may be
found with sufficient purity to be drawn from the ground in certain
environments and areas. Mountains, the underground, and magma caves all
have the potential to contain extractable minerals, and different cave
systems and mountain ranges may yield different minerals. The amount of
minerals is limited, however, and you will only be capable of extracting
each mineral once per room each day.

Your skill in Transmutation will determine which minerals you have
learned to extract.
