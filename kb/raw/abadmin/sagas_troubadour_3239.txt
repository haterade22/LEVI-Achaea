Ability: Troubadour (Record #3239)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Where ever the story takes you.
Syntax: passive
Abfile:
A good storyteller must go where ever the tale may take them, though
equally it is true that some legends just don't hit as hard when
constantly on the move.

You may now move while performing many of your sagas for an audience,
them no longer being interrupted if your location changes between recite
stages(*).

* Channeled sagas are excluded.

Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
