Ability: Startle (Record #2962)
Associated skill: Valour
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: The steed is often an easier target.
Syntax: EAGLE STARTLE <target>
Abfile:
This ability will allow your eagle to startle an opponent's steed,
tossing the poor rider off. Note that this works regardless of whether
your opponent is on the ground, in the trees, or flying. You simply need
be in the same room, and the eagle will fly to the correct elevation to
startle your target's mount.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
