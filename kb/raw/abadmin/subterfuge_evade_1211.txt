Ability: Evade (Record #1211)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 1499
Blurb: Misleading your pursuer.
Syntax: EVADE <direction|GRATE>
Abfile:
No difficult feat for one trained in the arts of subterfuge, you may
silently evade past obstructions, at once losing anyone who may be
following you.

* You may also evade through sewer grates while out of phase with
reality, assuming you have learned how to enter them normally via the
survival skill.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
