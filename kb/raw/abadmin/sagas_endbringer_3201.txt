Ability: Endbringer (Record #3201)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: The day when Death did die.
Syntax: RECITE ENDBRINGER <target>
Abfile:
A tale of tragedy and long held grudges, the Endbringer's Demise is a
story with only one inevitable and indefatigable conclusion. Reciting
this solemn recounting to a listener will take all of your focus, and
yet upon its final word they shall be cast from this life and into those
Halls that Thoth once did rule.

* Bypasses all forms of special resurrection such as starburst. The
target immediately embraces death upon being slain.
** This is a channeled action which resolves faster the more stacks of
the crescendo affliction are present on the target when you begin the
recital.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 25.00 seconds of voice balance
