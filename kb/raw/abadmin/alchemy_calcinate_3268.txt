Ability: Calcinate (Record #3268)
Associated skill: Alchemy
Skillpoints required: 35000
Blurb: Burn formicaries to the ground.
Syntax: CALCINATE FORMICARY
Abfile:
By creating layered circles of alchemical gold and iron around a
formicary you can burn the formicary to ashes, completely destroying it.
Those who are able to create formicaries may be notified of your attempt
to destroy one.

This will require concentration, and any disturbance will cause the
attempt to fail.
Useable on: room
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
