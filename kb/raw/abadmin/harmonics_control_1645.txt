Ability: Control (Record #1645)
Associated skill: Harmonics
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Master the manipulation of your harmonics.
Syntax: HARMONICS LIST                 - lists harmonics in the current room.
HARMONICS DISMISS <harmonic>   - terminates a harmonic early.
Abfile:
Harmonics Control, one of the fundamental abilities in the Harmonics skill, allows you to review and dismiss your harmonics. HARMONICS DISMISS accepts either an ID or a harmonic type. For example, HARMONICS DISMISS gigue, or HARMONICS DISMISS 12345.
