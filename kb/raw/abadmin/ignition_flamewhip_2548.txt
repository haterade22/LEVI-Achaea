Ability: Flamewhip (Record #2548)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 399
Blurb: Scourge their flesh with a lash of flame.
Syntax: IGNITE FLAMEWHIP <target>
Abfile:
Summon forth a whip of flame with which to scourge the flesh of your foe
and deal damage.

If the target is an adventurer, they will be set on fire; if they have
less than 75% of their maximum health, they will be afflicted with
sensitivity. If they have less than 50% of their maximum health or lack
the temperance defence, they will be afflicted with both paralysis and
sensitivity.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
