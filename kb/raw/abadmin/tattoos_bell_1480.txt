Ability: Bell (Record #1480)
Associated skill: Tattoos
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: Be warned when there are those spying on you.
Syntax: TOUCH BELL
Extra info: Inks: 3 Blue inks
2 Red inks
Abfile:
Upon touching this tattoo, you will gain an acute awareness of attempts
to appraise your possession. This effect may not trigger if you are
mesmerised.

In addition, having this defence active will alert you to some forms of
spying, most notably that accomplished via use of whisperstones.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
