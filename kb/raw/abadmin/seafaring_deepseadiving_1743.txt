Ability: Deepseadiving (Record #1743)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 16999
Blurb: Dive deep for the sea's hidden treasures.
Syntax: SHIP ENTER BELL - Available to all with deepseadiving.
SHIP LOWER BELL - The Captain can order the bell lowered into the ocean.
SHIP RAISE BELL - The Captain can raise the bell back up to the deck.
Abfile:
Using a diving bell, dive for treasure in the deep seas. Once the bell
is lowered fully divers can go DOWN to access the ocean floor and UP
from the arrival location to enter the bell once more.

Be ware. There are many perils to be encountered at sea, ignorance high
amongst them. Should a ship move, either by intention or currents, any
divers on the ocean floor will perish. Should a careless Captain raise
the diving bell while divers remain on the ocean floor, the unfortunate
divers will perish.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
