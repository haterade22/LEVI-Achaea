Ability: Link (Record #2719)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: Your mind is far superior.
Syntax: PSI LINK <target>
PSI UNLINK
Abfile:
Link your mind to another and you will take attacks against their mind
upon yourself. Be warned, if your mana falls below half of your maximum
you will die outright and if not resurrected by a starburst tattoo or
similar shall find your soul lacking significantly in substance(*).

* You will die and be left on low mana as a soul.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
