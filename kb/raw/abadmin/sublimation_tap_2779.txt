Associated skill: Sublimation

Blurb: Tap the power of the Wellspring.

Due to long exposure, you have learned the process of tapping the
Wellspring of energy which lays beneath the city of Hashan. Every so
often your reserves of energy shall refill from the Wellspring
passively.

You may enact the process actively yourself, though you may only do this
so often or risk catastrophic harm to yourself.

Useable paralysed: No
