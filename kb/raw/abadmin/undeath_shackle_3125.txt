Ability: Shackle (Record #3125)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: Fettered to mortality.
Syntax: SOULBLEED SHACKLE <target>
Abfile:
Bind a living in shackles of soulbleed, limiting their ability to
scramble away from your onslaught(*).

* While shackled, a person cannot leave the area.
** This ability can only be used every so often.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
