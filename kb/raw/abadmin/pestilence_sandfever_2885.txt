Ability: Sandfever (Record #2885)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: A single step is a quest indeed.
Syntax:
Abfile:
Beneath the oppressive sun of the desert the infamous sandfever was
born. A hapless individual labouring under this disease will find
movement a truly exhausting task; once mighty warriors will find
themselves drained by even the most simple of movement(*), and once
sharp minds shall fall into silent disarray(**).

* While afflicted with this affliction a person's celerity becomes 1:
this means you can only move one room before having to wait to move
again.
** You cannot focus your mind while afflicted with sandfever.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
