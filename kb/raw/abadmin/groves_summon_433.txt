Ability: Summon (Record #433)
Associated skill: Groves
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: After a time, bring anyone in the forest to your grove.
Syntax: GROVE SUMMON <target>
Abfile:
With this ability, you may begin the process of summoning any adventurer in a forest to your grove. It will take a small amount of time, during which the adventurer may escape the summoning by moving. Also any actions on your part during the summoning process will halt the summons.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 50 sunlight
