Ability: Burst (Record #304)
Associated skill: Shindo
Skillpoints required: 22999
Blurb: A crude release of force.
Syntax: BURST <target>
Abfile:
Shin energy is a source of power both pliant and potent. You may unleash
a measure of this energy upon a target in a crude but damaging burst of
raw force.

This attack will bypass, but not destroy, magical shields and prismatic
barriers, and does more damage against both prone opponents and those
suffering a damaged head(*).

* These bonuses both stack with one another, but with diminishing
returns.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 70 mana
