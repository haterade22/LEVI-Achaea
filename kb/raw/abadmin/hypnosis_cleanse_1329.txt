Ability: Cleanse (Record #1329)
Associated skill: Hypnosis
Skillpoints required: 36999
Blurb: Clean yourself or another of suggestions.
Syntax: CLEANSE <adventurer/ME> <suggestion>
Abfile:
This ability allows you to cleanse hypnotic suggestions from an
individual.

You may also cleanse yourself, but in this instance you can only remove
suggestions that would force you to carry out a physical action.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
