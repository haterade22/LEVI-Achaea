Ability: Tah'maal (Record #2446)
Associated skill: Terminus
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: Yet closer to the elusive legend.
Syntax: INTONE TAH'MAAL
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Though the true cloak of the Tsol'teth overlords is far out of your
reach, this word when uttered over a Shadowmancy cloak brings you yet
one step closer. It shall improve the cloak so that, when worn, it shall
grant you increased resistance to the destructive force of fire. In
addition, one wearing an augmented cloak who performs the rebirth
ability from Aeonics shall return with more health and mana than one
without. The recovery time of the rebirth ability shall also be
significantly reduced.
Useable on: none

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of word balance
