Ability: Powderise (Record #2531)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: Smash their bones to bloody powder.
Syntax: TERRAN POWDERISE <target> <limb>
Abfile:
Target the limbs of your enemies and smash them to bloody powder. The
more you have shaped your body, the more damage this attack will do
(both in terms of health damage and limb damage). Additionally,
powderising limbs has different effects.

Head: gives a stun, which is longer the more you have shaped your form.
Torso: knocks the target off balance, longer based on how much you have
shaped your form.
Either arm: breaks said arm.
Leg: knocks your target prone if you have sufficiently shaped your form.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
