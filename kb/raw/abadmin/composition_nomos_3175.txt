Ability: Nomos (Record #3175)
Associated skill: Composition
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: That most disciplined resolve.
Syntax: COMPOSE NOMOS
Abfile:
The relentless march of your nomos shall crush the resolve of your
enemies, destroying reflections raised about their person and revealing
them for who they are beneath(*).

* If they have only one reflection, it is destroyed. Otherwise the
number of reflections protecting them is halved.

When used on denizens while refrained, it will break shields.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
