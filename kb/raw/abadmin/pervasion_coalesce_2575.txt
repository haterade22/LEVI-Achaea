Ability: Coalesce (Record #2575)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Upon the Elemental Plane of Water.
Syntax: COALESCE
Abfile:
You may cross great distances, traversing the very planes themselves to
materialise upon the Elemental Plane of Water.

Should you already be present upon the Elemental Plane of Water, this
act will return you to the Prime instead.

Such an act takes great focus, requiring that you do nothing for several
seconds while focussing on the process.

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
