Ability: Songbird (Record #1703)
Associated skill: Voicecraft
Skillpoints required: 59099
Blurb: Whistle a helpful songbird to your shoulder.
Syntax: WHISTLE FOR SONGBIRD
Abfile:
Whistle for a songbird who will accompany many of your songs for a time,
assisting you in battle. When you sing one of the songs listed below as
"Triggers", the songbird will occasionally choose one of the songs
listed below as "Actions" and direct the song at your target.

Triggers: Solo, Chant, Passion, Pastorale, Qasida, Ditty, Isorhythm,
Requiem, Noise, Vibrato, Tremolo, Maqam, Epic, Haiku, Poem, Ode,
Limerick

Actions: Passion, Pastorale, Ditty, Requiem, Maqam, Haiku, Ode.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: Yes
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 500 mana
