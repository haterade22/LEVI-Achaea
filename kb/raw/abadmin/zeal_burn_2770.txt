Ability: Burn (Record #2770)
Associated skill: Zeal
Skillpoints required: 27999
Blurb: Let the fire speak for you.
Syntax: RECITE BURN <target>
Abfile:
Perhaps the most straight forward of all holy verses, but no less
powerful for that. Send fire to scourge the flesh of your profane foe,
leaving them tender and pained to such an extent that any attempt to
apply a salve shall cause them to suffer in mind(*).

* Drains mana on applying salves.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.60 seconds of prayer balance
