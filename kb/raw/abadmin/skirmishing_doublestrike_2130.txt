Ability: Doublestrike (Record #2130)
Associated skill: Skirmishing
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Strike, then strike again.
Syntax: DOUBLESTRIKE <target> [limb] [venom]
Abfile:
With this ability, you may use your chosen weapon to deal a lateral
slash, then using the momentum continue your attack to strike with the
haft of your weapon.

Against adventurers, the blow with the haft shall first deliver
impatience. If the target is already impatient, it shall deal epilepsy.

Against denizens, your damage will be split amongst the slice of your
weapon and strike of its haft.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance cost varies based on weapon speed
