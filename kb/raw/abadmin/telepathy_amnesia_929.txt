Ability: Amnesia (Record #929)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 32999
Blurb: Induce temporary forgetfulness into your subject.(*)
Syntax: MIND AMNESIA [target]
Abfile:
A simple-sounding, though extremely effective ability, this allows you to cause short-term amnesia in your locked adventurer. He will simply fail to execute the next action he attempts, as he will forget what he is doing before he manages to do it.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 mana
