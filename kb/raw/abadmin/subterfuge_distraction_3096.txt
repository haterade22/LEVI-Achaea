Ability: Distraction (Record #3096)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: The blindspot is where you thrive.
Syntax: CONJURE DISTRACTION <delay> [MINUTES]
Abfile:
With your mastery of illusion and knowing where to apply pressure just
so, you can now weave an illusory distraction over a city(*). You can
delay your distraction to activate up to 2 minutes(**) after you weave
it, and the process of weaving requires you do nothing for the initial
preparation. Due to the complexity of this process and how critical it
is that you be able to layer the illusion upon the correct reality,
distractions cannot be formed while phased.

When the distraction activates, the guards of said city will be too
focussed on identifying if it is a genuine attack or not to respond to
calls for help from citizens(***). The distraction itself lasts around
five minutes.

Guards who are already on high alert will not fall for the
distraction(****).

* You must be an enemy of the city in question.
** The delay is specified in seconds by default, though you can specify
it in minutes if so desired.
*** Guards will still respond to calls for help from other guards or
denizens, for instance if one is attacked.
**** Guards are on high alert during all sanctioned raids or for around
20 minutes after a previous distraction has ended.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 6.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 1500 mana
