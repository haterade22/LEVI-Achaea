Ability: Snake (Record #1265)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 56999
Blurb: A pet snake to assist you in combat.
Syntax: SUMMON <snake>
SUMMON <snake> INVENTORY|INV
Abfile:
The first time you summon a snake, you may have to try many times before
you're successful. Eventually though, the snakes of the wild won't be
able to resist your serpentine charms, and one will come slithering to
serve. Once you have your snake, you may summon it back at any time
should you lose it (simply SUMMON <snake> [INVENTORY|INV] again).
Your snake may be ordered to perform the actions listed in HELP
DENIZENS.

Note: City/House membership influences what precisely is summoned.
      Ashtan summon kraits.
      CIJ summon puffadders.
      Eleusis summon treesnakes.
      Hashan summon vipers.
      Mhaldor summon adders.
      Targossas summon moccasins.
      Cyrene summon lanceheads.
      All others summon rattlesnakes.
