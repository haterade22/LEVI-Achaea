Ability: Hyena (Record #2971)
Associated skill: Malignity
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: A vicious agent of the Inferno.
Syntax: HYENA
Abfile:
The vicious demonic Hyenas of the Inferno are bound to one of Lord
Sartan's infernal warriors. These vicious predators know not the
frailties of mercy or kindness, and only their dark masters may ever be
safe from the rending of their jaws.

Your hyena comes to you with assorted statistics which have been
randomly assigned.

If you cause your hyena to die, you will be refused another hyena.

There is absolutely no excuse for killing or conspiring to kill your
hyena.

Below is a list of commands that may be used once you have gotten your
hyena.
HYENA ASSESS    : See information about your hyena.
HYENA RECALL : Recall your hyena to your side.
HYENA SANCTUARY : Order your hyena to a sanctuary where it will be safe.
HYENA TRAIN : Order your hyena to go train, in order to raise a stat.
HYENA REPORT : Gain information on where your hyena is and who they
are tracking and attacking.
HYENA REPORT LOCATION : This will tell you the location of your loyal
(this will incur an equilibrium cost.)
HYENA SLAY      : Order your hyena to slay someone.
HYENA HUNT : When your hyena is hungry, have it go hunt for its food.
HYENA FLEE : Set the amount of health remaining when your hyena will
flee battle.
HYENA SLEEP     : Order your hyena to sleep.
HYENA FEED RAT  : Feed the corpses of rats to your hyena.

As your hyena gains in
* Stamina:  its health will increase.
* Speed: it will attack more quickly as well as move from place to place
* Strength: it will attack with more power.

You will gain other abilities later on that are related to your hyena.

You must visit the summoning chamber within the Mhaldor cathedral to
request your hyena.
Useable on: none
