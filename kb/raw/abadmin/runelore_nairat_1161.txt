Ability: Nairat (Record #1161)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 42999
Blurb: A rune to entangle.
Syntax: SKETCH NAIRAT ON GROUND/<totem>/<weapon>
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Yellow
Symbol: Butterfly

When sketched upon the ground or on a totem, an enemy will be entangled
upon seeing this rune. It will disappear after one use unless it is on a
totem.

When sketched upon a weapon, this rune causes the weapon to FREEZE its
target at random. To sketch it upon a weapon requires the weapon to be a
RUNEBLADE.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
