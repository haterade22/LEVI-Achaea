Ability: Bloodscent (Record #3245)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: Your prey shall not escape.
Syntax:
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Once you have the scent of your prey's blood in your nose, nothing will
stop you from tracking them down(*).

* When you strike a tumbling target who is already bleeding with your
spear, you will gain the bloodscent defence. This permits you to bypass
most forms of room hinder while it lasts (approximately ten seconds -
this timer does not refresh).
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
