Ability: Propagate (Record #2197)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 1999
Blurb: Enhance yourself with nature's bounty.
Syntax: PROPAGATE <HEAD|BODY|ARMS|LEGS> WITH <plant>
PROPAGATIONS
Abfile:
This is the core of the propagator's strategy. While in Viridian form,
you are able to propagate one of either your head, body, arms, or legs
with a plant. As you progress in propagation, you will learn to utilise
more plants in this way. Each plant conveys a different benefit
depending on the location you grow it.

Returning to human form will remove all of your propagations.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds
Resource: 200 mana
