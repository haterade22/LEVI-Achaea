Ability: Infuseearth (Record #2805)
Associated skill: TwoArts

Blurb: The unyielding earth shall empower your blade.

Abfile:
Channel shin energy into your blade, infusing it with the ferocity of
the unyielding earth. When so empowered you will strike past attempts to
dodge your blows, finding your mark no matter the difficulty of the
cut(*).

* Does not apply to multislash.
** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
