Ability: Titan (Record #2545)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Primordial fury.
Syntax: TERRAN TITAN
Abfile:
The ultimate gift imparted to the servants of Garash: the ability to tap
into the earth and transform into a form even greater than your own. In
this form you will be utterly impervious to damage, will be able to
perform the paste ability while upon the ground, the juggernaut ability
will have no preparation period, squeeze damage will be increased, and
your stats will be significantly increased. Note, however, the gift is
fleeting; it will only last a short time before dissipating and leaving
you stunned.

Note that to become a titan you must have shaped your form to the
maximum extent possible. This ability carries a significant cooldown
when performed outside of the Elemental Plane of Earth.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
