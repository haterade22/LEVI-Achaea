Ability: Barge (Record #2949)
Associated skill: Valour
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: Barging your opponents.
Syntax: BARGE <target> <direction>
Abfile:
This ability will barge an adventurer to the direction you choose. To
start the ability, you must be in a room adjacent to the room where the
target adventurer resides, and you must choose to barge him in a
direction that wouldn't take him into the room you are in. So, for
instance, say that Isildur is in a room to the north of me, and that
this room has another exit to the northeast. I would do BARGE ISILDUR
NE.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
