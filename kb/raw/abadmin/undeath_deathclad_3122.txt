Ability: Deathclad (Record #3122)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: Reborn for war.
Syntax: SOULBLEED CLAD
Abfile:
Summon your birthright. You are born of soulbleed, and so too shall you
wield it, both as blade(*) and shield(**).

* This skill will manifest two scimitars for you to wield for further
Undeath skills.
** You will also gain a defence that provides resistance to damage. This
resistance improves the higher rank of undead you are.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 2
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
