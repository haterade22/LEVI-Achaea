Abfile:
The forms of Shikudo are the backbone of the art. You can only be in one
form at a time, though you may flow between the forms at opportune
moments, once you further your studies. Many of the Shikudo strikes may
only be performed in certain forms, and each form conveys its own set of
benefits upon the practitioner.

The Tykonos form is primarily a defensive form, and you will find
yourself able to divert damage more easily when in this form.
