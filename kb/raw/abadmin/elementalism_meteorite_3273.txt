Ability: Meteorite (Record #3273)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 41999
Blurb: To sunder that which wards.
Syntax: CAST METEORITE AT <target> <FLAMING|FROZEN|PURE> [4-10]
Abfile:
This arcane work enables you to call down a delayed meteorite to shatter
magical shields. If no shield is present when the meteorite resolves
then the spell will fail, otherwise the shield is broken and an effect
is conveyed:

Flaming: a level of the ablaze affliction.
Frozen: a level of freeze.
Pure: a random broken limb.

* Note that in addition to the air channel always mandated by this
ability a fire, water, or earth channel will additionally be required
for the respective meteor type.
** If you do not specify a delay, a random one in the range is chosen.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.40 seconds of equilibrium
