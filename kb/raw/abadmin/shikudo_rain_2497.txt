Ability: Rain (Record #2497)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: Countless drops to conquer mountains.

Abfile:
The form of Willows in Rain Storm is an offensive form, but reliant on
repeated strikes rather than powerful blows. You may perform kata chains
of twice the normal length when in this form before risking stumbling
out of form, and as said kata chain grows longer the damage you deal
shall increase.

Additionally, your equilibrium recovery shall be significantly increased
when in this form.
