Ability: Sustenance (Record #2695)
Associated skill: Attainment
Skillpoints required: 47999
Blurb: Sleep is for the weak. Food too.
Syntax: CONFIG DEPRIVATION [ON|OFF]
Abfile:
You will no longer grow more tired or more hungry.

Note that this only applies to the natural effects of the passage of
time; some special abilities may still cause hunger or tiredness.

This is automatically OFF for all who reach level 80, but you can toggle
this with the DEPRIVATION configuration.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
