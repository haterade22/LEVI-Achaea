Abfile:
Seek out Certimene in Delos to begin learning another class, subject to
some restrictions.

Once you have learned an additional class, you may switch between
classes once every 12 Achaean days (12 RL hours), though this time can
be reduced in various ways.

See HELP MULTICLASS for details.

You will continue to gain additional class slots for every 10 levels
past 100.
