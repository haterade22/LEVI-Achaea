Ability: Soulspear (Record #152)
Associated skill: Necromancy
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Meld soul-matter from the dead into a physical weapon.
Syntax: EVOKE SOULSPEAR FROM <target corpse>
THROW SOULSPEAR AT <target> <direction>
Abfile:
Soulspear allows the skilled Necromancer to form a weapon forged in the
soul of the dead. By taking an adventurer corpse, you can form the
soulspear. A corpse from an adventurer level 30 and under has too weak a
soul to form such a powerful weapon out of. Beyond that, the higher the
level of the victim, the more powerful the soulspear.

Once you have it, you may throw it at someone who is outside your room,
anywhere in your line-of-sight. It will shatter shields and prismatic
barriers, or, if the person lacks one of those, the necromantic energy
will be released, summoning forth hands of the grave in your victim's
location.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 6.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 2.50% life essence
