Ability: Rigging (Record #1741)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 1999
Blurb: Climb up into the rigging above.
Syntax: CLIMB RIGGING
CLIMB DOWN RIGGING
CLEAR RIGGING
Abfile:
Climb up the ropes to a place on the rigging above the deck, from any
deck location. (You can use SWING UP and SWING DOWN as well.)
In addition, you may use clear rigging to attempt to disentangle the
rigging from unwanted obstructions.
Useable on: none

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
