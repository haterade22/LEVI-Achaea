Ability: Burst (Record #2938)
Associated skill: Kaido
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: When the external will not supply.
Syntax: KAI BURST
Abfile:
Sometimes there will be no simple way to take in kai energy the
conventional way. Though the highest workings of kaido may be out of
your reach in these situations, by sending a burst of uncontrolled kai
energy outward you may somewhat mitigate this limitation(*). This is
taxing however, and may only be done every so often, as well as
stripping any active kai boost defence you may have established(**).

* This fills your kai to a little under half.
** Once every 90 seconds, and you cannot reraise your boost defence
until this time has expired.
*** Note if you're above a certain kai threshold you can't use this
ability (i.e. when it would be pointless to do so).
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
