Ability: Enrage (Record #2134)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Drive one of your companions into a rage.
Syntax: ENRAGE <animal> <target>
Abfile:
Through your link with your companions, you are able to drive one into a
rage, causing them to do a more dangerous attack than they normally
would. This does not consume balance or equilibrium, and the animal does
not need to be actively summoned for you to call it forth for a single
attack.

Badger: will deliver bleeding and addiction. If the target already has
addiction, will deliver nausea.

Fox: will deliver healthleech.

Wolf: will give hallucinations.

Lemming: will strip shield or rebounding; if neither are present, it
will strip a random defence. Shield is always stripped before
rebounding.

Raven: will deliver sensitivity.

Butterfly: will unblind its target.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
