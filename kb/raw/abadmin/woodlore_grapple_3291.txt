Ability: Grapple (Record #3291)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 55500
Blurb: Spring one of your own traps to haul yourself out of danger.
Syntax: GRAPPLE <direction>
Abfile:
Spring a trap you have laid in the given direction to haul yourself into
the adjacent room. It is destroyed if you are successfully moved.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
