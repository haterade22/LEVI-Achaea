Ability: Chargeshield (Record #714)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: Create a magical shield to resist electric damage.
Syntax: CAST CHARGESHIELD AT ME/<target>

The chargeshield is a defensive spell which will provide you with some
protection from electric attacks.

In addition, possessing this defence will reduce the equilibrium penalty
caused by the confusion affliction.

Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 200 mana
