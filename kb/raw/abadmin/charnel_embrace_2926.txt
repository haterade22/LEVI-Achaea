Ability: Embrace (Record #2926)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: The bane of life.
Syntax: ACCURSED EMBRACE SICKNESS
Abfile:
Many terrible things were born of dabbling in the realm of death, but
perhaps one of the most terrible consequences was the sickness that the
Accursed brought into the living world. To embrace your nature as an
Accursed is to accept that sickness upon yourself; death shall have its
due(*), and time shall not heal this wound(**).

* If you die with the death sickness and are resurrected by starbursts
or similar means, you will not be cured of your afflictions. However,
the sickness will pass from you to your killer in this instance.
** Those labouring beneath the death sickness are entirely immune to
lesser aeonic workings: attempts to bring down the curse of the Aeon
upon such a target without foci to amplify the working (a vodun doll or
puppet for example) shall fail.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
