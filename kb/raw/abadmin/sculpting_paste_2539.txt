Ability: Paste (Record #2539)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 45999
Blurb: You are nothing if not decisive.
Syntax: TERRAN PASTE <target>
Abfile:
When either flying or in the trees, you may launch yourself down at one
who walks upon the ground, crushing them beneath your weight. This
incurs a stun and non trivial damage.

If used after assuming the form of a Titan, it will be usable from any
elevation against any foe(*), and additionally break limbs if your
target is already prone.

* When in Titan form, this skill can be used against denizens, dealing a
large amount of damage.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
