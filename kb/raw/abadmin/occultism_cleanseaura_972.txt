Ability: Cleanseaura (Record #972)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 32999
Blurb: Cleanse away the natural defences of an aura.
Syntax: CLEANSEAURA <target>
Abfile:
This obscure occultic ability allows one to strip away the natural
defences of a target's aura, exposing them to more invasive assaults.

When used against an adventurer, you will require your target's mana be
40% or lower to be successful. In this weakened state, certain eldritch
entities will be able to exploit the victim's aura to great effect(*).

Against denizens, this will cause your foe to take ten percent more
damage from occult abilities(**), chaos hounds(***), and the
weaponmastery attacks of your fellow Unnamables(****). In this case, the
equilibrium and karma cost of this occultic art shall be greatly
reduced.

* See AB DOMINATION GLAAKI for details.
** OCCULTISM WARP and ATTEND.
*** DOMINATION LYCANTHA.
**** All weapon-based attacks in the WEAPONMASTERY skill when performed
by an Unnamable.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 1% karma
