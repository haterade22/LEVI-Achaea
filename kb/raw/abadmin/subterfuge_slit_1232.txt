Ability: Slit (Record #1232)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: Cut the throat of a helpless victim.
Syntax: SLIT <target>
Abfile:
Provided you are wielding either a dagger or dirk, this ability will allow you to slit the throat of your enemy who is bound or not conscious (sleeping, meditating, unconscious), causing significant bleeding damage.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.50 seconds of balance
