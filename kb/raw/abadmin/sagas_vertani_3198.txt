Ability: Vertani (Record #3198)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: Empire's wrath unleashed.
Syntax: RECITE VERTANI <target>
Abfile:
A tale of peaceful approach turned to brutal war, the retelling of the
Vertan Empire's coming can end only one way. You may recite this Saga
while focused upon someone nearby and outdoors(*), and at its
culmination meteors shall descend to shatter the magical shields of said
individual and all who stand alongside them. Note that once the
retelling begins, even if the target flees, so long as they remain
outdoors the meteors shall still fall upon them, no matter how far they
might flee(**).

* Nearby for this purpose is within the same local area.
** Within reason. Crossing planar boundaries or similar may prove too
much, even for the glory of Vertan's Empire retold.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same area at any elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 18.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
