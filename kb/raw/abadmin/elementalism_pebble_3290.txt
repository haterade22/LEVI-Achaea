Ability: Pebble (Record #3290)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 30000
Blurb: Hurl a pebble to break one of your foe's limbs.
Syntax: CAST PEBBLE AT <target>
Abfile:
Compress a handful of earth into a single dense pebble and hurl it at a
target. Should the pebble strike true, it will shatter one of your
target's healthy limbs, as well as causing damage. If every limb has
already been broken, the pebble will still wound your target, and your
resonance will not grow.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 90 mana
