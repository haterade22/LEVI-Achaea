Ability: Raido (Record #1150)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Summon a spiritual steed to bear you home.
Syntax: SKETCH RAIDO ON GROUND
SAY RIDE HOME
RAIDO LOCATE
RAIDO SEVER
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Green
Symbol: Horse

After sketching the rune upon the ground, say 'ride home' from anywhere
on the continent, and be taken back to the rune, which will be
destroyed.

RAIDO LOCATE will show the location of your existing raido rune, if any.
RAIDO SEVER will remotely smudge your existing raido rune, if any.

Note: The northern areas only accessible through the wilderness are
treated as a separate continent.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 10.00 seconds of balance
