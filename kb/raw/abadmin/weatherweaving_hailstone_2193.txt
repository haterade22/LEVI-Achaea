Ability: Hailstone (Record #2193)
Associated skill: Weatherweaving
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: Simple is best.
Syntax: CAST HAILSTONE AT <target>
Abfile:
Summon forth a hailstone to strike your target in the side of the head.

If the target is an adventurer, this will give them clumsiness. If they
are already clumsy, it shall give epilepsy. If they have epilepsy, it
shall give impatience.

This spell also causes cold damage to both adventurers and denizens.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 33 mana
