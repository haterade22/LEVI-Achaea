Ability: Duet (Record #3249)
Associated skill: Composition
Skillpoints required: 58599
Blurb: Such harmony profound.
Syntax: DUET <target>
Abfile:
Though your own performances are doubtlessly grand beyond the reckoning
of all that would behold them, sometimes one may simply wish to raise
the level of another, perhaps tragically less gifted, musician. By
performing this ability you will greatly raise an actively building
crescendo about another(*), so long as someone other than yourself is
responsible for it(**).

* Sets their crescendo to one stack below the finale threshold.
** You cannot be the one who gave the crescendo affliction in question.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of equilibrium
