Ability: Nauthiz (Record #1155)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 29999
Blurb: Suck the nourishment from your foes.
Syntax: SKETCH NAUTHIZ ON GROUND
Abfile:
 Inks required: 1 Blue, 1 Yellow

Symbol: Leech

This destructive rune is unique in that it functions differently under
certain conditions.

When sketched in a magically flooded location(*), it will cause the
water to be drained away immediately, consuming the rune in the process.

When sketched out of water it will persist, and will suck the
nourishment from your enemies standing in the room.

* Nauthiz is special in that it can be sketched even through water.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
