Ability: Path (Record #3287)
Associated skill: Chivalry
Skillpoints required: 50000
Blurb: To walk Honour's path.
Syntax: WALK SIGIL
Abfile:
"Though we walk many paths, we journey upon the same road."

Through an Oath taken up by twelve knights of an earlier time, passage
into ancient catacombs beneath Aster Keep is available to those of the
Guild who are members of a Knightly Order.

A knight of the Guild may WALK a path upon Chivalry's sigil in the
Coenobium, tracing the steps that reveal their honour. They will then be
able to traverse starlight's veil and enter halls set aside for Orders
alone.

NOTE: Errants cannot complete this path, for they are without a Knightly
Order.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
