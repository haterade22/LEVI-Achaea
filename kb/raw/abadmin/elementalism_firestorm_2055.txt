Ability: Firestorm (Record #2055)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: The very world shall burn at your command.
Syntax: CAST FIRESTORM
Abfile:
This formidable higher order fire spell will call a rampant firestorm
into being. This will spread to all adjacent rooms and will periodically
sear the flesh of any who stand within said locations.

Be warned. Fire is truly an indiscriminate force. You shall not be
spared from a firestorm, even if you were the one to call it forth.
Useable on: room
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 mana
