Ability: Imbue (Record #2037)
Associated skill: Augmentation
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Imbue scrolls with transferrable enchantments.
Syntax: RECITE <scroll> [ON] <item>
Abfile:
Your mastery in the field of augmenting items has progressed to such a
degree that you are now able to imbue scrolls with enchantments rather
than standard items. This is done in the normal way, simply by
substituting one of the enchantable scrolls for the target item.

Once you have enchanted a scroll, anyone may recite the scroll on an
item, transferring the stored enchantment to that item. This will
destroy the scroll, and normal limitations apply (such as only being
able to recite the scroll on footwear if it is storing the walk on water
enchantment).

You may purchase scrolls from most general stores around Sapience.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
