Ability: Wreathe (Record #2164)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Wreathe your hands in searing flame.
Syntax: WREATHE
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Using some of your inner flame(*), you may wreathe your hands in fire
for a short time.

When mauling an adventurer, this will deliver the ablaze affliction; if
they are already on fire, it will do significantly increased damage.

When mauling a denizen, your damage will increase and be dealt as fire
damage rather than cutting.

* This ability will decrease your inner flame by one level.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Skill specialisation: meta_druid
