Ability: Shipmeld (Record #1789)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Summon the ship's sea spirit and share in its powers.
Syntax: INVOKE SEA SPIRIT
RELEASE SEA SPIRIT
TOUCH FIGUREHEAD
Abfile:
You have gained the power to meld your psyche with that of the ship
itself via the ship's figurehead, invoking a sea spirit to summon
seaspells for your ship. Touch the figurehead while shipmelded to find
out how much power it currently stores, and what seaspells are currently
active. RELEASE SEA SPIRIT will undo this melding.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
