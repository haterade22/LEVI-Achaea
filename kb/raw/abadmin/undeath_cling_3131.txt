Ability: Cling (Record #3131)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: You will not go. At all.
Syntax: SOULBLEED CLING
Abfile:
Should your physical form begin to crumble(*), this defence will ensure
you cling onto unlife for as long as possible(**). During these final
moments you will be invulnerable to all damage, but when your unholy
fortitude fades you will fall into death and attempt to drag all those
living with you(***).

* If you drop below 10% of your maximum health.
** You will become immune to all damage for 10 seconds.
*** After 10 seconds has elapsed, you will die and deal a percent of all
the damage you should've taken to those in your room.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of balance
