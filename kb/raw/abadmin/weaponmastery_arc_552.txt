Ability: Arc (Record #552)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 47999
Blurb: Swing your blade in a controlled arc.
Syntax: ARC [<venom>]
ARC <target> [<venom>]
Abfile:
By swinging your sword in a controlled arc, you will be able to damage
everyone in the room with your sword.

If you optionally specify a target, the arc shall only strike that
person, but the blow is far swifter to recover from (3.00 seconds).

Note that this ability may be performed off equilibrium.
Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.75 seconds of balance
