Ability: Haskor (Record #1074)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 47999
Blurb: The Hero made divine.
Syntax: RECITE HASKOR
Abfile:
The Saga of Haskor is one of many battlefields, but through it all runs
a single golden thread: Heroism.

Speak the saga of Haskor, and all mutual allies that hear your tale will
fall under the mantle of your heroism, defended against death so long as
you stand. Beware, though. If you fall... the day is dark indeed.

* For around half a minute after this saga takes effect, your allies
protected by said saga will never know death by the hands of other
adventurers so long as they stand with you. However, if you perish, all
who heard it shall fall with you as well...
** There is a cooldown of five minutes on using this ability, and it is
apparent to all when it is activated.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 20.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
