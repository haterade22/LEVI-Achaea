Ability: Engage (Record #2998)
Associated skill: Malignity
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Damage a cowardly opponent.
Syntax: ENGAGE <target>
Abfile:
There is nothing more despicable and dishonourable than a coward, and
this ability will allow you to punish cowards, as is their due. Once you
have engaged an adventurer, if he is otherwise unhindered and tries to
leave the room, you will get in an extra strike, whether you have
equilibrium and balance or not.

This ability may be initiated if off balance.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 mana
