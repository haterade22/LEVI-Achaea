Ability: Bloodboil (Record #717)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: Boil off that which afflicts you.
Syntax: CAST BLOODBOIL
Abfile:
Through the purifying powers of fire, and the recuperative powers of
water, you may cure yourself of an affliction with this spell. If you
suffer under the haemophilia affliction your blood will be too thin for
this spell to take effect, and if you have both major resonance with the
Elemental Planes of Fire and Water this ability will have a chance to
cure a second affliction.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 75 mana
