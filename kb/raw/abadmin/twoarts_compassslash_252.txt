Ability: Compassslash (Record #252)
Associated skill: TwoArts
Skillpoints required: 41999
Blurb: Send their blood to the points of the compass.
Syntax: COMPASSSLASH <target> <direction>
Abfile:
Draw and slash your target from one of the cardinal directions, causing more bleeding than a lesser slash.

The points of the compass:

  NORTH     - head
  EAST      - left arm
  SOUTHEAST - left leg
  SOUTH     - torso
  SOUTHWEST - right leg
  WEST      - right arm
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
