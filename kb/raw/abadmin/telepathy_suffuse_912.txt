Ability: Suffuse (Record #912)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: Suffuse the mind of your subject with mental energy.
Syntax: MIND SUFFUSE
Abfile:
Not all of the Telepathic arts are concerned with harming your target. This is one that is not. What it does is take some of your considerable mental strength and give it to the adventurer you have mind locked, albeit with some loss due to the energy it takes to transfer the mana over the telepathic link.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: Mana
