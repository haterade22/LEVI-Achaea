Ability: Cleave (Record #2360)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: A sundering blow.
Syntax: WEAVE CLEAVE <target>
Abfile:
Using an axe, cleave through magical protections surrounding your
foe(*). When you cleave through a magical shield, also knock your target
prone.

* Strips aura of rebounding and magical shield (both at the same time).
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
