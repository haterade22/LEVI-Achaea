Ability: Shadowstrike (Record #192)
Associated skill: Apostasy
Skillpoints required: 16999
Blurb: Use your daegger to strike at the shadow of a victim.
Syntax: DAEGGER SHADOWSTRIKE <target>
Abfile:
Once you have levitated your daegger, you can order it to perform the
deadly shadowstrike. Your daegger will fly towards your foe, seeming to
miss, but instead plunging into the gut of their shadow.

Against adventurers, this will have the same effect as if the daegger
had impaled the target through his or her stomach. Your target will take
damage for as long as he or she is impaled, and will have to writhe
painfully off the daegger.

When used against a denizen, your daegger will deal cutting damage upon
striking the target's shadow.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.50 seconds of equilibrium
The ability name DAEGGERSHADOWSTRIKE is mapped to this ability.
