Ability: Ekanelia (Record #3250)
Associated skill: Venom
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: A lethality profound.
Syntax: EKANELIA
EKANELIA STUDY <reaction>
Abfile:
Your grasp of venom has reached such a profound transcendence that you
are now able to turn the very immune system of your victim's against
them. Certain venoms, when delivered in just the right quantities, can
promote violent immune responses which in turn lead to cascading
failures and further afflictions manifesting throughout the subject's
body.

Each reaction has:
A trigger: the venom which sets off the cascading failure (this is what
you bite with).
Conditionals: the afflictions which the subject must already labour
under.
A result: the result of the reaction taking place.

Some reactions have:
A bonus conditional: a bonus conditional which can be met.
A bonus result: the bonus which is achieved if the bonus conditional is
met.

Naturally, with the exact dose and balance requirements of this high art
of venom, only the fangs of the serpent are sufficient for this
purpose(*).

* You may see what reactions you are aware of with the EKANELIA command,
and study one at further length with the EKANELIA STUDY <reaction>
command.
** Reactions fire on the bite command.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
