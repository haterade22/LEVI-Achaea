Ability: Sear (Record #200)
Associated skill: Apostasy
Skillpoints required: 24999
Blurb: Order your Baalzadeen to sear the flesh of an enemy.
Syntax: DEMON SEAR <target>
DEMON SEAR OFF
Abfile:
At your command, your Baalzadeen will sear the flesh of a target with
unholy fire.

When used against a denizen, your Baalzadeen will scorch them with its
breath, dealing fire damage. Your Baalzadeen does not lose health when
this skill is used against denizens.

When used against an adventurer, your Baalzadeen will begin to regularly
ignite your target. Each time it sets the foe ablaze in this way, your
Baalzadeen will use up some of its health.

DEMON SEAR OFF or DEMON REST will cause your demon to stop searing the
target.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 Baalzadeen health
Drain: Baalzadeen health
The ability name DEMONSEAR is mapped to this ability.
