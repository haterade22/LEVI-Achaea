Ability: Drain (Record #135)
Associated skill: Necromancy
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Drain your life essence into another Necromancer.
Syntax: DRAIN <target>
Abfile:
With this ability you can transfer life essence from yourself to another
necromancer. Unfortunately, it is not a perfect transfer and some life
essence will be lost in the process. You can avoid this imperfect aspect
if carrying out the transfer upon Mhaldor Isle (or within Mhaldor city
proper), as these are locations so closely tied to the necromantic arts
that the working becomes far easier.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Drain: Life Essence
