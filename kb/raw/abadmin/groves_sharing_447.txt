Ability: Sharing (Record #447)
Associated skill: Groves
Skillpoints required: 36999
Blurb: Granting the use of your grove to your allies.
Syntax: GROVE SHARE
GROVE WITHDRAW
Abfile:
Grove sharing will allow any grove user on your allies list to use your
grove as if it were his own. GROVE WITHDRAW will disable grove sharing.

The abilities hive, flow, portal, summon, gate, concealment, and
guardian cannot be used in a shared grove.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 200 sunlight
