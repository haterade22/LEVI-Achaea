Ability: Relentless (Record #2830)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 399
Blurb: His legion will not falter.
Syntax: HELLFORGE CHANNEL RELENTLESS
Abfile:
Empower your hellforged armour with the relentless endurance of the
Master, and you will be unswayed by those effects which would attempt to
inspire fear in you(*). You will also be less impacted by the negative
effects of the stupidity affliction.

* You still receive the afflictions, but you will not be affected by
them.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
