Ability: Hiraku (Record #2496)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 6999
Blurb: Some people simply talk too much.
Syntax: HIRAKU
Abfile:
Aim a strike at the face of your foe with your staff, shattering teeth
and making it impossible for them to eat(*). If they are also prone,
they will be stunned for a short time.

* They will be afflicted with anorexia and stuttering.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
