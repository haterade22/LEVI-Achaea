Ability: Catapult (Record #1187)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: A trap that will throw your enemies out of the room.
Syntax: ASSEMBLE CATAPULT TRAP
LAY CATAPULT <direction> <direction to throw person>
Abfile:
 Commodities: 1 Rope, 1 Wood

The catapult trap is laid in a direction like all traps, but is also set
to throw its victim in a particular direction.

So, for instance, say you are in a room with north and west exits. You
might do: LAY CATAPULT NORTH WEST. Then, when one of your enemies either
tries to leave the room going north, or tries to enter the room from the
north, he will be thrown west and thrown off-balance momentarily.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
