Ability: Agony (Record #2844)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 49999
Blurb: They shall bleed for the Lord.
Syntax: none
Abfile:
Your hellforged weapon will now feed upon the blood of your target. When
it strikes a heathen bleeding 100 or more you will be healed in body,
and if it strikes a heathen bleeding 200 or more you will additionally
be cured of an affliction. Additionally, any heathen that suffers from
the healthleech affliction delivered by you will cause you to be cured
of an affliction when their strength is drained(*).

This is passive and always active so long as you are wielding your
hellforged weapon.

* This effect has a twelve second cooldown. It does not lockout the
bleed
requirement, but it also does not stack with it.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
