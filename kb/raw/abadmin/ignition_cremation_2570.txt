Ability: Cremation (Record #2570)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: A fire to sear the soul itself.
Syntax: IGNITE SOUL OF <target>
Abfile:
Burning the physical is simple; with this ability, you may lash at the
soul of your target, harrying it to such a degree that upon it leaving
its prison of flesh upon true death, it shall immediately be forced to
flee to the halls of Ugrach.

On a successful cremation(*) you will not be able to use this ability
again for ten minutes.

* A successful cremation specifically is if your victim dies and auto
embraces death.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
