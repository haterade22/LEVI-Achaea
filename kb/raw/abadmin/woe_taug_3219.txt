Ability: Taug (Record #3219)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 16999
Blurb: A doom on Gruul's line.
Syntax: RECITE TAUG
Abfile:
Bound, but never broken. Imprisoned, but yet imperious. The tale of that
guise named Taug is one which doomed the line of Gruul and broke a
nation even in their triumph. Speak this tale most grim and the blood
rains will fall, and all will remember the horror of the king without a
crown.

* Blood rain in a location prevents magical travel into that location.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 19.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
