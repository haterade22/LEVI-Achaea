Ability: Astralform (Record #979)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Turn yourself into a being of pure energy.
Syntax: ASTRALFORM
HUMAN
ASTRALISE <direction>
Extra info: Human cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Abfile:
Having mastered all facets of your aura, you can now become pure spirit,
though you will moderately lose willpower as you strain to hold your
nebulous form together. HUMAN is the command to return to your normal
form.
In addition, you may ASTRALISE in a direction to bypass closed doors,
though this is a channeled action and will not bypass doors that are
locked.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 250 mana and 200 karma
