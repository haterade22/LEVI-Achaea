Ability: Crush (Record #935)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 43999
Blurb: Attack your subject with a crushing psychic attack.(*)
Syntax: MIND CRUSH [target]
Abfile:
Mind crush sends a crushing wave of mental energy at the target,
inflicting both physical and mental damage. This ability can be cast on
any target in the same room as you without the need for a mind lock.

When used at a distance, the target must be no more than five rooms away
or within line of sight.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.40 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 150 mana
