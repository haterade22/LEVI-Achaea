Ability: Strangle (Record #2618)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 23999
Blurb: Crush the life from them with bands of air.
Syntax: AERO STRANGLE <target>
Abfile:
Wrap bands of air about your foe, crushing the life from them.

Against adventurers, this will deprive them of oxygen and make them
light-headed and mentally slowed(*). A time after strangling your foe,
if they are still deadened in mind they shall become disrupted(**). If
they are not, the bands shall remanifest, giving them deadening once
again.

Note that this periodic tick will continue to occur until a tick occurs
where deadening is reapplied.

Against denizens, this will deal asphyxiation damage and then relapse,
causing additional damage after two seconds.

* Gives deadening and damage.
** This time is shorter the more pressure you have built up around your
target. See AB DURESS PRESSURE for details.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
