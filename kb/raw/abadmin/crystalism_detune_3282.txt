Ability: Detune (Record #3282)
Associated skill: Crystalism
Skillpoints required: 48999
Blurb: By your design, or none at all.
Syntax: DETUNE <target>
Abfile:
Sometimes a magi may find that another seeks to set the tone of an
engagement, creating vibrations to block the use of one's own.

This unacceptable state of affairs is no true barrier to the
accomplished sorcerer. You may DETUNE another magi, and all vibrations
span by them in your location will become detuned.

* Detuned vibrations hit the owner as well as their enemies.
* Detuned vibrations inform all magi in the current location when they
influence someone.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
