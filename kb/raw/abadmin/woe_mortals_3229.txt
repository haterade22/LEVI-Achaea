Ability: Mortals (Record #3229)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 53999
Blurb: United at the last.
Syntax: RECITE MORTALS
Abfile:
So now we speak of the closing days of the Age of Blackest Woe, and when
mortals at last united as one to bring down the forces of the Infernal
Prince. When reciting this tale, all Cyrenians in rooms adjacent to you
that share allyship with you(*) or who are in your current party will be
summoned to your side(**).

* Mutual allies.
** Rooms with a monolith when the recital begins are excluded. A
monolith at recital culmination will have no influence, however.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 20.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
