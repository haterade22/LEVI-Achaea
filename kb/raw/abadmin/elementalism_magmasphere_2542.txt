Ability: Magmasphere (Record #2542)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: A delayed eruption of magmatic fury.
Syntax: CAST MAGMASPHERE <seconds> (Between 10 and 60)
Abfile:
This powerful spell creates a sphere of magma that, after some time has
elapsed (specified by you), will violently explode. The flying stone has
a chance to break the limbs of any in the current location in addition
to the significant damage that it will always deal.
Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
