Ability: Jab (Record #3150)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Control the distance.
Syntax: BLADE JAB <target> <LEFT ARM|TORSO|RIGHT ARM>
Abfile:
The simplest of techniques with the rapier, this swift jab teaches the
student to gauge distance accurately while also inflicting piercing
wounds to an adversary. As with other bladedance techniques, the jab
will have different results based on your position in relation to your
partner and where the jab lands. These results are as follows:

From the front:
At the arms: the limb will be broken.
At the torso: afflicts with nausea.

From the side:
At the arms: afflicts with clumsiness.
At the torso: afflicts with asthma.

From the back:
At the arms: gives weariness. If the jab breaks the arm, deals bonus
damage.
At the torso: afflicts with anorexia. If the jab breaks the torso, tonal
trauma takes place(*).

* See AB BLADEDANCE TONAL.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.10 seconds of balance
