Ability: Sear (Record #1022)
Associated skill: Spirituality
Skillpoints required: 16999
Blurb: Order your angel to sear an unlucky adventurer.
Syntax: ANGEL SEAR <target>
ANGEL SEAR ICEWALL <direction>
Abfile:
With this ability, you may command your guardian angel to sear an
opponent with intense heat, causing them to burst into flames. If the
target is a denizen, this will not consume any of your angel's power.

If your target is an adventurer and already ablaze, they shall become
sensitive to pain(*).

Alternatively, your angel can target an icewall with its flames, melting
it.

* This respects the deafness defence: if this is present the angel shall
strip it instead.

Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 10 Angel power and 100 mana
