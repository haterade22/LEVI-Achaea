Ability: Preempt (Record #2417)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: There is no escape.
Syntax: CHRONO PREEMPT <target> [BOOST]
CHRONO UNPREEMPT
Abfile:
By predicting where a person will be, whenever they try to move to an
adjacent location from your current one you shall preempt them, moving
to that location first.

This ability requires you be unhindered(*). The effect lasts 15 seconds
if unboosted. If boosted, it lasts for twice that. Additionally, if
boosted the ability will not incur an equilibrium cost.

* This requires you not be prone, have paralysis, or writhe-based
afflictions; it does not include having balance or equilibrium.

Note that after preempt expires against a target, that target is immune
to -any- Depthswalker using preempt on them for a short time afterwards.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 250 age
