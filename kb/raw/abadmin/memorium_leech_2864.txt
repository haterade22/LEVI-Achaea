Ability: Leech (Record #2864)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: To draw out the poison.
Syntax: TRACE LEECH <ME|target>
Abfile:
One of the lesser logographs, the leech cannot be worked into an
epitaph, though it is useful all the same. When traced for yourself or
another this logograph shall periodically draw out the afflictions which
plague you(*).

* Roughly every ten seconds, and lasting for five minutes.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.50 seconds of balance
