Ability: Soulcage (Record #158)
Associated skill: Necromancy
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Harness your life essence to escape death itself.
Syntax: SOULCAGE ACTIVATE
SOULCAGE DEACTIVATE
SOULCAGE STATUS
Extra info: Essence: 30% - New soulcage
3% - Reactivate soulcage
2% - Deactivate soulcage
Abfile:
This is perhaps the greatest of the necromantic abilities, and carries with it the greatest life essence cost. It allows you to spin a web of life essence around your very soul, protecting it from death. Soulcage may only be used once every 6 Achaean days.

The soulcage can be deactivated by raising a barrier of life essence between it and your soul, preventing it from saving you from death. It may be reactivated later for a much lower essence cost.

SOULCAGE STATUS will show the current state of your soulcage, if any.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 12.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 30% life essence
