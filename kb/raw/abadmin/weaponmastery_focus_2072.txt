Ability: Focus (Record #2072)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 30000
Blurb: Alter your technique.
Syntax: BATTLEFURY FOCUS <SPEED|PRECISION>
Abfile:
This ability allows a trained knight to adjust their stance to influence
their next attack.

Focusing on speed increases the speed of your next attack.

Focusing on precision alters your next attack for greater accuracy and
effect(*). Against adventurers, this reduces the damage dealt but
doubles the number of limb-based afflictions inflicted. Against
denizens, it increases the damage dealt.

* Your attack will also bypass any Shield of Absorption effect your
foe possesses.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of battlefury balance
Skill specialisation: knight_twohanded
