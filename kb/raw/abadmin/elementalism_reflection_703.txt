Ability: Reflection (Record #703)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 299
Blurb: Create a distracting illusion of yourself.
Syntax: CAST REFLECTION AT ME/<target>
Abfile:
By focusing the light of fire through a prism of elemental air, you can
create a magical reflection of yourself, which will absorb one attack
before being destroyed. You may only have one reflection at a time.

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
