Ability: Extinction (Record #3064)
Associated skill: Anathema
Skillpoints required: 57999
Blurb: Creation's scourge.
Syntax: CROON EXTINCTION <target> <limb1> <limb2>
Abfile:
Croon to your victim of the power of extinction, that which breaks and
destroys. If they are already fragile to its workings, they shall know
the power of devastation firsthand(*).

* Any limb that you specify which is broken to the point of needing a
restoration salve shall take very significant (around 25% per stack)
further limb damage. This is increased substantially for each stack of
the horror affliction said target suffers from.
** There is a 1 minute cooldown on you crooning this ability, and a
target has a 45 second immunity from it after being hit with it once.
*** You cannot target this ability against the torso.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of anathema balance
Resource: 4% karma
