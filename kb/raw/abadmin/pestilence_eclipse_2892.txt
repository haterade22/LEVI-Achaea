Ability: Eclipse (Record #2892)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 42999
Blurb: Darkness beneath the writhing clouds.
Syntax: SWARM ECLIPSE <swarm>
Abfile:
There will always be those that think that they can outfly your swarms.
They are wrong. By directing one of your swarms made up of flying
insects to eclipse the sky in a location, any who try to take to the air
to escape that location will find that your minions are not so simply
evaded(*). If you attempt to take to the skies yourself, your swarm will
immediately disperse from its task.

* They will be knocked back to the ground and off balance.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of equilibrium
