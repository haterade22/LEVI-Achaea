Ability: Funeral (Record #3232)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: The tale to end all tales.
Syntax: RECITE FUNERAL
Abfile:
All tales speak of dread Woe's final rest, yet with its speaking you may
yet put another to theirs in turn. Recite this story over the corpse of
a mighty denizen(*), and all within the location that have not
participated in a funeral recently(**) will be bolstered(***).

* The denizen must be one immune to critical strikes and of a
sufficiently potent level. The corpse is destroyed.
** You can only recite a funeral every 24 hours and participate in one
every 24 hours.
*** Everyone in the audience receives some experience.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 20.00 seconds of voice balance
