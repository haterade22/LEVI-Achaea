Ability: Muddle (Record #2715)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 19999
Blurb: A gentle touch to confound and mislead.
Syntax: PSI MUDDLE <target>
Abfile:
This subversive psionic art will not harm your enemy directly, but will
cause mental maladies to affect them more significantly(*).

* They shall have a greater chance of failing actions due to stupidity,
and are more likely to suffer the adverse effects of dizziness.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
