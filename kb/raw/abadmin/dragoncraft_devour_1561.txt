Ability: Devour (Record #1561)
Associated skill: Dragoncraft
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Destroy them in a single bite.
Syntax: DEVOUR <target>
Abfile:
Use your massive jaws to rip your opponent's head from their shoulders.
The more restoration broken limbs your victim suffers from, the faster
you may complete your devouring.

Note that a damaged torso will boost the speed of this ability
significantly more than other limbs. If your devour would take less than
six seconds to complete in total, it will not be interruptable via
several of the standard methods, such as via the paralysis affliction or
being knocked to the ground.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
Skill specialisation: dragonform
