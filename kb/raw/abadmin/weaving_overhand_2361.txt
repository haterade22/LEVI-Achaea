Ability: Overhand (Record #2361)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: Bring them down with a crushing blow.
Syntax: WEAVE OVERHAND <target>
Abfile:
Deliver a crushing overhand blow with a mace.

If the target is already knocked to the ground, this blow will rattle
their skull so much that they will become impatient; if they are not,
they will be knocked to the ground and suffer from a bout of stupidity.

In addition, impatience will be delivered if this blow also breaks the
target's head with the strike, if the target is afflicted with
stupidity, or if their head is already broken.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
