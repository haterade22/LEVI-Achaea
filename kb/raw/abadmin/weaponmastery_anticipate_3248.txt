Ability: Anticipate (Record #3248)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 38999
Blurb: You see every blow that's coming.
Syntax: ANTICIPATE
Abfile:
Once you have gained an understanding of the flow of a given battle, you
may enter a state of heightened anticipation, during which you will
mitigate most attempts to physically strike at you.

* Requires maximum ferocity and resets to zero.
** For six seconds (or until it fires three times), you will reduce most
physical damage blows to zero.
*** This ability has identical bypass conditions as the shield of
absorption artefact.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: equilibrium and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Skill specialisation: knight_snb
