Ability: Devour (Record #3271)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Leave no scraps.
Syntax: SWARM DEVOUR <swarm> <target>
Abfile:
Command a ravenous swarm of burrowing insects to surge towards a
denizen, where they will rip and tear flesh, dealing cutting damage.
This ability can only be performed every so often(*), unless the swarm
fully devours its target(**).

* Every 40 seconds.
** If this attack lands the killing blow, the cooldown is immediately
refunded.

Useable on: denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 40.00 seconds
