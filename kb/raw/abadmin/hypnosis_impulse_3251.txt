Ability: Impulse (Record #3251)
Associated skill: Hypnosis
Skillpoints required: 30999
Blurb: Some pressure here, some manipulation there.
Syntax: IMPULSE <target> <suggestion>
Abfile:
It is one thing to sink your mark into a hypnotic trance and set their
mind into a downward spiral, but quite another to simply lay them low
with a glance. Nevertheless, you are now equal to both tasks.

By triggering impulses in the mind of your victim, you may inflict them
with one of the trivial suggestions(*) that are known to you.

* See ab hypnosis for the list of trivial suggestions.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.70 seconds of equilibrium
