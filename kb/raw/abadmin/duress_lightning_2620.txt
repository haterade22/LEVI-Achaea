Ability: Lightning (Record #2620)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 29999
Blurb: Become the storm.
Syntax: MANIFEST LIGHTNING <target>
Abfile:
Become living lightning itself, allowing you to cross vast distances in
an instant.

If you are on the ground and enact this ability against someone flying
above you in the skies, you shall instantly move to the flying elevation
at no balance cost.

If you enact this ability against someone on the same continent who is
also outdoors while you are flying, you shall immediately transverse to
their location (though be warned, you will transverse to the ground
rather than the sky). This manifestation of lightning does incur a short
balance cost.

Note that both forms of becoming lightning carry a short delay before
you may do so again.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
