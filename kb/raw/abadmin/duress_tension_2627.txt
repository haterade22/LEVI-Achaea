Ability: Tension (Record #2627)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 47999
Blurb: Turn the air against them.
Syntax: AERO TENSION <target>
Abfile:
Increase the tension around a target(*), as well as slightly increasing
the pressure of the air surrounding them(**).

* It is not possible to land if in flight, though external sources may
still pull you back to the ground.
** Increases pressure by one if present, otherwise gives the pressure
affliction.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
