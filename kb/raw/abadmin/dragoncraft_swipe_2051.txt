Ability: Swipe (Record #2051)
Associated skill: Dragoncraft
Skillpoints required: 44999
Blurb: Maul your prey with your talons.
Syntax: SWIPE <target> <limb> <HEAD|TORSO> [venom]
Abfile:
Swipe at the upper body of your prey with your deadly talons, carving
into a limb followed swiftly by a followup strike at either the head or
torso. This does the equivalent damage of rending with your claws, but
equally split across both limbs(*).

* Note that both limbs are still vulnerable to being parried.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
Skill specialisation: dragonform
