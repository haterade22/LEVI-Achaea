Ability: Sting (Record #2887)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: Pain that comes on countless wings.
Syntax: SWARM STING <swarm> <target>
Abfile:
Send a swarm composed of stinging and flying insects to descend upon
your enemy. Beyond simply suffering the pain of the ordeal, if your
victim is an adventurer they shall also be infected by whatever plague
your swarm is infected with.

Weariness will affect this ability.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: equilibrium
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
