Ability: Unbowed (Record #3042)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: For Creation, you stand.
Syntax: PERFORM UNBOWED
Abfile:
This defence will grant you a great surge of health when you enter a
drastically wounded state, similar to a monk's vitality. Notably
however, your faith is so strong that unlike a monk, this ability will
even save you from blows that would otherwise slay you(*).

* Excluding if the damage was unblockable.
*** There is a cooldown after this fires before you may raise the
defence again.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
