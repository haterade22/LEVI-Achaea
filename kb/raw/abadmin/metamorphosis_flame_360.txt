Ability: Flame (Record #360)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Blast your foe with magical flame.
Syntax: SUMMON FIRE
FLAME <target>
FLAME <icewall-direction>
FLAME GROUND
BELCH
RELAX FLAME
HYDRA FLAME <target>*
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Before this ability may be used, you must build up fire inside you. Type
SUMMON FIRE to begin this process. When you are ready to breathe yellow
fire, you may FLAME <target> or FLAME <icewall-direction>. BELCH or
RELAX FLAME will get rid of the flame prematurely.

* Hydra morph only. Unleashes a devastating blast of magical fire
towards your target. This version can be used against denizens.

Useable on: adventurers, room, and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Equilibrium
