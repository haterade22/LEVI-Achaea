Ability: Jars (Record #2889)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: A little goes a long way.
Syntax: JAR STORE <jar|person>
JAR RETRIEVE <jar|PERSON>
JARS CARRIED
JARS STORED
JAR FILL
CRUX BLOODY <person>
JAR CLEANSE <person name|jar ID>
Abfile:
With blood such an important medium, it is always worth having some on
hand. The practiced Pariah will store the blood of their most dangerous
foes on their person for rapid use; the truly great will also keep an
extra reserve within the depths of their crypt.

JAR FILL: wipe the blood from your ritual blade into a jar.
CRUX BLOODY <person>: immediately bloody your ritual blade with the
blood from a jar on your person.
JARS CARRIED: see what jars you are carrying, and how many applications
you will be able to derive from their sanguine contents.
JARS STORED: in your reliquary, see what jars you have stored.
JAR STORE <jar ID|person name>: store a jar in your reliquary.
JAR RETRIEVE <jar ID|person name>: retrieve a jar from your reliquary.
JAR CLEANSE <person name|jar ID>: cleanse a jar you are holding of
blood.

You may carry five jars on your person at a given time, and your
reliquary may hold up to ten in addition.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
