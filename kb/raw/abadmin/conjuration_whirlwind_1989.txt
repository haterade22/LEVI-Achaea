Ability: Whirlwind (Record #1989)
Associated skill: Conjuration
Skillpoints required: 26999
Blurb: The roaring whirlwind of Vastar.
Syntax: BEGIN CREATION OF WHIRLWIND OF VASTAR
THROW WHIRLWIND [AT <target>]
Abfile:
This item is destroyed on use.

Type:
   Created item.

Actions:
   4x4, 16, 9, 4x4

Comms:
   1 silver, 1 diamond dust, 1 stag's horn

Throwing this whirlwind skywards will knock all in the air to the
ground.

Alternatively, throwing it at a specified target will cause the
whirlwind to pick you up and attempt to carry you after that person, if
they recently fled the area you are in via magical wings of any kind.
The window of opportunity is around five seconds, and people will not be
able to follow you through the process of being flung up by the
whirlwind.
