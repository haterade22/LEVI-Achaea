Ability: Onslaught (Record #3123)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: Relentless is death.
Syntax: SOULBLEED ONSLAUGHT <target> <limb>
Abfile:
Unleash a flurry of two blows at one of the limbs of your victims. Your
wrath is so great that you may not be parried, and will infect them with
two diseases; if you cannot inflict two diseases (because they already
have too many of the options for the given limb), you'll add a stack of
fracture to that limb instead.

Head: slickness, anorexia, confusion.
Either arm: paralysis, nausea, darkshade.
Torso: paralysis, asthma, healthleech.
Either leg: Paralysis, weariness, clumsiness.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
