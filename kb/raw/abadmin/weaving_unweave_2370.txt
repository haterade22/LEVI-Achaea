Ability: Unweave (Record #2370)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 25999
Blurb: From dust to dust.
Syntax: WEAVE UNWEAVE <target> <BODY|MIND|SPIRIT>
Abfile:
Begin the process of unweaving part of an individual's fundamental
essence. Each type of unweaving has separate effects and the longer the
unweaving process persists, the more deadly these effects become:

Body: health loss whenever the unweaving proceeds.
Mind: mana loss whenever the weaving proceeds.
Spirit: each time the unweaving proceeds, an additional cure is required
to cure the spirit unweave. This is unique to the spirit unweave.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
