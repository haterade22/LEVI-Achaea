Ability: Lineage (Record #3098)
Associated skill: Chivalry
Skillpoints required: 5000
Blurb: Know the past.
Syntax: LINEAGE [member of the guild]
Abfile:
Every aspiring or current knight should know of the lineage of which
they may be becoming a part of. Taking on a patron knight should not be
done lightly by any aspiring squire, and knights would do well to keep
their lineage untarnished, lest all the guild know of the mark.

* If you do not specify a target you will see your own lineage, starting
with you.
