Ability: Burrow (Record #2886)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 13999
Blurb: Into the warm and dark.
Syntax: SWARM BURROW <swarm> <target>
Abfile:
Send your swarm to burrow into the flesh of your foe, lingering within
their body. This utilisation of your swarms does not do more than cause
bodily discomfort(*), but there are many opportunities available to one
who can invade the corpus once this has been achieved(**).

* Does damage.
** There are further abilities which can be utilised via a burrowed
swarm.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
