Ability: Conflagrate (Record #2217)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 44999
Blurb: Summon forth a terrible conflagration.
Syntax: CAST CONFLAGRATE AT <target>
Abfile:
This higher order fire spell will cause a conflagration to engulf your
target. This conflagration will deal periodic damage until the person is
able to extinguish it. The only way to do this is to remove all traces
of the ablaze affliction from their person.

For the purposes of this spell, suitably vulnerable requires that the
target has two or more stacks of the ablaze affliction.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.35 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 150 mana
