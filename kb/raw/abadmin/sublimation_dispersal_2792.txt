Ability: Dispersal (Record #2792)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: Before its even close.
Syntax: SUBSTANTIATE DISPERSAL <type1> [type2]
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Impose your will upon reality, causing certain forms of energy entering
your location to be dispersed when focussed upon you(*).

Valid types are cutting, blunt, fire, cold, poison, electricity,
psychic, or asphyxiation.

Overdraw effect: you may specify a second type of energy to disperse,
and the effect remains for longer.

* You may reduce the damage taken by that type for yourself in the
location.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 3% Wellspring Energy
