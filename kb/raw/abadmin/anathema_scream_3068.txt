Ability: Scream (Record #3068)
Associated skill: Anathema
Skillpoints required: 41999
Blurb: Let the cry of desolation sound once more.

Unleash a psychic scream against your victim, one no barrier shall avail
them against(*).

* This deals damage that bypasses magical shields and prismatic
barriers.

Additionally this skill consumes no karma when targeted against
denizens.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.60 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 0.50% karma
