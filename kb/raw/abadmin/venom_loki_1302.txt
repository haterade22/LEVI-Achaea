Ability: Loki (Record #1302)
Associated skill: Venom
Skillpoints required: 42999
Blurb: An unpredictable toxin.
Syntax: SECRETE LOKI
BITE <target>
Abfile:
While the unpredictable loki toxin is well documented to deliver a
random venom when introduced to the victim's bloodstream, that's not the
entire story.

When injected through the fangs of one of the descendents of its
namesake, the Loki venom first lies insidious within the bloodstream,
apparently not doing anything at all: and yet having a severe effect in
truth(*). Repeated bites of the loki venom will continue to deliver the
well known effect.

* Significant negative poison resist, and hypnotic impulses relapse a
short time after first being introduced.

* This venom can be used to envenom arrows, but not other weapons.
