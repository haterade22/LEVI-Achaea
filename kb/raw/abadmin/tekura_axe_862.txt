Ability: Axe (Record #862)
Associated skill: Tekura
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: A slow but powerful kick.
Syntax: AXK <target>
Abfile:
This powerful kick is highly inaccurate and can only be landed against a
prone opponent. However, it does extremely high damage to the target's
head.

This kick will briefly deliver the scrambled brains affliction. If used
against a target who suffers from a mangled head, it shall
deliver a severe blackout.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 6.50 seconds of balance
