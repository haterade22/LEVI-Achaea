Ability: Frontkick (Record #2500)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 15999
Blurb: Bring them down to the ground.
Syntax: FRONTKICK <LEFT|RIGHT>
Abfile:
This straight kick may be targeted at either arm, arming to strike the
vulnerable nerve clusters just below the shoulder. The blow is powerful
enough that it will throw the target off their feet if it connects,
proning them.

Note that this ability along with all other Shikudo strikes and kicks
must be performed in a combination. See AB SHIKUDO COMBO for details.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
