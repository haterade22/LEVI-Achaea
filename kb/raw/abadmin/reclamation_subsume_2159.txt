Ability: Subsume (Record #2159)
Associated skill: Reclamation
Skillpoints required: 43999
Blurb: All will become one.
Syntax: RECLAMATION SUBSUME
Abfile:
This powerful ability is situationally one of the greatest in the
reclaimer's arsenal. If your reclaimed land meets another's (two rooms
become adjacent to one another) and the reclaimer of the other land is a
mutual ally, you may subsume his or her land and make it part of your
own network.

To do this, you must stand on the room that is adjacent to your network
that they have reclaimed and perform the ability.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 7.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 1000 mana
