Ability: Mudslide (Record #3144)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 34999

Summon up a sticky tide of mud to knock your foe sprawling and leave
them covered in sludge.

knocking the target prone.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 90 mana
