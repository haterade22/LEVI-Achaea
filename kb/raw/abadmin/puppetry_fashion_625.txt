Ability: Fashion (Record #625)
Associated skill: Puppetry
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Fashion puppets from wood.
Syntax: FASHION PUPPET OF <target>
PUPPET UNRAVEL <target>
Extra info: Initial fashion cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance, 50 mana, and 2 Wood
Standard fashion cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance, 50 mana
Unravel cooldown: 10.00 seconds of balance, 750 mana
Abfile:
 Requires: 2 Wood

In order to fashion a puppet, you first need to get yourself 2 wood
commodities. After you fashion the first time, you will have for
yourself a crude humanoid puppet. In order to make it actually useful,
you must then wield the puppet, and FASHION PUPPET OF <target>.

As you fashion your puppet of an adventurer over and over, you'll notice
that it gets closer and closer to being a semi-living puppet. Further,
the more fashions you have with your puppet, the more Puppetry powers
will be able to be channeled through the puppet.

Additionally, your puppet will decay at a rate of 50% every 6 minutes
after the last fashion. This effect does not occur for puppets of allied
adventurers.

If you find yourself in need of destroying a puppet that is no longer in
your possession, you may unravel it from afar. Note that this is a
difficult feat, and will result in being thrown off balance for a long
period of time.
