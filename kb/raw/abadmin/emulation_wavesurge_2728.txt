Ability: Wavesurge (Record #2728)
Associated skill: Emulation
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: The might of the raging waters.
Syntax: ENACT WAVESURGE
Abfile:
Call upon the might of the waves to carry you away. This shall throw you
between 1 and three rooms in a random direction.

There is a delay before you may call upon this after doing so
previously.
Useable on: self
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of equilibrium
