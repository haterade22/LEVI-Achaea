Ability: Transcend (Record #2723)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Perfect unity of the mind and body.
Syntax: PSI TRANSCEND [skill to use if full transcendence only]
Abfile:
By attaining a truly transcendent mind, you may operate your physical
and mental abilities in complete harmony(*). To attain this state of
mind you must exercise your body's potential while focusing on the
state of transcendence(**).

* Once your mind and body achieve full transcendence, you can perform a
psionics action while off equilibrium and for no incurred equilibrium
cost.

** Each time you perform a weaving-based attack while transcendence is
active you will increase how harmonised your mind and body are. A
failure to attack after some time will cause your state to regress.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
