Ability: Rampage (Record #2842)
Associated skill: Oppression
Skillpoints required: 23999
Blurb: Crush their hope.
Syntax: RAMPAGE AGAINST <target>
Abfile:
It is expected that the weak shall seek to flee your wrath. You may
rampage against these feckless cowards. If there are hands of the grave
in your location and in the target's location, your rampage shall
resolve immediately. If there are not, you shall rampage more swiftly
against one in an adjacent location, though anyone in your local area
cannot escape your reach.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: continent-wide
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 1
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.10 seconds of balance
