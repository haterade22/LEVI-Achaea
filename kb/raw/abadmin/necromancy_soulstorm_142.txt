Ability: Soulstorm (Record #142)
Associated skill: Necromancy
Skillpoints required: 29999
Blurb: Mark their souls with profane essence.
Syntax: SOULSTORM <target>
Abfile:
Unleash your necromantic essence in a deadly soulstorm, cruelly
assaulting the soul of your foe. This ability will attempt to force a
metamorphosised adventurer back into his mortal form.

Against denizens, you will instead profane their soul with malign
energy, causing them to take ten percent more damage from necromantic
abilities(*), the curses of evileye(**), and the weaponmastery attacks
of your fellow Infernals(***). In this case, the equilibrium and essence
costs of this dark art shall be greatly reduced.

* NECROMANCY DECAY and TAINT.
** EVILEYE BLEED.
*** All weapon-based attacks in the WEAPONMASTERY skill when performed
by an Infernal.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 1% life essence
