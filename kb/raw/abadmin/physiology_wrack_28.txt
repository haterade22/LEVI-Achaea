Ability: Wrack (Record #28)
Associated skill: Physiology
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: Afflict an opponent by assaulting the humours.
Syntax: WRACK <target> <humour|affliction>
Abfile:
By assaulting an opponent's humours, you can cause damage or afflict
upon them several common maladies. Each humour has several afflictions
and a damage type associated with it, and any afflictions from wracking
a humour will come only from this pool. When wracking, you may specify
the affliction you wish to use, or you may allow the affliction to
manifest at random at the cost of a slight delay in speed. It is
possible to wrack an opponent's untempered humour, though this is done
at a balance penalty and may be done only via specifying the affliction.

When used against denizens, wracking will deal damage with the type of
damage determined by the chosen humour.

Wracking operates outside of humour balance, and may be used
off-equilibrium immediately following a symbology ability when targeted
against an adventurer.

The humours and their affliction pools are:

Choleric: Nausea, sensitivity, slickness, (poison damage).
Melancholic: stupidity, anorexia, impatience, (cold damage).
Phlegmatic: clumsiness, weariness, asthma, (asphyxiation damage).
Sanguine: Haemophilia, recklessness, paralysis, (magic damage).
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 60 mana
