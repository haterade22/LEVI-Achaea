Abfile:
After building up white fire within you, you may turn your thermal
destruction on an opponent, causing him massive damage and possibly
instant death. BELCH will get rid of the flame prematurely.

* Kills if the target is below 40% of maximum health, this threshold
rising based on how badly they are on fire (50% at one stack of burning,
55% at two, 60% at three).
