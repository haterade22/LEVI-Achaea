Ability: Illusion (Record #1240)
Associated skill: Subterfuge
Skillpoints required: 23999
Blurb: Create an illusion of your choosing.
Syntax: CONJURE [target] ILLUSION <illusion>
CONJURE [target] ILLUSION LETHAL <illusion>
Abfile:
Create an illusion of your choosing in the room you are in, optionally
targeted at a specific person. You may conjure an illusion without
balance, but this will increase the equilibrium cost to 3.1 seconds. It
is also possible to bite someone immediately after conjuring your
illusion, before you have recovered equilibrium. You may embed a single
newline in your illusion by including the character sequence \n at the
appropriate spot. For limitations and restrictions on illusions, please
refer to HELP ILLUSIONS.
Useable on: adventurers and room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 25 mana
