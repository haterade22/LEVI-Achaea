Ability: Plundering (Record #1753)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Command your Swashbucklers to board a grappled ship to pilfer their goods.
Syntax: SHIP PLUNDER <full name/short name>
SHIP PLUNDER
Abfile:
This will send your crew of Swashbucklers to board an enemy ship
provided both vessels are adjacent, and one ship is grappling the other.
Be wary, as subsequent plunder attempts will put the defending
Swashbucklers on high alert, resulting in lower success rates and
increased danger to your invading crew.
