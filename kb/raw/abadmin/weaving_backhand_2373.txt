Ability: Backhand (Record #2373)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 33999
Blurb: His face was bad enough already.
Syntax: WEAVE BACKHAND <target>
Abfile:
Deliver a vicious backhanded blow with a mace, shattering teeth and
rattling your target. This attack delivers stupidity and dizziness, and
if the unfortunate individual is prone with either one of these
afflictions already, they will also be knocked off of focus balance.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
