Ability: Wreck (Record #2595)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: They shall break upon the rocks.
Syntax: MANIFEST WRECK <target>
Abfile:
Summon forth a vortex of water to dash a foe against the
ground. They must be prone or paralysed for this to be
possible, otherwise they shall evade the water's
clutches.

A target that is struck with this ability shall receive
stupidity and recklessness. If they have either affliction
prior to this, they shall also take damage(*).

* the damage is significantly reduced if the target is
not both prone and paralysed.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
