Ability: Track (Record #386)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 36999
Blurb: Traverse to and aerially follow someone.
Syntax: TRACK <target>
PURSUE <target>
Extra info: PURSUE: 1.00 seconds of equilibrium and 50 mana.
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
While flying, you will be able to fly to an adventurer very quickly using TRACK. PURSUE is a complementary ability, and allows you to aerially pursue someone. Your target must be on the ground, and you will follow him from the air while he moves.
Useable on: adventurers

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 mana
