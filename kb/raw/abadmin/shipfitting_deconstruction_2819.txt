Ability: Deconstruction (Record #2819)
Associated skill: Shipfitting
Skillpoints required: 59999
Blurb: Dismantle an entire ship.
Syntax: SHIPFIT DISMANTLE SHIP <ship>
Abfile:
For those wizened enough to wish to retire from the sea, you may
deconstruct a ship in its entirety, returning a portion of the
commodities used for construction.

This is not possible on a ship owned by an organisation, or on a ship
with artefacts, or for a ship outfitted with resetting equipment.
