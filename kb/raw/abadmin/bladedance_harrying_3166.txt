Ability: Harrying (Record #3166)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: The show is not over.
Syntax: DANCE HARRYING
Abfile:
The dance of the harrying is one focussed on swift blows of the rapier
to keep your enemy pinned within your deadly encirclement. Though these
blows typically do no real harm, the threat of your blades point will
make it difficult for even the fleetest foe to escape your lethal
performance.

*You may switch from harrying to another dance without consuming
balance.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
