As you grow closer to the transcendent being, so too does your hunger
grow. Once you are sufficiently mutated and focused on devouring all
you are able, anytime someone dies in your location you will consume
their corpse for a burst of health, if another does not get there
first...

active to do this.

*** This is indiscriminate. You will eat allies too if they die.
