Ability: Quake (Record #2529)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 10999
Blurb: The land shall tremble at your command.
Syntax: MANIFEST QUAKE
Abfile:
To anger a servant of Garash is to anger the earth itself. At your
bidding the earth shall violently quake, throwing all that stand within
your location to the ground and having a chance to disrupt their
balance.

If you have shaped your form more than once, you will lose one
level of shaping but this ability will not consume balance.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
