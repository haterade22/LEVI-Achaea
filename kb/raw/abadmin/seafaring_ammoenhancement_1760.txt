Ability: Ammoenhancement (Record #1760)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Improvements to ship ammunition.
Syntax: LOAD <weapon> WITH <ammo> [DIPPED]
Abfile:
Your mastery of ship munitions is such that you can now enhance certain
types of ammunition.
Star-shot, chain-shot, and shafts may be coated in a special liquid,
causing them to set aflame either the adventurers aboard the target
ship, the target ship's sails, or the target ship's deck, respectively.
Vats containing this rare liquid, called "dragon's tears", may be
obtained from an apothecary upon a certain isle.

Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
