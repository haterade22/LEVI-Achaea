Ability: Overdraw (Record #2783)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 29999
Blurb: A pragmatic price.
Syntax: MANIPULATE OVERDRAW
Abfile:
You may overdraw from the Wellspring when carrying out an act of
manipulation, substantiation, or alignment. Doing so is very taxing and
will cause you to be unable to tap the Wellspring for a time (passively
and actively). See the relevant abilities for the effects of overdrawing
in a given case.

* To overdraw, append the word OVERDRAW to the end of an action. For
instance, MANIPULATE SOLAR <target> OVERDRAW.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
