Ability: Perfection (Record #2936)
Associated skill: Shindo
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: Alone against the horde.
Syntax: SHIN PERFECTION
Abfile:
By enhancing your battle instincts with shin energy, they can become as
close to perfect as a mortal warrior can aspire to. Though this is of
little benefit when meeting foes in single combat, when facing armies
all but your primary adversary(*) shall find that your sense of danger
is so profound that any attacks they direct against your person may be
evaded outright(**).

* The first person to hit you will never miss. This resets every 5
seconds if they have not hit in that time.
** All subsequent single target attacks from other adventurers have a
chance to miss you outright. This does not work against ranged
abilities, and you must be relatively unhindered for it to work.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 40 Shin energy
