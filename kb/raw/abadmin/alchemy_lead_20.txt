Ability: Lead (Record #20)
Associated skill: Alchemy
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Density from the lowest of the metals.
Syntax: EDUCE LEAD
EDUCE LEAD <target>
Extra info: Symbol: Two concentric circles.
Abfile:
Calling upon the energy of lead, the lowest of the alchemical metals,
will grant great density to your body.

You may also target this at another adventurer flying in the sky above
you, dragging them back to earth by drastically increasing their weight
for a short time and briefly knocking them off balance.

Use without a target incurs a small equilibrium loss.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 150 mana
