Ability: Benediction (Record #2775)
Associated skill: Zeal
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: A holy blessing to protect the righteous.
Syntax: RECITE BENEDICTION <target>
Abfile:
This holy benediction shall grant all of the blessings gained from
reciting the verses of fire, void, protection, will and endurance to an
individual in your location. Due to the nature of this supplication, it
is not considered a holy verse and does not compose any aspect of a
prayer.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
