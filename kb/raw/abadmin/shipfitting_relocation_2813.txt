Ability: Relocation (Record #2813)
Associated skill: Shipfitting
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Movement of equipment and weapons.
Syntax: SHIPFIT RELOCATE <equipment>
Abfile:
For the foolish, the forgetful, or the indecisive, you may have a ship's
crew assist you in the relocation of a weapon or piece of equipment that
has already been constructed. Uprooting equipment or weapons in such a
manner will cause minor damage to the ship, which will take one shipwood
and one shipiron to repair.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
