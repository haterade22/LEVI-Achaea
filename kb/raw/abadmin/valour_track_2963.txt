Ability: Track (Record #2963)
Associated skill: Valour
Skillpoints required: 38999
Blurb: There is no hiding from the light.
Syntax: EAGLE TRACK <target>
Abfile:
This ability is an improved version of the follow ability. With track,
even if the eagle temporarily loses its target, because the target went
indoors, it will continue to follow the target once the target comes
back outside.
