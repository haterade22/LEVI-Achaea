Ability: Hound (Record #3001)
Associated skill: Dominion
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: Your loyal nightmare.
Syntax: HOUND
Abfile:
The hungering hounds of Lycantha are a gift to each of the Unnamables of
Ashtan, part of the great pact between Imperator Glaaki and the Seat of
Chaos.

Your hound comes to you with assorted statistics which have been
randomly assigned.

If you cause your hound to die, you will be refused another hound.

There is absolutely no excuse for killing or conspiring to kill your
hound.

Below is a list of commands that may be used once you have gotten your
hound.
HOUND ASSESS    : See information about your hound.
HOUND RECALL : Recall your hound to your side.
HOUND SANCTUARY : Order your hound to a sanctuary where it will be safe.
HOUND TRAIN : Order your hound to go train, in order to raise a stat.
HOUND REPORT : Gain information on where your hound is and who they
are tracking and attacking.
HOUND REPORT LOCATION : This will tell you the location of your loyal
(this will incur an equilibrium cost.)
HOUND SLAY      : Order your hound to slay someone.
HOUND HUNT : When your hound is hungry, have it go hunt for its food.
HOUND FLEE : Set the amount of health remaining when your hound will
flee battle.
HOUND SLEEP     : Order your hound to sleep.
HOUND FEED RAT  : Feed the corpses of rats to your hound.

As your hound gains in
* Stamina:  its health will increase.
* Speed: it will attack more quickly as well as move from place to place
* Strength: it will attack with more power.

You will gain other abilities later on that are related to your hound.

You must visit Lycantha on the Chaos Plane to request your hound.
Useable on: none
