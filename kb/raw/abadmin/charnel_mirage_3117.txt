Ability: Mirage (Record #3117)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Trust not your eyes or flesh.
Syntax: ACCURSED MIRAGE
Abfile:
This high art of the Accursed allows you to breathe forth your poisonous
nature upon the location, for a short time(*) bending the rules which
govern the wounds wrought upon the mortal man. In practice, this means
that if someone strikes another adventurer, for a brief moment after
said attacker will find they cannot inflict any lasting harm upon anyone
else.

* This lasts thirty seconds and has a five minute cooldown.
** For two seconds after you strike someone directly or otherwise (and
for damage that isn't trivial), you will find that you can inflict no
damage to anyone but that target while the mirage is active.
*** Other Accursed are immune to the implications of the mirage, and
unblockable damage bypasses its unholy nature entirely.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.80 seconds of equilibrium
