Ability: Glacial (Record #2043)
Associated skill: Artificing
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: There is cold, then there is Cold.
Syntax: WORKSHOP REFINE GLACIAL CRYSTAL
Abfile:
When set into your staff, this crystal will enhance your control over
your spell of horripilation, allowing you to bleed off the excess water
to bind your target with bonds of water, making it hard for them to
leave the location for a time(*).

* The duration of the bonds is highly variable. If they are frozen, they
shall last for 45 seconds. If they are only shivering, they will last
for 35 seconds. If they are neither shivering nor frozen but lacking the
insulation defence, they will last 25 seconds. Otherwise, they will only
last 15 seconds.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
