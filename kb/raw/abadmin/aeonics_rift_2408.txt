Ability: Rift (Record #2408)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: Distance is no barrier.
Syntax: CHRONO RIFT <target> [BOOST]
Abfile:
This ability will allow you to open a rift to serve as a portal between
you and another adventurer. Note that there is a delay between you
creating the rift and it fully manifesting; a monolith at either end
shall prevent the rift from being opened.

Note that if you boost the rift, a monolith on your end of the attempt
shall not prevent it from opening, though one on the target's end shall
still.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 140 age
