Ability: Dissonance (Record #774)
Associated skill: Crystalism
Skillpoints required: 46999
Blurb: Cause your enemies to become dissonant.
Syntax: EMBED DISSONANCE
Abfile:
 Shapes required: Cylinder, Sphere, Spiral

Every person vibrates with a certain fundamental frequency. This
vibration will cause that fundamental frequency to be disturbed, and for
those under its influence to begin vibrating dissonantly. Those that
vibrate dissonantly will become more and more vulnerable to the workings
of sorcery as their traditional defences against it become degraded(*).

* Each time you vibrate dissonantly your elemental and magical
resistance is degraded for as long as the affliction persists upon your
person.
** Dissonance is a special affliction, and is always the last goldenseal
affliction to be cured.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 300 mana
