Ability: Cooking (Record #1746)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 35999
Blurb: Improve crew morale and performance culinarily.
Syntax: COOK <fish>
Abfile:
Prepare any caught or killed fish and store them in the ship's larder.
Doing this will increase both your ship's food supply and the crew's
morale.

Note those with at least rank 3 in the deckhand specialisation shall
cook good food instead of merely common.

Requires legs: 0
