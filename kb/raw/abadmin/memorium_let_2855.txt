Ability: Let (Record #2855)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 299
Blurb: A crude but necessary step.
Syntax: CRUX LET <target|ME>
Abfile:
Collecting the blood of your foe is the first step to overcoming them.
Your ritual blade can retain the blood of one you let for later
workings; you may also let your own blood, either for emergencies or
specialised rituals.

Probing your ritual blade shall show whose blood it currently retains,
if any.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
