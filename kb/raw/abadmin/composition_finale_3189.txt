Ability: Finale (Record #3189)
Associated skill: Composition
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: The curtain falls.
Syntax: FINALE <target>
Abfile:
If a crescendo has built sufficiently about a target(*), you may bring
your ongoing performance to a grand finale. This ends your current
performance.

* Five stacks of crescendo.

Finales:

Paean: gives the reeling affliction for 20 seconds, which causes all
bladedance attacks to be performed from the back position until it
fades.

Bhajan: Strikes the hearer with both stupidity and epilepsy.
Additionally, if they are suffering from head trauma, they will lose
focus balance for a significant period of time.

Nomos: mangles an already level two broken leg. Prioritises the left
over the right.

Gusheh: mangles an already level two broken arm. Prioritises the left
over the right.

Ode: your foe will be rendered unable to communicate by any means for
five minutes.

Ghazal: causes your foe to succumb to a love-wrought madness for thirty
seconds. While succumbed to said madness, they will be unable to
perceive the state of themselves (similar to recklessness) and all
physical damage shall become unblockable.

Elegy: any blessing of the stars protecting your target will be removed,
and any starburst tattoos upon their person will be removed.

Prosodion: transfixes the hearer with the power of the Accentato, making
earworm incurable for twenty seconds.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of equilibrium
