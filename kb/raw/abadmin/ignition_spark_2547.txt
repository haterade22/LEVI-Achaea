Ability: Spark (Record #2547)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: The smallest ember can burn the world.
Syntax:
Abfile:
Every lord of Kkractle's domain possesses a primal spark at the core of
their being. This spark is quiescent much of the time; but these lords
tend to be swift to anger and swifter to dispense their own brand of
justice. When driven to battle the spark shall rage, and as it burns
brighter so to shall the elemental lord, and so shall the lord's power
grow.

As you carry out hostile attacks against your enemies the spark shall be
fanned brighter, though if you go without performing a hostile act for a
short while it shall slowly decay.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
