Ability: Petrifying (Record #1804)
Associated skill: Formulation
Skillpoints required: 10000
Blurb: Partial petrification comes with many advantages.
Syntax: IMBIBE PETRIFYING
Extra info: Reagents: 2 ferrum, 1 plumbum
Abfile:
This compound alters the physiology of the alchemist. By partially
turning one's bones into a dense, iron-like substance, the user shall
find themselves slowed but more able to resist physical damage. However,
they should be wary of attacks that draw their power from lightning, as
they will be particularly vulnerable to such.

If the compound is highly volatile, its effects shall expire after five
minutes.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
