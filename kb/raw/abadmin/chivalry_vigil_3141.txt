Ability: Vigil (Record #3141)
Associated skill: Chivalry
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Ever watchful.
Syntax: STAND VIGIL
Abfile:
Before even the Ur'Vampires there existed an honour guard, unliving
custodians born to protect Finality's child. Through mortal emulation of
their duty without end was Chivalry first enkindled, echoing the ancient
vigil of the Death Knights.

When standing vigil, you will keep the keenest of eyes on your immediate
surroundings(*), though this will take much of your focus(**).


* You will gain the equivalent of skywatch, treewatch, groundwatch, and
the lifevision defences for most purposes.
** This is a channeled action.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
