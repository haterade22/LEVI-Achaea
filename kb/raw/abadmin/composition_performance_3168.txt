Ability: Performance (Record #3168)
Associated skill: Composition
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: The fundamentals of the stage.
Syntax: COMPOSE <song> [movement]
PERFORMANCE SHOW
PERFORMANCE END
Abfile:
The fundamental of the performer, what more critical piece of the
undertaking can there be than the beginning? Performances are split into
two parts:

Songs: these strike all your enemies in your location periodically in
some manner dependent on the type of song you are playing.

Movements: these passively benefit all of your allies in your location
in some way as long as your song is playing.

When starting out, your performance can only contain one movement, but
as your skill grows, so too will the scope of your performance.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
