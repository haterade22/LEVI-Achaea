Ability: Pacify (Record #922)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Soothe all violent thoughts from your subject's mind.
Syntax: MIND PACIFY
Abfile:
By drawing a soft cloak of telepathic power over the mind of a locked adventurer, you are able to make offensive actions abhorrent to him. This will last for a short, random amount of time, or until an offensive act is directed at him.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 200 mana
