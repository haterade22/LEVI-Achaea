Ability: Talon (Record #2809)
Associated skill: Tattoos
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Bypass protective wards.
Syntax: TOUCH TALON <target>
Abfile:
This tattoo has but one charge and requires a full 20 black inks to
place upon your skin, but it shall allow you to pull an individual from
behind the protective wards of a house(*). The process takes some time,
but the only means by which it may be avoided is for your target to
leave the dwelling in question(**).

* House in this context refers to player housing, not the house
organisations.
** If the target is in a bed during the initiation and termination of
this process, the talon tattoo will instead move them to outside that
bed. The talon is not wiped in this case.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
