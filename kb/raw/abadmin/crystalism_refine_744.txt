Ability: Refine (Record #744)
Associated skill: Crystalism
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Refine a piece of crystal out of the Master.
Syntax: REFINE [1-10] <shape>
Abfile:
This ability must be done in the presence of the Master Crystal. It will refine one of the possible crystal shapes from the Master, reducing the Master Crystal by one facet.

Possible shapes are:

Pyramid         Torus          Pentagon
Cylinder        Cube           Diamond
Sphere          Polyhedron     Spiral
Egg             Disc

In order to replenish the facets of the Master Crystal, you must find pearls, and FOLD PEARL INTO CRYSTAL.

Ability range: none

Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 50 mana
