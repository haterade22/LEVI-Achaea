Ability: Enervate (Record #954)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 6999
Blurb: Suck the strength from your enemy's aura.
Syntax: ENERVATE <target>
Abfile:
Using the power of your aura, you can send forth a tendril and suck out
the energy from the aura of another, thereby draining another's mental
energy to add to your own. If your subject is afflicted with manaleech
or they are frozen, the amount of their mental strength you drain will
be increased.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 50 mana
