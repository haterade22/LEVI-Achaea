Ability: Doublewhirl (Record #2098)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 8000
Blurb: Strike twice as fast.
Syntax: DOUBLEWHIRL <target> [limb1] [limb2]
Extra info: Two flails or morningstars.
Abfile:
You have reached a point in your training where you may now utilise two
weapons at once to attack. Your skill is such that you may target each
separate attack to a different limb of your target. When you strike an
already damaged limb of an opponent with this attack, that limb will
gain a fracture(*).

* Excluding the legs.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance cost varies based on weapon speed
Skill specialisation: knight_dualblunt
