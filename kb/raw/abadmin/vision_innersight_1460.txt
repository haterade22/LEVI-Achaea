Ability: Innersight (Record #1460)
Associated skill: Vision
Skillpoints required: 57999
Blurb: Sense your experience more accurately.
Syntax: SCORE
Abfile:
With Innersight, you see your level of experience to a higher degree of
accuracy when reviewing your SCORE. This is further increased while you
have set CONFIG SHOWXPDROPS ON.

In addition, adventurers with classes that use life essence, karma, or
holy wrath will be able to determine the value of those reserves to a
finer degree.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
