Ability: Chorale (Record #1668)
Associated skill: Harmonics
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: A group harmonic that gives experience to the listeners.
Syntax: PLAY CHORALE
PLAY CHORALE CRESCENDO
Abfile:
From four to eight bards must combine in the Chorale. One plays, then each join in (same command in each case) and then when one decides that the time is right, performs the crescendo with PLAY CHORALE CRESCENDO. The crescendo grants experience to everyone hearing. The amount of experience varies with the number of bards in the Chorale. One person may only receive the benefits of hearing a Chorale once per Achaean month.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.60 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 400 mana
