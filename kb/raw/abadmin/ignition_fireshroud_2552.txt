Ability: Fireshroud (Record #2552)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 1999
Blurb: Wreathe yourself in Kkractle's grace.
Syntax: IGNITE SHROUD
Abfile:
Wreathe yourself in Kkractle's fiery grace, granting yourself increased
resistance. In addition, your fires shall leap out at any that raise
their hand against you and damage them, though this will naturally be
ineffective against fellow servants of the Primal Fire.

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
