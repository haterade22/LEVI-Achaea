Ability: Undercut (Record #2480)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 35999
Blurb: Take them down to the ground.
Syntax: UNDERCUT <target> <LEFT|RIGHT LEG>
Abfile:
Swing at a leg of your target with a battleaxe. If the leg breaks,
you'll take the opportunity to drive your target to the ground. If it
does not break the limb, the balance on this attack is significantly
increased.

This will always break the limb when it is above 90% damaged, so long as
you are proficient with the Battleaxe.

Note that this attack does not deliver a venom.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Skill specialisation: knight_dualcutting
