Ability: Decapitate (Record #1466)
Associated skill: Weaponry
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: Hack the head from an adventurer's corpse.
Syntax: DECAPITATE <corpse>
PIKE <head>
Abfile:
Provided you have an edged weapon in one of your hands, you will be able to decapitate a corpse. You may then do whatever you like with the head, including buying a pike and then PIKEing the head. Note that heads, being organic matter, do not tend to stick around too long, and if put on a pike, the pike will also decay away when the head does.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
