Ability: Calcify (Record #2543)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 57999
Blurb: From dust to dust.
Syntax: TERRAN CALCIFY <target> HEAD|TORSO
Abfile:
The dreaded power of calcification is one limited to the servants of
Garash. By laying hands upon the head or torso of your enemy, you may
begin the transmutation of the calcium in their bones into true stone.
This will cause their head or torso to be damaged after a short time.
For instance, someone with no internal damage would receive mild trauma
upon calcification of the torso completing, and someone with mild trauma
when it completed would receive serious internal trauma.

However, if the limb is at the maximum damage possible (mangled head or
serious internal trauma), alternative effects occur.

If calcification completes against someone with a mangled head, their
skull shall completely transmute to stone, killing them instantly.

Note that if you have shaped your form as much as possible, the balance
on this ability is completely removed, though it will reduce your
shaping by two levels on use.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
