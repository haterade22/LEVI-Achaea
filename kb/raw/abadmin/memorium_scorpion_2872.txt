Ability: Scorpion (Record #2872)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: The striking predator of the sands.
Syntax: TRACE SCORPION <target>
Abfile:
This logograph is unique in that it takes more power to imbue than blood
alone can provide. For this reason, it requires some preparation: it can
only be traced as the final logograph in your epitaph, and said epitaph
must be sufficiently long(*). However, such preparation comes with
expected results: the scorpion shall inflict your foe with a terrible
curse, thinning their blood(**). Notably, this logograph is so complex
that it does not suffer alterations of any kind: you cannot utilise the
scorpion with the heartbeat ability.

* At least a length of four.
** Gives the thinned blood affliction (similar to the scytherus venom).
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
