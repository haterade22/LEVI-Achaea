Ability: Whirl (Record #2096)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 2000
Blurb: The most basic of blunt attacks.
Syntax: WHIRL <target> [limb]
Extra info: Either a flail or a morningstar.
Abfile:
This ability delivers a single, powerful blow with your chosen weapon.
When striking a limb that is already damaged to the point of needing a
restoration salve, it will deliver a fracture to that limb(*).

* Excluding the legs.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance cost varies based on weapon speed
Skill specialisation: knight_dualblunt
