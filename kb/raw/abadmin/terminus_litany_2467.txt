Ability: Litany (Record #2467)
Associated skill: Terminus
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: A power out of legend.
Syntax: INTONE LITANY <delay> <command>
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
One of the most feared powers of the Tsol'teth Overlords was their
ability to bend an entire battlefield to their will with nothing but the
power of their voice. After mastering this research tree, that power is
now yours.

When you utter the litany and specify the command that you wish to
dictate, all those that stand in your location or adjacent to it shall
be forced to carry out that command(*).

* The delay must be no less than 4 (seconds) and no longer than 10
(seconds).
* The delay time determines the windup time.
* This ability both has a windup time and informs both your location and
those adjacent when you begin to initiate it.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 9.00 seconds of word balance
