Ability: Latency (Record #2891)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: A bleak future awaits.
Syntax: SWARM LATENCY <swarm>
Abfile:
Your myriad minions are undoubtedly deadly when used overtly, but there
are more subtle workings you may also employ. Once the defences of your
foe have been breached(*), and they are sufficiently vulnerable(**), you
may utilise your minions as a vector through which to ravage your foe
internally with your sorcery(***).

* A target who possesses three of: pyramides, flushings, rebbies,
mycalium, and sandfever.
** You must have a swarm burrowed into your target.
*** Their body will be unable to process immunity elixirs for thirteen
seconds after your arcane strike.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
