Ability: Effervescence (Record #2624)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: An insidious vapour to lay low your foes.
Syntax: MANIFEST EFFERVESCENCE
Abfile:
You may manifest a vapour that will linger in a location for a time,
periodically robbing your foes of breath. Notably, holding one's breath
is only a partial defence against this sinister gift of Whiirh.

Useable on: adventurers and room

Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
