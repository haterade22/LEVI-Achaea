Ability: Infusefire (Record #242)
Associated skill: TwoArts
Skillpoints required: 12999
Blurb: Wreathe your blade in crackling flame.
Syntax: INFUSE FIRE
Abfile:
By channelling accumulated Shin energy into your sword, you may heat
your blade to a scorching temperature. A weapon so empowered will burn
as fire the next time it is drawn, potentially setting your target
ablaze while also afflicting them with weariness.
If a target is already ablaze, extra damage may be dealt.
** Your blade may only be infused with one element at a time.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 5 Shin energy
