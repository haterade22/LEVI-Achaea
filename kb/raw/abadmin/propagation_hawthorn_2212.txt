Ability: Hawthorn (Record #2212)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 54999
Blurb: A plant that grows in hilly locations.
Syntax: PROPAGATE <HEAD/BODY/ARMS/LEGS> WITH HAWTHORN
Abfile:
When propagated, this plant conveys the following bonuses:

Head: You can shout longer messages without passing out from the strain.
Body: When eating the hawthorn plant, you will gain the deafness defence
faster than usual.
Arms: Your thornrend attack will deliver the sensitivity affliction if
deafness is not present; otherwise it shall strip the deafness defence.
Legs: Move faster through hilly locations.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
