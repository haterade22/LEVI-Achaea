Ability: Guide (Record #3280)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 49999
Blurb: You need but show them the way.
Syntax: ACCURSED GUIDE <target>
Abfile:
Sometimes there are those of ambition that simply need a guiding hand,
to be shown a path to transcendence beneath the eternal sands. While
standing in your ritual chamber, you may send forth a portal to such an
individual, though it is taxing like all worthy endeavours, and requires
all of your focus.

* This creates a portal to someone on the same continent as you, and is
a channel. The portal is two-way, beware!

Ability range: INVALID

Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
