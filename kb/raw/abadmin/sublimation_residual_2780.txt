Ability: Residual (Record #2780)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Waste not even the scraps.
Syntax:
Abfile:
Those that have dedicated themselves to the study of the Wellspring
shall find that specialisation comes at a cost. With their focus upon
the various schools of Sublimation, their grasp of the biological
sciences falters, reducing their ability to utilise the physiology
inundate ability substantially, as follows:
* Choleric, melancholic, and sanguine's effects are halved.
* Phlegmatic will no longer give weariness at all, and the progression
is changed to lethargy, anorexia, slickness at 2, 6, and 8 tempers
respectively.

However, such a loss is a minor inconvenience at best. After learning to
utilise residual energy in their everyday lives, Alchemists may empower
their wrack and truewrack attacks to give the following passive
benefits:
* Choleric: wrack and truewrack attempts will deal unblockable damage.
* Melancholic: wrack and truewrack attempts shall deal mana damage.
* Sanguine: wrack and truewrack attempts shall incur bleeding.
* Phlegmatic: above two tempers, your truewrack attempts will no longer
miss due to clumsiness. Above five tempers, your truewrack attempts will
have a reduced balance cost (increasing for each stack above five).

Note that all residual effects scale to the number of tempers of the
relevant humour present on the target.

NOTE: should your connection to the Wellspring be severed, the passive
benefits will also no longer function.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
