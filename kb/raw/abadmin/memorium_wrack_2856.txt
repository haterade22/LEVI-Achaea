Ability: Wrack (Record #2856)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: A basic application of the craft.
Syntax: BLOOD WRACK <target>
Abfile:
A basic application of your arcane craft: wrack the blood in the veins
of your foe, and they will suffer(*). The suffering is increased for
each of the plagues of pestilence ravaging your target.

* Does damage.
** Sanguinehand will activate off of this ability.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
