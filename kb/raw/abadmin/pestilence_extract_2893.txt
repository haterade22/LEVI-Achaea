Ability: Extract (Record #2893)
Associated skill: Pestilence
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: Call them home post haste.
Syntax: SWARM EXTRACT <swarm>
Abfile:
If one of your swarms has burrowed into a victim, you may call them
urgently to return with predictably bloody results. A swarm ripping
itself free in this manner will cause blood to splatter the location(*),
bodily harm, and convey any plague it is carrying.

* See AB MEMORIUM SANGUINEHAND.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
