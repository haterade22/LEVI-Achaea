Ability: Consume (Record #3133)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: To snuff the candle.
Syntax: SOULBLEED CONSUME <target>
Abfile:
It is time for the living to die. If you have wounded someone enough(*)
you may consume what is left of their life, ending it in an instant.

* For the purposes of this skill, sufficiently wounded means they either
have 15 grievous wounds OR are under 50% of their maximum health.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 2
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
