Ability: Eagle (Record #2941)
Associated skill: Valour
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: Your loyal companion.
Syntax: EAGLE
Abfile:
The golden eagles serve only those loyal to the Bloodsworn divine,
forming a lifelong partnership with a paladin in their struggle against
the forces of Evil, Chaos, and Darkness.

Your eagle comes to you with assorted statistics which have been
randomly assigned.

If you cause your eagle to die, you will be refused another eagle.

There is absolutely no excuse for killing or conspiring to kill your
eagle.

Below is a list of commands that may be used once you have gotten your
eagle.
EAGLE ASSESS    : See information about your eagle.
EAGLE RECALL : Recall your eagle to your shoulder.
EAGLE SANCTUARY : Order your eagle to a sanctuary where it will be safe.
EAGLE LAND      : Order your eagle to land on the ground.
EAGLE CIRCLE    : Order your eagle to fly up into the skies.
EAGLE TRAIN : Order your eagle to go train, in order to raise a stat.
EAGLE REPORT : Gain information on where your eagle is and who they
are tracking and attacking.
EAGLE REPORT LOCATION : This will tell you the location of your loyal
(this will incur an equilibrium cost.)
EAGLE SLAY      : Order your eagle to slay someone.
EAGLE HUNT : When your eagle is hungry, have it go hunt for its food.
EAGLE FLEE : Set the amount of health remaining when your eagle will
flee battle.
EAGLE SLEEP     : Order your eagle to sleep.
EAGLE FEED RAT  : Feed the corpses of rats to your eagle.

As your eagle gains in
* Stamina:  its health will increase.
* Speed: it will attack more quickly as well as fly from place to place
* Strength: it will attack with more power.

You will gain other abilities later on that are related to your eagle.

EAGLE REQUEST: You must visit Anost in the excised Ruins of the
Dawnspear in order to request your eagle.
