Ability: Lightsteal (Record #2366)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 13999
Blurb: Darkness falls.
Syntax: WEAVE LIGHTSTEAL <target>
Abfile:
Using a dagger, slash your target across the eyes, blinding them(*). The
length of this blindness is increased if they're upon the ground, and
they will also suffer a short blackout in this instance.

* Delivers the true blind affliction.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
