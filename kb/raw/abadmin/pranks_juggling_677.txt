Ability: Juggling (Record #677)
Associated skill: Pranks
Skillpoints required: 16999
Blurb: The ancient art of juggling.
Syntax: JUGGLE <item1> [item2] [item3]
JUGGLED
JUGGLE STOP
Abfile:
You may juggle up to two items at once, or three if you have reached
Transcendent pranks. Once you are juggling, you may throw the items
you're juggling at a greatly decreased cost in balance. JUGGLED will
show you what, if anything, you are juggling. Be aware that juggling
comes with an endurance drain and, no matter how nimble your fingers,
you are not adept enough to juggle bombs.

NOTE: the more items you attempt to juggle at once, the greater the cost
in balance.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
