Ability: Emanation (Record #3148)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: The elements unleashed.
Syntax: CAST EMANATION AT <target> <AIR|EARTH|FIRE|WATER>
Abfile:
This higher order spell will allow you, when you are majorly resonant
with one of the Elemental Planes, to unleash all of your accumulated
power in a single devastating strike. This clears any resonance you have
with the given Plane, and each type has a various effect:

Air: your target will be struck down with paralysis and dizziness, as
well as being stunned for a short time. If they already have dizziness,
they will be given asthma instead.

Earth: your target's torso will become calcified. Additionally, if cast
against someone with serious internal trauma, your foe shall be slain

Fire: your target will be given two stacks of the ablaze affliction.

Water: your target will be given a level of freeze (or have the
insulation defence stripped), and will be disrupted along with being
afflicted with stuttering.

All emanations deal damage.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.40 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 100 mana
