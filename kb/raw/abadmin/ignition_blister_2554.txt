Ability: Blister (Record #2554)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: They shall burn beneath your touch.
Syntax: IGNITE SKIN OF <target>
Abfile:
Sear the skin of your enemy to such an extent that horrific blisters
form. These blisters shall make every movement agony, delaying your
foe's movement between rooms and causing them pain on each successful
time they escape you(*).

* There is a cooldown on a person taking damage from successfully moving
with blistered, though there is no cooldown on them being delayed on
each movement.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
