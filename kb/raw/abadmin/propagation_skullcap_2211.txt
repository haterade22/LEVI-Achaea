Ability: Skullcap (Record #2211)
Associated skill: Propagation
Skillpoints required: 45999
Blurb: A plant that grows in the harsher environments.
Syntax: PROPAGATE <HEAD/BODY/ARMS/LEGS> WITH SKULLCAP
Abfile:
When propagated, this plant conveys the following bonuses:

Head: You will be able to see when adventurers fall in battle as if you
had the deathsight defence.
Body: You will recover more quickly from smoking the skullcap plant.
Arms: Your thornrend attacks will funnel power into an arcane storm if
one is present (requires feedback on your target) OR bypass the shield
defence against denizens(*).
Legs: Move more swiftly through deserts.

* Your thornrend attacks will bypass, but not strip, the magical shield
defence on denizens.

Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
