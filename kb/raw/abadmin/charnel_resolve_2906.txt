Ability: Resolve (Record #2906)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 34999
Blurb: Pain is an old adversary.
Syntax: ACCURSED RESOLVE
Abfile:
The tribulation of the Accursed is to suffer long after others would
not. Yet those that survive resolve to fight on through wounds that
would slay an ordinary man or woman; while suffused with your Accursed
resolve the damage you suffer from bleeding will be halved, and will
never slay you outright(*).

* The actual amount of bleeding you receive is unmodified, just the
damage taken from the periodic ticks.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.40 seconds of equilibrium
