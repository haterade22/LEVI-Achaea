Ability: Entwine (Record #2364)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: What a tangled web.
Syntax: WEAVE ENTWINE <target>
Abfile:
Using a whip, you may attempt to lash it about your target, entangling
them. This only works against a target who is upon the ground; if
they're still standing, you will tangle their feet and bring them down
to the ground instead.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.30 seconds of balance
