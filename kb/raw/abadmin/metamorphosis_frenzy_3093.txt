Ability: Frenzy (Record #3093)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: By stinger and claw.
Syntax: FRENZY <target> <sting venom> <claw venom> [limb]
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Unleash a frenzy at your prey, first plunging your venom-filled stinger
into them before ripping at them with your claws.

You may sting with: darkshade, vardrax, euphorbia, kalmia, or sumac(*).
You must have a vial of venom on hand to envenom your claws with.

* You may only sting with sumac every so often: your venom sacs have a
limited amount and take time to refill after each use.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
Skill specialisation: meta_druid
