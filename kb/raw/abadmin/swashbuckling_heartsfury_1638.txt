Ability: Heartsfury (Record #1638)
Associated skill: Swashbuckling
Skillpoints required: 42999
Blurb: An aggressive stance that gives a chance of counterattack.
Syntax: HEARTSFURY
UNSTANCE/STANCE NONE
Abfile:
Enter the stance of heart's fury, focussing on your assault no matter
that which would attempt to stop you(*).

There is a mana drain associated with this stance. To end the stance,
either switch to another stance, or select STANCE NONE or UNSTANCE.

* Your tune defence will provide more resistence against the blows of
denizens while in this stance, and you have an increased chance to
bypass movement hinder abilities such as gravehands or tentacles.
Useable on: denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.00 seconds of balance
Drain: Mana
