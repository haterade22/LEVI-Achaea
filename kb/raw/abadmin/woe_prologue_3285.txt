Ability: Prologue (Record #3285)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 59999
Blurb: Master the dramatic pause.
Syntax: RECITE PROLOGUE <saga>
Abfile:
Masters of storytelling gain the ability to delay the conclusion of a
tale for dramatic effect.

Using this ability will recite another tale up to its culmination, at
which point the completion will be delayed.

The tale may be completed for the next 5 minutes instantly at the cost
of 3 seconds of balance. This will incur a cooldown of 5 minutes. Not
all tales can be completed in this way.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 20.00 seconds of voice balance
