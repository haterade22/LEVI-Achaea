Ability: Seaspells (Record #1748)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 18999
Blurb: Invest a ship's consecrated figurehead with powerful Seaspells.
Syntax: SEASPELLS LIST
SEASPELLS INVEST <spell>
SEASPELLS UNINVEST <spell>
SEASPELLS INVESTED
INVOKE <spell>
Abfile:
SEASPELLS LIST will allow you to peruse the Seaspells available while on
a voyage. If you are the captain of a vessel with a consecrated
figurehead mounted, while you are in port at a harbour you may SEASPELL
INVEST up to four Seaspells for use in your travels. Note that only one
of Shipcloak and Wavecall may be invested, and only one of Shield or
Rainstorm may be invested. You may also use SEASPELLS UNINVEST to remove
a Seaspells to make room for others. Once at sea you may INVOKE the
invested Seaspells. Finally at any time you may show the SEASPELLS
INVESTED to see which Seaspells are available in your figurehead. You
may TOUCH FIGUREHEAD to identify any active Seaspells while at sea.

Shipcloak: INVOKE CLOAK, DISSOLVE CLOAK
Power Cost: 250
A seaspell to surround your ship in a cloak of darkness from other
ships. Flares shot within a certain distance of your ship will bring
your ship to light, however. Shooting a flare from your own ship while
cloaked will also dissolve your cloaking.

Wavecall:  INVOKE WAVECALL <direction> <distance between 1-8>
Power Cost: Varies depending on speed.
A seaspell to summon a wave to propel your ship a great distance,
instantaneously, at a great cost in personal endurance, hull damage, as
well as figurehead power. All costs increase with greater distance.
Personnel on other ships nearby with SHIPWARNING active will be informed
of the direction in which a ship wavecalls away.

Shield: INVOKE SHIELD
Power Cost: 200
A seaspell to weave a shield around your ship to protect it from damage
attacks. Can be shattered by a wavescythe seaspell.

Rainstorm:  INVOKE RAINSTORM
Power Cost: 50
While shipmelded, you can invoke this seaspell to douse all deck and
sails fires at once.

Barrier:  INVOKE BARRIER
DISSOLVE BARRIER
Power Cost: 300
A seaspell to invoke prismatic barriers around all those upon your ship.
Be careful: each and every barrier raised once this seaspell is active
drains power from the figurehead, until you DISSOLVE BARRIER.

Windboost:  INVOKE WINDBOOST
Power Cost: 150
A seaspell to temporarily boost your ship's speed.

Whirlpool:  INVOKE WHIRLPOOL <dir>
Power Cost: 200
You may call upon this Seaspell to summon temporary whirlpools around
your ship, which make it difficult for vessels to enter or leave that
spot of the ocean.

Wavescythe:  INVOKE WAVESCYTHE <target ship>
Power Cost: 200
A seaspell to shatter the magical shield of a ship within a small
radius.

Hunger: INVOKE HUNGER <target seamonster>
Power Cost: 300
A seaspell that will cast a spectral net upon a target seamonster. If
the seamonster dies before the net disappears, the casting figurehead
will be replenished a significant amount of power.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
