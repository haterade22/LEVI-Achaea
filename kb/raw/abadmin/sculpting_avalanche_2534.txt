Ability: Avalanche (Record #2534)
Associated skill: Sculpting
Skillpoints required: 24999
Blurb: Under rock and stone.
Syntax: MANIFEST AVALANCHE <target>
Extra info: You must have shaped your form at least twice.
Abfile:
Call down an avalanche to batter and break your foe. This skill behaves
differently when used against adventurers or denizens.

If the target is an adventurer, they will be knocked prone and take
damage. If they are already prone, they will take increased damage, any
limb that isn't crippled shall be crippled, and any limb that is already
crippled shall take very significant limb damage.

In addition, an avalanche striking shall immediately escalate head
damage from a damaged state to a mangled state.

If the target is a denizen, this skill shall deal a large amount of
blunt damage but requires that you have shaped your Earthen form as much
as possible(*).

* You must have 4 shaping stacks, and using this skill against a denizen
will reduce your shaping to 0.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
