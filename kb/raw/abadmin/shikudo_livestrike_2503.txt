Ability: Livestrike (Record #2503)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 24999
Blurb: Rob them of breath with a single blow.
Syntax: LIVESTRIKE
Abfile:
A powerful thrust aimed at the solar plexus, this staff attack will rob
your target of breath, delivering asthma.

Note that this ability along with all other Shikudo strikes and kicks
must be performed in a combination. See AB SHIKUDO COMBO for details.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
