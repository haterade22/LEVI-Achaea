Ability: Accord (Record #3200)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: Betrayal most foul.
Syntax: RECITE ACCORD
Abfile:
This tale is one of agreement, but more, one of the most profound
betrayal. All that hear it shall know an echo of that most dread choice,
and will be struck down with the betrayal affliction for a time.

* All afflicted with betrayal have a chance to consider anyone an enemy
in a given moment, or an ally as no ally at all. Note that this does not
in fact modify either ally or enemy lists in the process.

Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 15.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
