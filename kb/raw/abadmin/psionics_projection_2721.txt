Ability: Projection (Record #2721)
Associated skill: Psionics
Skillpoints required: 42999
Blurb: Your will made manifest.
Syntax: PSI PROJECTION
PSI PROJECTION CREATE
PSI PROJECTION LOCATE
PSI PROJECTION SWAP
PSI PROJECTION DESTROY
Abfile:
By creating a psychic projection of yourself in a location, you may
overlay it with yourself from anywhere on the same plane and continent.
Doing so will swap your location with the projection(*).

* The projection will fade after three swaps.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
