Ability: Eminence (Record #2913)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Power everlasting.
Syntax: ACCURSED PERFORM THE RITUAL OF EMINENCE
Abfile:
He who strives to escape his Accursed fate shall find only a blacker one
besides. The ritual of Eminence shall gain you great power, but subsume
more of you in the process; he who walks under its terrible aegis shall
drive imperfection from his person(*), be eternal beyond other
Accursed(**), and shall radiate such a malign aura that where ever he
may walk pestilence shall be made manifest(***).

* Passive affliction healing.
** Significant passive health healing.
*** Periodically afflicts everyone in a location with a random plague.

(This lasts approximately an hour of being logged in.)
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 10.00 seconds of equilibrium
Skill specialisation: charnel_rituals
