RELEASE <target>

Abfile:
From within the skies, you may swoop down on an opponent and HOIST them off the ground. Once you have them clasped within your mighty talons, you can move about with them and either RELEASE them, causing them great damage if they are unfortunate enough not to be able to fly, or DEPOSIT them on the ground somewhere.
