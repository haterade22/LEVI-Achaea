Ability: Incandescence (Record #2574)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Let them look upon you and despair.
Syntax: MANIFEST INCANDESCENCE
Extra info: Clears all spark on use.
Abfile:
By becoming a conduit for Kkractle's power, you may surround yourself in
the terrible incandescence of the Primal Fire. Anyone looking upon you
while targeting you with a hostile act will be transfixed. If you are
under 25% of your maximum health, your fire will burn so bright that
they will instead be slain outright.

You may not perform any actions while incandescent, though you may
during the short windup period for said incandescence to manifest.

Note that on successfully using this ability all of your spark will be
wiped and you will become unable to become incandescent again for three
minutes, unless upon the Elemental Plane of Fire.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
