Ability: Transverse (Record #153)
Associated skill: Necromancy
Skillpoints required: 48999
Blurb: Cross vast distances in a single step.
Syntax: TRANSVERSE <target>
Abfile:
This ability allows for swift travel across the land. You must initiate
the activation from a room where hands of the grave have been summoned
forth.

If your target also stands in a room where the hands grasp at the
living, this travel is instantaneous. If the hands are not present, the
target is required to be a practitioner of necromancy, and the travel
will be delayed.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 2% life essence and 500 mana
