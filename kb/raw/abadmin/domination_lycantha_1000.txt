Ability: Lycantha (Record #1000)
Associated skill: Domination
Skillpoints required: 45999
Blurb: Lycantha, Keeper of the Hounds.
Syntax: COMMAND HOUND AT <target>
Abfile:
The Keeper of the Chaos Hounds shall send one of his charges to serve
you when called upon.

When commanded to strike an adventurer, the hound shall inspire
weariness in them.

Against denizens, the hound will rend their flesh, dealing damage(*).

When primebonded, the hound shall periodically break magical shields on
adventurers when such are present. In addition, when commanded to attack
denizens, it shall tear apart their magical shield with its assault.

* Commanding your hound against denizens requires and uses equilibrium,
rather than entity balance.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of entity balance
Resource: 50 mana
The ability name HOUND is mapped to this ability.
