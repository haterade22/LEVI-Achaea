Ability: Ensorcell (Record #2876)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 49999
Blurb: Bound in blood and fate besides.
Syntax: CRUX ENSORCELL <target>
Abfile:
Higher mastery with your ritual blade will enable you to both bind and
let in a single stroke. When you ensorcell your foe, they will find it
difficult to flee from your arcane power(*), and you shall claim the
life which sustains them in preparation for higher workings(**).

* They have a chance to fail to leave on movement attempts.
** You will gain their blood upon your ritual blade, similar to let.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
