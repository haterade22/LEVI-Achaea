Ability: Chaoslords (Record #3221)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 23999
Blurb: Of uplifting and twisted rule.
Syntax: RECITE CHAOSLORDS
Abfile:
When the Chaos Lords first arose and those in power shifted by the day,
so too did their vulnerabilities change seemingly at random. At the
culmination of this tale, a field of damage absorption shall be created,
periodically changing to make a given damage type utterly negated in
your location(*).

* The shifting cannot be controlled.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 16.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
