Ability: Gauge (Record #2549)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 699
Blurb: The briefest glance to divine their end.
Syntax: GAUGE <target>
Abfile:
Gauge your target's vital power with but a glance, discerning how much
health they have remaining. This ability does not consume either balance
or equilibrium, but has a short time after use during which you may not
use it again.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
