Ability: Discordance (Record #2735)
Associated skill: Emulation
Skillpoints required: 27999

Abfile:
There were very few forays into this field of study before it was simply
agreed upon to be abandoned. The effects were simply too unpredictable,
even if sometimes beneficial. By enacting this power, you will change
the health of everyone in your location to better over represent the
whole.

* There is only so much this can modify a person's health by, and only
so much a given person can influence the overall result.

Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
