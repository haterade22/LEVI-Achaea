Ability: Sustain (Record #3039)
Associated skill: Excision
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: You will give your life for the cause.
Syntax: PERFORM SUSTAIN <target>
Abfile:
By giving up your own health, you may heal another.

When used for your bloodsworn's benefit, the amount given up is far
reduced and the amount they are restored by is increased. When healing
an adventurer, you will restore both health and mana, though the amount
of mana restored is lesser.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
