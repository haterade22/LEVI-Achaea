Ability: Follow (Record #2947)
Associated skill: Valour
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: Ever watchful.
Syntax: EAGLE FOLLOW <target>
Abfile:
You have established enough of a rapport with your eagle now that you
may ask it to follow, from the air, a particular adventurer. When the
eagle loses the adventurer, generally because the adventurer goes
indoors, it will stop following.
Useable on: none

Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
