Ability: Suppress (Record #2108)
Associated skill: Survival
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: Suppress protective vibrations.
Syntax: SUPPRESS REVERBERATION [direction]
Abfile:
You may now suppress active reverberation vibrations in a room. Given
the difficulties involved, this requires all of your concentration, and
may be interrupted.

In addition, if a magi is present you require a secondary influence to
assist in your attempt to disrupt said vibration. You may accomplish
this by throwing a cube sigil at the ground, although the influence
shall only last a short while.

Sometimes it is not possible to enter a location, but one must still
remove a reverberation vibration that protects more dangerous barriers.
In order to do this, you may specify a direction in which to suppress
vibrations. This requires you be adjacent to the location you are
targeting and the channel time is somewhat increased compared to doing
it in the location proper.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 150 mana
