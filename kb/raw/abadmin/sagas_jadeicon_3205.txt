Ability: Jadeicon (Record #3205)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 11999
Blurb: When dream turns to nightmare.
Syntax: RECITE JADEICON
Abfile:
The power of dreams is one so frequently underestimated, and perhaps no
tale tells this so clearly as that of the jade icon. For a time after
this retelling, any attempt to put someone to sleep in the location will
still succeed, but the one who would seek to impose said slumber shall
suffer in their turn(*).

* Afflicts anyone who gives the sleep affliction to another with a
mental affliction.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 16.00 seconds of voice balance and 2.30 seconds of equilibrium
