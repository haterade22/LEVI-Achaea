Ability: Runicarmour (Record #1165)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: Empower armour with the strength of the earth.
Syntax: EMPOWER <armour>
Abfile:
Calling upon the powers of the earth, you are able to empower any suit
of armour upon which the runes gebo and gebu have been sketched.
Empowering armour will render it usable only by he who empowered it, but
it will bear the defensive powers of the runes of protection for one
hundred months.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 10.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 2000 mana
