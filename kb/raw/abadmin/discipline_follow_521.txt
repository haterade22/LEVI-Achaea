Ability: Follow (Record #521)
Associated skill: Discipline
Skillpoints required: 6999
Blurb: Order your falcon to aerially follow someone.
Syntax: FALCON FOLLOW <target>
Abfile:
You have established enough of a rapport with your falcon now that you
may order it to follow, from the air, a particular adventurer. When the
falcon loses the adventurer, generally because the adventurer goes
indoors, it will stop following.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
