Ability: Overwhelm (Record #2518)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 53999
Blurb: Dispatch them with a single catastrophic blow.
Syntax: BATTLEFURY OVERWHELM <target>
Extra info: Requires a warhammer.
Abfile:
Set your feet and raise your hammer, in preparation for a single
catastrophic blow. Your next brain attempt will be altered to do damage
based on how many skull fractures the target has and does not require
your opponent to be prone.

This ability requires you possess both standard balance as well as
battlefury balance, and consumes both.
