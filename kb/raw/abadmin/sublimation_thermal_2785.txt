Ability: Thermal (Record #2785)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 2999
Blurb: A slight shunting of heat.
Syntax: MANIPULATE THERMAL
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Thermal energy is surprisingly difficult to manipulate directly.
Nevertheless, shunting it out of a location is simple enough. Drain a
location of heat and cause all within to be given a level of freeze.

Overdraw effect: destroys all firewalls in the current location.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of balance
Resource: 4% Wellspring Energy
