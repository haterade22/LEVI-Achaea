Ability: Legion (Record #3137)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Rise and dine.
Syntax: SOULBLEED LEGION
Abfile:
You have risen so far in the Legion of the Underworld, and so many
wretches and drudges now lie below you. Command them from the depths and
they will hearken to your call(*), but understand that this grave act
comes with a dire cost(**).

* This skill will summon a legion of 3-5 high-level undead in your
location. These denizens will be loyal to you and can be ordered to
follow or attack. (Be aware, some attacks from these mobs hit the entire
room, including you.)
** Using this skill will consume one of your remaining lives.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 2
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of balance
