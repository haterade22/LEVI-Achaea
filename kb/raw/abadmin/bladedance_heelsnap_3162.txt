Ability: Heelsnap (Record #3162)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 31999
Blurb: Two legs are for amateurs.
Syntax: BLADE HEELSNAP <target> <LEFT|RIGHT>
Abfile:
This low lateral slash with the rapier is aimed at one of the legs of
your partner. As with other techniques with the rapier, it has various
effects based upon your position in relation to your partner:

From the front: breaks the leg in question.

From the side: if aimed at the left leg, afflicts with healthleech. If
aimed at the right leg, afflicts with sensitivity.

From the back: afflicts with hamstrung and dismounts your partner if
they are mounted.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
