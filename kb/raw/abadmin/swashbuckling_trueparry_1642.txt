Ability: Trueparry (Record #1642)
Associated skill: Swashbuckling
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: Such is your mastery that you may parry a larger area.
Syntax: PARRY left leg|left arm|right leg|right arm|head|torso
TRUEPARRY centre|left|right|none
Abfile:
The trueparry ability, combined with the weaponry parry ability, allows you to actively defend attacks against two parts of your body. 'centre' parries attacks to both the head and torso. If you do not have the weaponry parry ability, then trueparry allows parrying, but not trueparrying.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
