Ability: Scour (Record #2612)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 7999
Blurb: A slashing wind to tear flesh from bone.
Syntax: AERO SCOUR <target>
Abfile:
Summon forth a slashing wind to scour your foe with Whiirh's relentless
fury.

Against adventurers, this flesh-tearing gale shall render them so
hideous that even their most loyal allies will turn against them(*). A
time after you use this ability(**) either the wind shall come again if
your foe has cured disloyalty, or if they still labour under the
affliction they shall be stunned and knocked off balance for a short
time.

Note that this periodic tick will continue to occur until a tick occurs
where disloyalty is reapplied.

Against denizens, these winds shall deal cutting damage and then
relapse, causing additional damage after two seconds.

* Gives disloyalty and damage.
** This time is shorter the more pressure you have built up around your
target. See AB DURESS PRESSURE for details.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
