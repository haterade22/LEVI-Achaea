Ability: Degenerate (Record #2423)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 45999
Blurb: The ravages of time.
Syntax: CHRONO DEGENERATE <target> [BOOST]
Abfile:
If a target is sufficiently afflicted(*), you may drastically accelerate
the effects of said afflictions.

Against adventurers, this will cause the unfortunate subject to age in
an instant, decreasing their constitution by one for each affliction.
When boosted, the target will lose two constitution per affliction.

When used against a physically-plagued denizen(**), this aeonic command
will deal significant magical damage and cannot be boosted.

* Valid adventurer afflictions are: weariness, clumsiness, healthleech,
nausea, asthma, and paralysis.
** Valid battlerage afflictions against denizens are: inhibit, weakness,
sensitivity, and clumsiness.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 700 age
