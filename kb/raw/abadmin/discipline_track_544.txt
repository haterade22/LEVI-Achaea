Ability: Track (Record #544)
Associated skill: Discipline
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Order your falcon to follow and track someone.
Syntax: FALCON TRACK <target>
Abfile:
This ability is an improved version of falcon follow. With track, even if the falcon temporarily loses its target, because the target went indoors, the falcon will continue to follow the target once the target comes back outside.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
