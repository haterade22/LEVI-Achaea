Ability: Wolf (Record #1203)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 56999
Blurb: Call the mighty grey wolf to your side.
Syntax: SUMMON WOLF
Abfile:
The most powerful of the animals that will come to your call, the mighty
wolf will attack with its fangs and claws, damaging its victims. If your
foe is lacking the deafness defence they will also be shaken by the
wolf's howl, increasing the damage taken.

* Those enemied to the forests will not be able to call upon the aid of
Nature to assist them.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
