Ability: Connection (Record #3059)
Associated skill: Anathema
Skillpoints required: 1
Blurb: The fundamental which underpins.
Syntax: KARMA
KARMA DRAW
Abfile:
Each Unnamable requires vast amounts of karma to facilitate their works,
and the Living Book of Eschaton is the greatest repository of karma
there is. Those citizens of Ashtan who undertake this path may seek it
out, and TOUCH it to begin the process.

Once a connection is established, the student of Anathema may DRAW upon
the connection when in the Eschaton's presence, restoring some of their
karmic reserves. Note however, this process is painful, and doing so in
excess may have consequences.
