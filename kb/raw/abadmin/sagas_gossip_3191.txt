Ability: Gossip (Record #3191)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: You'll never believe what I heard...
Syntax: GOSSIP SPREAD <gossip>
GOSSIP LISTEN
Abfile:
The art of storytelling is one step removed from the art of
conversation, and as a master of both, sometimes you just need to get
your message out there in a less obvious way.

By seeking out various establishments(*), you can, around once an
Achaean day, spread some gossip. Similarly in said locations, you may
also listen for gossip every so often, and in such a way gossip is bound
to spread.

* Inns and similar, generally.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance and equilibrium
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of balance
