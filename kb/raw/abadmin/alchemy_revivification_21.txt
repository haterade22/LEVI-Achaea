Ability: Revivification (Record #21)
Associated skill: Alchemy
Skillpoints required: 51332
Blurb: Grant life to the deceased.
Syntax: REVIVE <target>
Abfile:
Using the alchemical energies and a catalyst of five pieces of the salt
prime, you may return a corpse to life. Ensure that the corpse is whole
and in good condition, lest the results be less than desirable.

NOTE: this ability will only work if the target is a mutual ally, or if
they are a member of the same city as you.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 5.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 400 mana
