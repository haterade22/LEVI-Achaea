Ability: Transform (Record #3254)
Associated skill: Augmentation
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: The manipulation of gear.
Syntax: GEAR TRANSFORM <gear>
Abfile:
At a cost of arcane ephemera, you may transform a piece of gear that you
possess (or that someone else possesses through the GEAR TRADE
interface).

Transforming a piece of gear will reroll that item (in its given slot
and set). This may produce an entirely new set of effects, or reroll the
existing magnitudes on current affixes.

* A transform costs one alteration, and a given piece of gear may only
be altered up to three times.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.50 seconds of equilibrium
