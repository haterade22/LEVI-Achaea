Ability: Deteriorate (Record #2425)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 50999
Blurb: Madness comes to all.
Syntax: CHRONO DETERIORATE <target> [BOOST]
Abfile:
If a target is sufficiently afflicted(*), you may drastically accelerate
the effects of said afflictions.

Against adventurers, this will cause the unfortunate subject to age in
an instant, decreasing their intelligence by one for each affliction.
When boosted, the target will lose two intelligence per affliction.

In addition, adventurers suffering under this status will take
significantly more health and mana loss from the depression affliction.

When used against a mind-addled denizen(**), this aeonic command will
deal significant magical damage and cannot be boosted.

* Valid adventurer afflictions are: stupidity, confusion,
hallucinations, depression, shadowmancy madness, vertigo, masochism,
agoraphobia, and claustrophobia.
** Valid battlerage afflictions against denizens are: recklessness,
charm, fear, aeon, and amnesia.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 300 age
