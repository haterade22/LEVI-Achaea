Ability: Magisterion (Record #3199)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 30999
Blurb: And so the dead did stir and...
Syntax: RECITE MAGISTERION
Abfile:
The tale of the Magisterion is a grim one, and one inexorably tied in
with the Underworld and the lord who once ruled there. When reciting it,
your retelling will destroy necromantic constructs(*), though a
necromancer being present will require much more effort to overcome(**).

* On the first tick with no necromancer present, gravehands are
destroyed. On the second tick gravehands are destroyed even if a
necromancer is present. On the third tick, infestation maggots are
destroyed.
** If a necromancer (Oppression users count as necromancers for the
purposes of this ability) is present, this recital is a channeled
action.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 18.00 seconds of voice balance
