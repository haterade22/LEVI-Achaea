Ability: Curse (Record #1573)
Associated skill: Dragoncraft
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Lay down a terrible curse to cripple your foe.
Syntax: DRAGONCURSE <target>
DRAGONCURSE <target> <affliction(*)> <delay in seconds(**)>
Abfile:
With no affliction and delay specified, your victim will be cursed with
a number of random masked afflictions.

With an affliction specified, the curse will strike the target after the
specified delay. It should be noted that the curse will not consume
equilibrium when employed in this way, but may only be invoked
periodically. Your target may only have a single delayed curse at a time
- attempting to lay a delayed curse upon one already awaiting their doom
shall fail.

(*) Affliction must be one of: paralysis, impatience, sensitivity,
asthma, stupidity, weariness, or recklessness.

(**) Delay must be between one and ten seconds.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 500 mana
Skill specialisation: dragonform
