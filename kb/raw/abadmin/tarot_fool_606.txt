Ability: Fool (Record #606)
Associated skill: Tarot
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: Shrug off afflictions with blind courage.
Syntax: FLING FOOL AT <target>
Abfile:
The Fool is pictured as being immune to fear and full of courage. With
this Tarot you may throw off three afflictions by denying their
existence. You must wait a time(*) before the Fool will attend you
again.

* 35 second cooldown.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
