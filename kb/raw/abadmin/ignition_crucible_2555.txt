Ability: Crucible (Record #2555)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 8999
Blurb: Where only the strong survive.
Syntax: IGNITE CRUCIBLE
Abfile:
Providing another lord has not done so already, you may claim a location
as your crucible. Though this state of affairs is only temporary, it
offers several advantages the able servant of Kkractle may take
advantage of. All those that do not walk with the gift of Elemental Fire
will have their fire resistance reduced, and any that are afflicted with
ablaze will immediately be seared by the flames, no matter how swiftly
they may act to douse the fire.

You may only have one crucible at a time, and igniting a new crucible
shall immediately destroy your old one.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
