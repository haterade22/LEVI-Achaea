Ability: Choke (Record #2586)
Associated skill: Pervasion
Skillpoints required: 17999
Blurb: Fill their lungs with water.
Syntax: MANIFEST CHOKE <target>
Extra info: Flux effect: If nausea and weariness are present, also gives paralysis.
Abfile:
Fill the lungs of your target with water, causing them to be unable to
breathe(*).

* Gives asthma if they are an adventurer or deals asphyxiation damage if
they are a denizen. If cast on someone already suffering asthma, they'll
become so deprived of oxygen that they will begin to hallucinate.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
