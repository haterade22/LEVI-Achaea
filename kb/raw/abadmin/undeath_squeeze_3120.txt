Ability: Squeeze (Record #3120)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: Until the eyes burst.
Syntax: SOULBLEED SQUEEZE <target>

Wrap your arms about your victim and crush the life from them. If your
victim has no internal trauma, they will gain mild internal trauma. If
they already have mild internal trauma, it will become serious. If it is
already serious, the damage taken will be increased.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 2
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.80 seconds of balance
