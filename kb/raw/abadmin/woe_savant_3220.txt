Ability: Savant (Record #3220)
Associated skill: Woe
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Of vengeance in the making.
Syntax: RECITE SAVANT
Abfile:
The tale of the traitor Danaeus begins with a mortal life and ends with
the ascension of a Chaos Lord. Through fell magics, the once-companion
of the wizard Hycanthus despaired of a finite existence and chose the
path of blood and betrayal, turning on one master, then another, and
then another. All that hear this tale will know a vengeance yet
unfilled, and long in the making.

* Gives the vengeance defence to all in the location.

Vengeance can be relaxed with RELAX SAVANT or RELAX VENGEANCE, and will
eventually fade over time.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 18.00 seconds of voice balance and 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
