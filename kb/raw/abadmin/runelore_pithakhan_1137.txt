Ability: Pithakhan (Record #1137)
Associated skill: Runelore
Skillpoints required: 1499
Blurb: Attack the mental reserves of your enemies.
Syntax: SKETCH PITHAKHAN ON GROUND/<totem>
SKETCH PITHAKHAN ON <weapon>
Abfile:
Symbol: Square Box

When sketched on the ground or on a totem, it attacks an enemy's mana
when he or she encounters it. Disappears after one use, unless it is on
a totem. Won't affect blind people.

When sketched upon a weapon, it enables the weapon to occasionally drain
mana from a target, though this always occurs if the target has a
damaged head (requiring a restoration apply to heal it). This will only
apply if the weapon is a runeblade.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
