Ability: Sun (Record #2869)
Associated skill: Memorium
Skillpoints required: 30999
Blurb: Protector. Destroyer.
Syntax: TRACE SUN <target>
Abfile:
The Sun which both nourishes and destroys. All are equal beneath its
brilliant glare, though not all can withstand that which it reveals.
When traced against a foe, their minds will be pushed to breaking
beneath its power, their thoughts thrown into disarray(*).

* Gives epilepsy.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
