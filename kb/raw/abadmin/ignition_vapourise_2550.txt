Ability: Vapourise (Record #2550)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 999
Blurb: A minor application of heat.
Syntax: MANIFEST SUPERHEAT <target>
Abfile:
By superheating the air surrounding a target who is protected by a
magical shield, you may use said protection as an amplifier for your
power, both destroying the shield and afflicting the target with ablaze
in the process.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
