Ability: Thornlash (Record #3269)
Associated skill: Groves
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Lash your prey with Nature's fury
Syntax: GROVE THORNLASH <target>
Abfile:
Tap into Nature's fury and command your grove to lash at a denizen,
dealing significant cutting damage to them. The potency of this
invocation is determined by the Druid or Sylvan casting it(*).

* The damage dealt by this ability will scale with the higher of your
strength or intelligence stat.

Useable on: denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.00 seconds of balance
