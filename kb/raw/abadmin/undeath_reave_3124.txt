Ability: Reave (Record #3124)
Associated skill: Undeath
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: Flesh and bone and soul and more.
Syntax: SOULBLEED REAVE <target>
Abfile:
Reave the weakened body of your foe, inflicting grievous wounds(*).

* A target with cracked ribs will gain as many grievous wounds as they
have stacks of cracked ribs, with a max of three per strike.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.80 seconds of balance
