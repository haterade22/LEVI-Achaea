Ability: Rebirth (Record #2428)
Associated skill: Aeonics
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Death shall never claim you.
Syntax: CHRONO REBIRTH
Abfile:
This greatest of the time abilities exacts a terrible price upon the
user. Not only can it only be used sparingly, but when activated it
shall immediately raise you to the maximum age possible for you to
survive.

However, the power it grants is equally terrible. You may return from
the very brink of death, reforming your mortal shell around your soul.
Be warned. You shall return extremely weak in mind and body.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
