Ability: Enkindle (Record #3281)
Associated skill: Spiritlore
Skillpoints required: 52999
Blurb: For the most desperate of times.
Syntax: SPIRIT ENKINDLE
SPIRIT SLACKEN <spirit>
SPIRIT SLACKEN SOLIDIFY <spirit>
Abfile:
The master practitioner can learn to slacken bonds to the point of
breaking while in communion, and yet in a time of need draw them all
back to full potence for a brief time, though with dire consequences.

* Slackening a bond puts it into a dormant status, it keeps its
attunement. You cannot have a slackened and active bond with the same
spirit.
* You can have as many slackened bonds as you can have active bonds and
these limits do not impact one another, so you can have five active and
five slackened for instance.
* When you enkindle, all active bonds are severed and all slackened
bonds are made active. After five minutes all bonds will be broken and
you will be unable to commune for a further five minutes.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.60 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 500 mana and 200 willpower
