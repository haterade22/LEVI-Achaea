Ability: Lament (Record #2459)
Associated skill: Terminus
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: And thus, they fell.
Syntax: UTTER LAMENT
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
The power to tap the ancestral memories of the Tsol'teth line is one
usually restricted to those of the true blood, but due to your studies
you are now able to glance back a single generation.

Speak the lamentation of deSangre, and all those that you see as allies
shall be granted an enhancement truly terrible in nature. Whenever one
under the effect of the lamentation slays another(*), they shall become
stronger, faster, tougher(**).

Due to the taxing nature of tapping the ancestral memories, this ability
carries a fairly significant cooldown.

* Killing an enemy does not reset the lamentation timer.
** Each kill grants them +1 to each stat and a balance/equilibrium
bonus.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.40 seconds of word balance
