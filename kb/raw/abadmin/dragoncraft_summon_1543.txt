Ability: Summon (Record #1543)
Associated skill: Dragoncraft
Skillpoints required: 0
Blurb: Summon your breath weapon.
Syntax: SUMMON <breath weapon type>
Abfile:
Summon your breath weapon. Each type of Dragon has a different breath weapon

   Black:  Acid
      - Acid breath. Unblockable damage.

   Blue:   Ice
      - Cold damage. Chance of becoming frozen.

   Gold:   Psi
      - Psychic damage instead of breath weapon. Chance of eq loss.

   Green:  Venom
      - Poison damage. Chance of a random venom affliction.

   Red:    Dragonfire
      - Fire damage. Sets the target on fire also.

   Silver: Lightning
      - Electric damage. High chance of epilepsy.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Resource: 300 mana
Skill specialisation: dragonform
