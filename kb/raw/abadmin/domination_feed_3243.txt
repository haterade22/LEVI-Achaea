Ability: Feed (Record #3243)
Associated skill: Domination
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: A transaction most profane.
Syntax: PRIMEBOND FEED <chaos lord>
Abfile:
Melding your knowledge of Occultism with the bonds you have wrought with
the Lords of the Chaos Court, you may pour karma down one of these
established connections to empower the servants of that Chaos Lord that
stand alongside you.

* Doubles the tick rate of that entity and forces a tick to happen very
shortly after the empowerment, though that entity will be destroyed
within eighteen seconds.
** You may only perform this ability once per minute.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 8% karma
