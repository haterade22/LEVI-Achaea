Ability: Flick (Record #3211)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Sometimes steel, sometimes song.
Syntax: BLADE FLICK <target>
Abfile:
This flick with the blade focuses more on song than steel, permitting
the bard to channel the nature of their songblessed weapon to greater
effect. If successful your foe will be struck down with certain deadly
conditions:

From the front: crescendo, an enthralling condition which will only grow
more dire the longer they dance to your tune(*).

From the side: earworm, a condition that is sure to grow worse over
time(**).

From the back: both crescendo and earworm.

* Each time someone afflicted with crescendo eats an herb that is not
ash (which cures crescendo), the crescendo will build. At a certain
point, this makes other abilities available (see AB COMPOSITION FINALE).
** All bladedance attacks that deal damage deal additional damage
against a target afflicted with earworm. This damage increases the
longer the earworm remains uncured.
*** When used against denizens, this ability deals psychic damage to
your foe.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.10 seconds of balance
