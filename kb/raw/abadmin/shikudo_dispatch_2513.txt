Ability: Dispatch (Record #2513)
Associated skill: Shikudo
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: Beware an angered Shikudo practitioner.
Syntax: DISPATCH|INCAPACITATE <target> [form]
Abfile:
Though much of the Shikudo style is based upon not delivering fatal
blows, sometimes there are few other options. If your foe is prone with
a damaged head and windpipe, you may dispatch them with a single blow of
your staff, ending their life instantly. In the event that you believe
that death is not necessary, you may instead INCAPACITATE your foe,
stunning them for a great length of time rather than ending their life.

Note that this ability is unique among the Shikudo strikes: it is
performed outside of a combination and may be used regardless of your
form. You may also specify a form when carrying out the act, which you
will immediately flow into if your dispatch is successful, regardless of
any other limitations.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
