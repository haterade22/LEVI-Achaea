Ability: Impart (Record #967)
Associated skill: Occultism
Skillpoints required: 21999
Blurb: Impart your karmic knowledge to others with Occultism.
Syntax: IMPART <target>
Abfile:
This ability will allow you to transfer some of your own karma to another Occultist. It should be noted that this is not an even exchange - more karma is lost by you than is gained by the recipient of the transfer.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 9.00 seconds of equilibrium
