Ability: Monoxide (Record #1813)
Associated skill: Formulation
Skillpoints required: 42000
Blurb: A gas to disorientate your subjects.
Syntax: WIELD MONOXIDE
THROW MONOXIDE <AT GROUND|direction>
Extra info: Reagents: 1 ferrum, 1 realgar, 1 plumbum
Abfile:
This potent gas causes those who inhale it to become extremely
disorientated.

If flung from an adjacent room, any who breathe the gas in shall find
themselves periodically falling to the ground. If flung at the ground,
those that inhale it shall also suffer an immediate balance loss.

Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of balance
