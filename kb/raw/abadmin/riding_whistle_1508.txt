Ability: Whistle (Record #1508)
Associated skill: Riding
Skillpoints required: 14999
Blurb: Whistle one loyal steed to your side.
Syntax: WHISTLE MOUNT|WHISTLE MOUNT <mount type>|<mount#>
Abfile:
Upon issuing a high-pitched whistle, you can call one of your loyal mounts to you, so long as it is on the same continent as you. Note that this ability may only be used to call -one- mount to you. If you have multiple mounts, you will only be able to call one of them to you at a time. You can whistle for a specific mount with WHISTLE MOUNT <mount type> or WHISTLE MOUNT mount# (if you have more than one of the same type).
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
The ability name WHISTLING is mapped to this ability.
