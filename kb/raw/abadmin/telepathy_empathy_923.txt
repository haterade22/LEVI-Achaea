Ability: Empathy (Record #923)
Associated skill: Telepathy
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Establishing a physical link through your telepathy.
Syntax: MIND EMPATHY
MIND UNEMPATHY
Extra info: MIND UNEMPATHY cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium.
Abfile:
This ability will channel much damage done to a locked adventurer over the telepathic link to you. Be careful with it though, for it is possible to die if too much damage is inflicted upon you via the telepathic link. This ability costs a regular drain of mental power.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 250 mana
