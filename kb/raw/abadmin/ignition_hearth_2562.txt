Ability: Hearth (Record #2562)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 23999
Blurb: Forged from your crucible.
Syntax: IGNITE HEARTH
Abfile:
If you have created a crucible, you may return to it providing you are
on the same plane of existence and close enough(*). A monolith on the
initiation end will stop the travel, though a monolith in your crucible
will not. Additionally, all that stand within your crucible that are not
blessed by the Primal Fire(**) shall take minor damage upon your
entrance.

* Must be on the same continent.
** Fire Elemental Lords.
Useable on: adventurers, room, and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
