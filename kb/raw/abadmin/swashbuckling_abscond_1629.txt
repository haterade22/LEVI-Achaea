Ability: Abscond (Record #1629)
Associated skill: Swashbuckling
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Swing down and abscond with the rector's daughter!
Syntax: ABSCOND <target>
Abfile:
Swing down from the trees, hanging on to a perilously thin rope. Grab your target and continue in a graceful arc back to the trees, victim in hand! This requires a rope commodity. You must be in the trees with your target below you. A failed abscond attempt may result in a broken limb.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of balance
