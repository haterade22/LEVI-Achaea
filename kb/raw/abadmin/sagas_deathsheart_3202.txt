Ability: Deathsheart (Record #3202)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 55999
Blurb: To give everything and more.
Syntax: RECITE DEATHSHEART <target>
Abfile:
This solemn tale is one of greatest sacrifice, and giving up of one's
self so that others may survive. If you speak this tale over where
another fallen adventurer did make their final stand(*), upon its
completion they will be resurrected, but you will be cast into the Halls
of the Finality immediately(**). This tale can only be told so often,
and takes all of your focus(***).

* You must recite this saga in the room where an adventurer fell. Their
corpse is not required, but they must currently be dead.
** You will immediately embrace death and you will leave no corpse
behind.
*** This carries a thirty minute cooldown upon success and is a
channeled action.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 30.00 seconds of voice balance
