Ability: Tumbling (Record #1393)
Associated skill: Survival
Skillpoints required: 14999
Blurb: Tumble past obstructions.
Syntax: TUMBLE <direction>
Abfile:
Your body is agile enough that you are able to escape nearly any
predicament by tumbling out of the room. Be warned though that tumbling
is not the swiftest method of moving and that furthermore, you will lose
your entourage and perhaps some defences while doing this.

Note that if you attempt to move while tumbling, the tumble attempt will
be aborted.
Useable on: room
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance
