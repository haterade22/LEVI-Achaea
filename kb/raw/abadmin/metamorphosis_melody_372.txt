Ability: Melody (Record #372)
Associated skill: Metamorphosis
Skillpoints required: 20999
Blurb: Sing a melody of invulnerability about you.
Syntax: SING MELODY
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
While you are singing this gorgeous melody, people will not be able to
attack through the prismatic barrier. However, due to the difficulty of
this, you cannot do anything else and maintain your song at the same
time.

When this barrier is lost there is a 3 second cooldown before another
can be raised.
Useable on: self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
