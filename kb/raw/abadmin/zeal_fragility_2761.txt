Ability: Fragility (Record #2761)
Associated skill: Zeal
Skillpoints required: 5999
Blurb: The profane are weak in body as with mind.
Syntax: RECITE FRAGILITY <target>
Abfile:
Direct this holy verse at a profane enemy of Creation and the next blow
you deal with your mace shall deal greatly increased damage to the
chosen limb(*).

* This increases the next mace attack's limb damage you deal by
approximately 30%.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.60 seconds of prayer balance
