Ability: Gravitywell (Record #2787)
Associated skill: Sublimation
Skillpoints required: 35999
Blurb: Control the distance.
Syntax: MANIPULATE GRAVITYWELL
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
Manipulate the fundamental forces and bring a gravitywell into being.
All in adjacent rooms will be pulled inwards upon creation.

Overdraw effect: The well will destabilise four seconds after its
creation, with a chance to throw all those present from the well's room.
Any attempt to recast this ability will reset any ongoing gravitywell
destabilisation.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
Resource: 5% Wellspring Energy
