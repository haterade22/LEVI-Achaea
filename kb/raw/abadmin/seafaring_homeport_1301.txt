Ability: Homeport (Record #1301)
Associated skill: Seafaring
Skillpoints required: 60000
Blurb: A seaspell to return you to safe harbour.
Syntax: INVOKE HOMEPORT
Abfile:
This powerful seaspell will return your vessel to the harbour that it
last departed. Once cast, provided you are not already docked at a
harbour, the spell will summon a powerful storm to transport you
great distances.

Be warned, using the Homeport ability will come at a great cost,
destroying your figurehead in the process (or draining and dismounting
resetting ones). Your vessel must be in prime condition to withstand the
rigours of this journey, and any damage to this ship while returning
will cause the spell to fail.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
