Ability: Smash (Record #2081)
Associated skill: Weaponmastery
Skillpoints required: 6000
Blurb: Deliver a crushing blow with your shield.
Syntax: SMASH <target> [HIGH|MID|LOW]
Abfile:
This powerful strike with your shield will have various effects
depending where you strike your foe.

Targeting high shall deliver a mental affliction(*), as well as
delivering
the scrambled brains affliction.
Targeting mid shall cause their muscles to lock in paralysis.
Targeting low shall cause them to stumble and become hopelessly clumsy.

If no target is specified for this attack, the damage dealt is more
significant.

* It will first deliver dizziness if not present, then recklessness,
stupidity, confusion, and finally epilepsy.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: Balance cost varies based on weapon speed
Skill specialisation: knight_snb
