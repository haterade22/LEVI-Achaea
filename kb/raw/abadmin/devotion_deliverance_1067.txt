Ability: Deliverance (Record #1067)
Associated skill: Devotion
Skillpoints required: 39999
Blurb: Deliver a target unto you.
Syntax: PERFORM DELIVERANCE <target>
Abfile:
This will allow you to summon an adventurer from any distance should
they have been blessed with the PREACHING ability. It should be noted
that a monolith sigil placed in the room of the summonee or summoner
will prevent this from working.

Be aware that delivering another adventurer out of combat may open you
up for repercussion.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 1200 devotion
