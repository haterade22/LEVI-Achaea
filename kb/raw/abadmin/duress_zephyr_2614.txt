Ability: Zephyr (Record #2614)
Associated skill: Duress
Skillpoints required: 13999
Blurb: A freezing rush to leech away strength.
Syntax: MANIFEST ZEPHYR <target>
Abfile:
Call upon Whiirh to grant you a frigid zephyr, which will then be cast
toward your foe.

Against adventurers, this will freeze your target to such a point that
they will be unable to retain their mental fortitude(*). After a
time(**) the zephyr will come again, either re-afflicting your foe if
manaleech isn't present, or giving deadening if their mental faculties
are already failing. If your foe has both deadening and manaleech when
the zephyr comes again, your target shall receive a short mental
blackout.

Note that this periodic tick will continue to occur until a tick occurs
where manaleech is reapplied.

Against denizens, these winds shall deal cold damage and then relapse,
causing additional damage after two seconds.

* Gives manaleech and damage.
** This time is shorter the more pressure you have built up around your
target. See AB DURESS PRESSURE for details.

Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
