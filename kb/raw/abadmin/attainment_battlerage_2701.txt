Ability: Battlerage (Record #2701)
Associated skill: Attainment
Skillpoints required: 3599
Blurb: The rage of battle.
Syntax:
Abfile:
As you fight denizens, you will build up Battlerage. Your Rage will
increase as you do damage to denizens, and will slowly decrease if you
stop attacking. Certain abilities only useful against denizens require
you to build up and expend Rage.

Each class has access to six Battlerage abilities, which will be earned
as you increase in level from 6 to 65.

By default, you will be notified every time you gain Rage and you can
also do BATTLERAGE (or simply BR) to see your current rage.

For configuration options, details, and more, see HELP BATTLERAGE.
