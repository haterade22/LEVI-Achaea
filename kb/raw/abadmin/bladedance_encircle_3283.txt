Ability: Encircle (Record #3283)
Associated skill: Bladedance
Skillpoints required: 55000
Blurb: Work with an ally to encircle foes.

Stand beside an ally, ready to encircle your foe.

Using this ability on a mutual ally will allow you to circle behind your
target from the front position, so long as your partner is in the room.

Pre checks: balance
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
