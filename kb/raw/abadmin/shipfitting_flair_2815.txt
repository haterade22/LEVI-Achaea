Ability: Flair (Record #2815)
Associated skill: Shipfitting
Skillpoints required: 26999
Blurb: Aesthetic ship customisation.
Syntax: SHIPFIT DYE SAILS <colour>
SHIPFIT <PAINT|STAIN> HULL WITH <colour>
SHIPFIT TRIM HULL WITH <material>

Abfile:
Beyond the mere function of lesser shipfitters, add true flair to a
ship, personalising it to suit your own tastes. Dyeing sails, painting,
and staining will require pigments and ship commodities, while trimming
a hull will require ship commodities and material samples. This ability
will also allow a shipfitter to sample materials.
