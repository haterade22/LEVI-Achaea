Ability: Elegy (Record #3184)
Associated skill: Composition
Skillpoints required: 43999
Blurb: Weep for the dead.
Syntax: COMPOSE ELEGY
Abfile:
This solemn song for the dead will lay the weariness of loss upon all
who hear it. Each time an enemy hears your song, their celerity will be
greatly reduced(*), and if they are already lethargic, it will be
eliminated entirely(**).

* Their celerity will be set to one movement below maximum to whatever
they can normally move.
** They will have their celerity maxed out completely.
Useable on: none
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.50 seconds of equilibrium
