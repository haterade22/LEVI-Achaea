Ability: Jab (Record #842)
Associated skill: Tekura
Skillpoints required: 699
Blurb: A straight-forward punch.
Syntax: JBP <target> <HEAD|ARMS>
Abfile:
A quick strike with the fingers. When targeting the head, this will
restore someone's hearing. When targeting the arms, it will disable the
target's ability to parry for a very short time.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of arm balance
