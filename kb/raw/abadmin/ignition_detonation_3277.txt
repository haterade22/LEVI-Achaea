Ability: Detonation (Record #3277)
Associated skill: Ignition
Skillpoints required: 40999
Blurb: A single moment to send them thus.
Syntax: MANIFEST DETONATION
Abfile:
While standing in your crucible, you may cause it to detonate in a
single moment of explosive force(*), casting all those not of Kkractle's
legion(**) before it and scattering them to all corners(***). This
ability has no fixed spark requirement, but does require you have at
least one level of spark fanned.

* Destroys your crucible on use.
** Does not work against other fire lords.
*** Has a chance to throw everyone else in a random direction. The
higher your spark, the higher the chance each person will be thrown.
**** A target having the mass defence makes them less likely to be
thrown, equivalent to you having one less level of spark than you
actually do when determining whether they will be moved.
Useable on: room
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 4.00 seconds of equilibrium
