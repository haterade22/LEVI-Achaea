Ability: Destroy (Record #3257)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: A sliver of the Primal Fire.
Syntax: CAST DESTROY AT <target>
Abfile:
 When dealing with a sufficiently weakened foe(*), you will be able
to unleash the wrath of Kkractle directly upon them, destroying them.

* They must be afflicted with a conflagration and have 40% of their
health or less.
** You must be wielding an elemental staff to perform this ability.
*** While this ability may be cast normally, if you also possess an
ashbeast it will be enhanced, and will bypass starburst protections and
similar in this case.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.60 seconds of equilibrium
