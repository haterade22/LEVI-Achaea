Ability: Carve (Record #2903)
Associated skill: Charnel
Skillpoints required: 27999
Blurb: They dare walk on hallowed stone.
Syntax: CRYPT CARVE <logograph>
CRYPT TRAP DISMANTLE LOGOGRAPH
Abfile:
The stone of your crypt is so saturated with arcane power that you may
use its very stone as a base for the logographs of Memorium. By carving
them into the stone, all that enter their location excluding yourself
will find themselves struck down by said logograph's power(*).

* See memorium ability files for details.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: none
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of balance
