Ability: Djinn (Record #2053)
Associated skill: Artificing
Skillpoints required: 9999
Blurb: A subordinate agent of Whiirh.
Syntax: ELEMENTAL LEVITATE [target]
Abfile:
Once summoned, this lesser elemental of Whiirh's domain will allow you
to levitate yourself into the sky, or another if you so desire. It will
only be able to do this every so often, but it should be noted that the
power of this elemental's levitation is quite formidable, and will
bypass most things that would otherwise prevent you from taking to the
skies.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.60 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 100 mana
