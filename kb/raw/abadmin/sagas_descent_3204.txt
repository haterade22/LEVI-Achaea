Ability: Descent (Record #3204)
Associated skill: Sagas
Skillpoints required: 45999
Blurb: Of renegades and sorrow.
Syntax: RECITE DESCENT <target>
Abfile:
There was fire and fury a plenty on that day when the renegade host
descended upon the city of Targossas, and so this recounting conveys.
Upon its conclusion, everyone in the location will be cleansed of a
random affliction which afflicts them, excluding the one you are
reciting the story to and yourself. Then, the unfortunate subject of
your grand recital will be inflicted with damage based on the number of
afflictions cured from the audience.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.80 seconds of equilibrium and 25.00 seconds of voice balance
