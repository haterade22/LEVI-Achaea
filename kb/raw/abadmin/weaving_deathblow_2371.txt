Ability: Deathblow (Record #2371)
Associated skill: Weaving
Skillpoints required: 29999
Blurb: Precision over power.
Syntax: WEAVE DEATHBLOW <target>
Abfile:
A deadly slash across the throat, this attack with a dagger will make it
impossible for a target to breathe (*), as well as inflicting them with
a bad case of excessive bleeding (**).

* Delivers asthma.
** Gives bleeding, which is increased if the target is prone at the time
of the attack.
Useable on: adventurers and denizens
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.20 seconds of balance
