Ability: Uraiad (Record #2441)
Associated skill: Terminus
Skillpoints required: 3999
Blurb: Why settle for worse?
Syntax: INTONE URAIAD <target>
This ability will be hidden on ablistings.
Abfile:
A truly potent ability, speak this word targeted at one who considers
you a mutual ally. For the next thirty minutes you shall possess the
same constitution and strength as your chosen target.

* Note that this carries a one hour cooldown.
** Additionally note that this is a direct link. If your linked target's
stats change while you are linked with them, your stats shall also
change to mirror them.
Useable on: adventurers and self
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 10.00 seconds of word balance
