Ability: Badger (Record #1189)
Associated skill: Woodlore
Skillpoints required: 22999
Blurb: Call for the aid of a very grumpy badger.
Syntax: SUMMON BADGER
Abfile:
The grumpy badger is a fearsome foe and is able to rip into opponents
with its claws, causing great amounts of bleeding(*). If the target is
sufficiently bleeding already(**), the badger will enter a frenzy and
additionally give asthma.

* The bleeding is increased if the target also has haemophilia.
** 200 bleeding or more.
*** Those enemied to the forests will not be able to call upon the aid
of Nature to assist them.

Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks:
Ability range: none
Aggression type: non-aggressive
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds of equilibrium
