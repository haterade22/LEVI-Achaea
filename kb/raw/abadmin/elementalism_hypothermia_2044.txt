Ability: Hypothermia (Record #2044)
Associated skill: Elementalism
Skillpoints required: 4999
Blurb: The freezing touch of Sllshya.
Syntax: CAST HYPOTHERMIA AT <target>
Abfile:
With this higher order spell, if your enemy is already frozen, you may
bring a terrible cold down upon him or her, removing their ability to
heal their frozen state.

* This affliction is cured via the restoration salve.
Useable on: adventurers
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (blocked by shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 1
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 2.60 seconds of equilibrium
Resource: 60 mana
