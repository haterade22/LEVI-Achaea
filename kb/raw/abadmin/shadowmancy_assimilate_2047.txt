Ability: Assimilate (Record #2047)
Associated skill: Shadowmancy
Skillpoints required: 58999
Blurb: You will take it all.
Syntax: SHADOW ASSIMILATE <target> <STRENGTH|SPEED|YEARS>
Abfile:
With a victim's shadow held within your phylactery, you may take of that
which is their province and make of it your own.

Strength: for the next thirty seconds, your unnatural strength will
permit you to surpass simple physical realities, striking with your
scythe even when paralysed.

Speed: for the next forty seconds, your reap and cull abilities will be
somewhat faster, though they will deal somewhat less damage as well.

Years: for the next thirty seconds, each time you land a blow on your
victim and they survive the assault, you will steal of their years and
restore your youth if needed.

* Only one assimilation bonus may be in play at once, and they only
apply against the target you used the shadow of to instigate the
process.
** Consumes the shadow upon use.
Useable on: none
Pre checks: balance, equilibrium, and prone
Ability range: same room and elevation
Aggression type: aggressive (bypasses shield/prismatic)
Requires arms: 0
Requires legs: 0
Useable entangled: No
Useable paralysed: No
Cooldown: 1.80 seconds of balance
