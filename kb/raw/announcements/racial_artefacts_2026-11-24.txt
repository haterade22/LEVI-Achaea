Hi folks!

As part of our ongoing strategy toward lowering barrier of entry for new players, each race now carries natural talent equal to the power of a level 1 artefact.

For example: a human adventurer without any artefacts will now benefit as though they owned a Mayan ring.

If you already own an arefact power your race would gain, your artefact applies with priority, so a level 2 or higher artefact remains an upgrade.

The racial bonuses are:

Human - Health sip
Siren - Intelligence
Grook - Faster equilibrium
Tash'la - Reserves
Dwarf - Constitution
Tsol'aa - Mana regeneration
Rajamala - Dexterity
Fayad - Mana sip
Troll - Strength
Satyr - Health regeneration
Mhun - Endurance regeneration
Xoran - Magic damage

Horkval and Atavians already have racial abilities which have been improved to be on par with artifact equivalents:

Horkval - Your racial leap now recovers as quickly as a leap with chitin greaves.
Atavian - Your wings now perform equivalently to a ring of flying.
Wingless Atavians will instead get the benefit of a fishscale tunic and gain underwater breathing.

If you do not have a equivalent or better power, you can see your racial bonus at the bottom of ARTEFACT POWER LIST OWNED.

A few things to note:

The racial bonus is fixed at level 1 and cannot be upgraded. To go beyond it, you will need the level 2 or 3 artefact.
This has to be this way due to the extremely messy situation that would arise across multiclassing and race changing and such.
The racial bonus does not carry over to Dragon or Elemental Lord.
This change is primarily aimed at helping onboard new players.
Like artefacts, racial bonuses do not apply during honourable duels.
Changes to your stats may take up to a minute to show after you log in.

Anyone who has recently purchased a L1 artefact that is now invalidated by their character race can file an ISSUE for a refund. Likewise, any Atavians or Horkvals who own rings or greaves can also request a refund on these but please see point #2 above!

Enjoy!
