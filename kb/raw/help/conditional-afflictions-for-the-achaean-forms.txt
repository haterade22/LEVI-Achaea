CONDITIONAL AFFLICTIONS FOR THE ACHAEAN FORMS

This file pertains to the topic of toxin locks, and explains how you can
hinder the active and passive healing abilities that each of the Achaean
classes have, including Elemental and Dragon form, and which you must
hinder to be able to lock your target successfully.

Initially, know that there is a difference between passive and active
healing capabilities. Active healing requires the use of a balanced
action, like fitness, alleviate and the like. Passive healing requires
an initial action, but then heals independently without further need for
interaction from the person performing the action. Examples of passive
healing are devotion rites and a Baalzadeen's syphon.

The main obstacle on the road to a toxin lock are the active healing
abilities. All passive healing abilities can be disrupted by use of the
toxin Voyria. By design, passive healing cures Voyria before anything
else, which means the opportunity to stack another affliction or two
within the healing rate window, before administering Voyria again.
