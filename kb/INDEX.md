## Classes
- [classes/](classes/): one page per class (21) with every ability of its skills -- syntax, cost, cooldown, target, the afflictions it names (GENERATED from the wiki by `tools/kb_wiki_skills.py`; WIKI confidence). `abilities.json` is the machine copy (60 skills, 1,929 abilities)
- [classes/abadmin-vs-wiki.md](classes/abadmin-vs-wiki.md): the game's own ABADMIN SHOW record of each ability (`kbcapture abilities`, `kb/raw/abadmin/`) against the wiki: where cost or target differ, abilities the wiki does not list, and the skills the wiki pages do not cover (GENERATED by `tools/kb_abadmin.py`; `abadmin.json` is the machine copy: skillpoints, syntax, target, range, arms and legs needed, entangled/paralysed use, cost, resource, specialisation). ABADMIN costs are BASE costs. Every id the game has, 1-3296, filed 2026-11-13; the capture is complete. Only values both sides state are compared; the game corrects Serpent BITE (1.90 s, the wiki 4.00). **An ABADMIN flag is the game's data, not proof of what it enforces**: FITNESS, SALT and ERUPTION say "Useable paralysed: No" and fired under real paralysis in our logs (`CONFLICTS.md` #82); a record with no Pre checks value shows default flags, and `Aggression type: non-aggressive` is a default too, so a shield question needs a log (`CONFLICTS.md` #83, IMPULSE)
- [classes/battlerage.md](classes/battlerage.md): every class's battlerage from the game's records (188 records, 31 classes): rage, cooldown, target, the denizen affliction each gives or the two it uses, and its own text (GENERATED by `tools/kb_abadmin.py`). What the basher sends and what it leaves out: `.claude/projects/basher/battlerage-pve.md`, "The game's battlerage records"

## Simulator
- [sim/forward.md](sim/forward.md): the victim's decisions on real asthma + weariness boards, 20 s forward against a strong attacker, with ROBUST, CONDITIONAL or UNSETTLED verdicts. A model: it never says who wins. Generated by `tools/kb_sim.py`
- [sim/events.md](sim/events.md): the four Serpent logs as events. The census of every attacker, cure, defence and refusal line; the race of each DSTAB against the eat (PHASE, winner, the golden Z5 row); the suppliers re-measured in every log. Generated by `tools/kb_events.py`
- [sim/rules.md](sim/rules.md): every rule of the whole-fight engine with its tag (MEASURED, READ, WIKI, PLAYER, ASSUMED), its source and its sweep values. Generated by `tools/kb_rules.py`
- Theorycraft a fight: `python tools/theorycraft.py --victim runewarden --gap 1.83 --median --script "dstab curare kalmia" ...`, or a real log in the same columns (`--log Z5 --from 196`), or a fork from a real row (`--log Z5 --at 204 --then "dstab curare vernalius"`). MODEL output; the bars it must pass before any verdict are frozen in `tools/sim/gates_whole.json`
